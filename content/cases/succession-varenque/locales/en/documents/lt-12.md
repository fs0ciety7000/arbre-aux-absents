*Half a sheet of plain white paper with no letterhead, an engineer’s hand, upright and regular,
except for the first line, which shakes.*

Moulinvert, 18 June 1944

Adélaïde,

They have killed my son. Augustin. Yesterday morning, at the Citadel.

We learnt it from the Regency’s notice, pasted up even on the door of the Town House, the way one
learns the price of bread.

Irène does not know yet. The doctor at the sanatorium says she must not, not now. I do not know who
will tell her. Not me: I do not know how to say things.

I shall do what has to be done. Don’t ask me what.

::signature[Gaspard]
