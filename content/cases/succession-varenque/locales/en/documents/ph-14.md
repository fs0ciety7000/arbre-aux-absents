## Front

Slightly underexposed print, cockled. A wooden quay, nets drying, the hull of a fishing boat dressed
with pennants; on the bow, half hidden by a pennant, the letters *MAAR…*.

On the left, a man of about thirty, clean-shaven, tanned, a gold ring in his left ear. A new
Ostrian zigzag jersey, sailor’s cap pushed back. He has rolled up his right sleeve: on the forearm,
a large tattooed seabird, wings spread, hooked beak. He is laughing with his mouth wide open.

On the right, a young woman of about twenty-five with a broad face, braids pinned up in a tight
crown, an embroidered zigzag costume, a small posy of heather and gorse. On the right side of her
neck, a dark birthmark that the embroidered collar does not quite hide. She is watching her husband
laugh, and looks as though she thinks he is overdoing it.
