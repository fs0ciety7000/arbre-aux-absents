::stamp[REGENCY OF VELMARCHE — ORDER — NATION]

## NOTICE

By judgment of the Court of Order of the Aubrelieu garrison, delivered on 16 June 1944 in application of the Act of 2 February 1938 and of the Decree of 12 March 1940 on seditious propaganda, the persons named below have been sentenced to the capital penalty for having, on the night of 14 to 15 June and on the nights following, affixed to public buildings posters insulting to the Regent and to the Velmarchois Order, and for membership of a criminal association known as “Fern”:

**VARENQUE Augustin**, aged 20, **student**, born at Moulinvert, residing at Aubrelieu, 9 rue de l’Université;

**MALTERRE Jean**, aged 24, journeyman typesetter, of no fixed abode;

**OSTRAND Pierre**, aged 31, of no occupation, already struck off.

## THE CONDEMNED WERE SHOT THIS MORNING, 17 JUNE 1944.

Whosoever destroys, tears or covers over the present notice shall incur the same penalties as the authors of the posters it condemns.

Whosoever knows of members of the association known as “Fern”, or of persons who shelter, feed or transport them, is bound to declare it to the Registers Bureau. Silence is complicity.

Aubrelieu, 17 June 1944.
