## REGISTER OF REGISTRATIONS ON DECLARATION (REGISTERS DESTROYED)

### Registration No. 58 — year 1946

In the year nineteen hundred and forty-six, on the eleventh of May, at ten o’clock, before us,
registrar of the commune of Vellaak, in the presence of the delegate of the Registers bureau of the
province of Ostrie, there appeared a woman declaring herself to be:

**Nora Ilvess**, photographer, residing at Vellaak, quai des Séchoirs,

who, the birth registers of Vellaak having been destroyed in August nineteen hundred and
thirty-nine, requested her registration on declaration, in accordance with Article 14 of the Act of
2 February 1938.

She declares that she was born at Vellaak in **nineteen hundred and eight**, day and month unknown,
of deceased Ostrian parents by the name of Ilvess, with no further particulars; that she has never
been married; that she has never been registered anywhere else.

There appeared with her, as witnesses to common repute, of full age, domiciled in the province, and
declaring that they have known her “since childhood”:

**Firmin Varenque**, aged forty-six, fishing skipper at Vellaak;

**Teodor Haask**, aged thirty-four, keeper at the Kessaar penal establishment, passing through
Vellaak;

who certified on their honour that the declaration is true.

**Opinion of the Bureau’s delegate:** *“Ilvess: no card, no removal known under this name. No
objection.”*

Having heard this read over, the declarant and the witnesses signed with us.

::signature[N. Ilvess]

::signature[F. Varenque — T. Haask]
