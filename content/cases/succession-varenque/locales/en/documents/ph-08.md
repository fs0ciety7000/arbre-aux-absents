## Front

Small amateur print, slightly blurred at the edges. A cluttered studio under a glass roof: canvases
turned to the wall, a half-painted poster pinned to a board, a stove, washing lines strung across
the room.

On the left, a man of about thirty at the easel, in three-quarter view: brown beard trimmed to a
point, broad-brimmed felt hat, a big loose bow in place of a tie, an open smock. He has turned his
head towards the lens; his left eye turns slightly outwards. His fingers are stained with paint up
to the knuckles.

On the right, on a low dais, a young woman of about twenty poses as a washerwoman: canvas apron,
sleeves rolled up, a basket of washing on her hip. Curly black hair in a loose bun. She cannot hold
the pose: she is laughing, and her laugh shows a gap between her front teeth. A small scar marks
her chin.

On the board, the sketch for the poster takes up her outline, basket and all.
