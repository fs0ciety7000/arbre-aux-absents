## ORIGINAL DEED No. 418 — DEED OF ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

Before **Maître Achille Brunesseau**, notary resident at Saint-Ormel, the undersigned, in the
presence of the two attesting witnesses named below,

**There appeared:**

Mr **Lucien Marie Varenque**, painter and poster artist, residing at Aubrelieu, 12 rue des Tanneurs,
at present mobilised as an army painter (camouflage section) and on regular leave, born at
Moulinvert on the thirtieth of September eighteen hundred and seventy-five;

Who has declared, by these presents, that he **acknowledges as his natural son** the child entered
in the civil registers of Aubrelieu (4th Ward, entry No. 731 of 1906) under the name of **Victor
Sauge**, born at Aubrelieu on the twelfth of May nineteen hundred and six to **Pauline Sauge**,
residing at 14 rue des Tanneurs, and to a father not named.

The person appearing requests that the present acknowledgement be noted in the margin of the
child’s birth certificate, **to be acted upon should any misfortune befall him**, and annexes hereto
a declaration written in his own hand, which reads as follows:

> *“I, the undersigned Lucien Varenque, painter, acknowledge as my son Victor, born on 12 May 1906 to
> Pauline Sauge, whom I loved and did not manage to marry. I want him to bear my name if he so
> wishes, and to know that he owes it to no one but me. Done at Saint-Ormel, on leave, 6 October
> 1917.”*
>
> ::signature[Lucien Varenque]

Duly recorded, made and executed at Saint-Ormel, in the office, on the sixth of October nineteen
hundred and seventeen. And having heard this read over, the person appearing signed with the
witnesses, Messrs Alphonse Giraud and Henri Pagès, clerks of the office, and the notary.

::signature[L. Varenque — A. Giraud — H. Pagès — A. Brunesseau, notary]
