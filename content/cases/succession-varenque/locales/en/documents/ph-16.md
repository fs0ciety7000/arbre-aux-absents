::stamp[REGENCY OF VELMARCHE — B.R.R. — IDENTIFICATION SERVICE — CARD No. 41-2206]

## Photographs

Two prints pasted on, 4 × 5 cm each, with a graduated measuring rule behind. **Full face:** a woman
of about thirty, brown hair drawn back and tied short, hollow cheeks, thick eyebrows; above the
forehead, on the left, a white forelock that has escaped the elastic. She stares into the lens
without blinking. **Profile (right):** the same woman; in the foreground, her right hand rests on
the number slate, and on the back of the hand there is a pale, shiny scar.

The slate reads, in chalk: *41-2206 — 24.10.41*.
