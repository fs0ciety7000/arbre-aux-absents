## Front

Brown print on thick card. High summer: the shade of a great lime tree falls across a garden bench
in front of the steps of a gentleman’s house; behind, the open window of a drawing room and a
curtain that moved during the exposure.

On the bench, on the left, a man of about forty, receding hairline, brown hair swept back,
mutton-chop whiskers; a pale scar across his left eyebrow. Dark frock coat, waistcoat, watch chain.
He holds a folded newspaper on his knee.

Beside him, a woman of about thirty-five, hair in bandeaux, a white forelock above the forehead, on
the left; a high collar fastened by a brooch in the shape of a fern frond. On her lap, a little girl
of four or five in a smocked dress, who also has a white forelock, sharp and clear, in the same
place.

Standing behind the bench:

- on the left, a tall boy of about thirteen, sailor collar, with the solemn look of the eldest who
  has been put in charge of the pose; a fair forelock above the forehead, on the left;
- on the right, a smaller boy, eight or nine, short trousers, grazed knees. He is looking at the
  lens, but his left eye turns slightly outwards, so that he seems to be looking at something to
  one side as well.

## Back

The card is bare, with no photographer’s name: an amateur’s print, or a travelling
photographer’s.
