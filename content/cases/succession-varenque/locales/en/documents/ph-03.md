## Front

Faded print, corners eaten away by damp. The couple pose in front of the whitewashed wall of a low
house; behind them, the base of a round tower and, right at the top on the left, cut off by the
frame, a lighthouse gallery. The wind has blurred the ribbons of the headdress and the hem of the
skirt.

On the left, a young man of about twenty-five, broad nose, full black beard, a gold ring in his
left ear. Officer’s cap, double-breasted pea jacket, a mate’s braid on the sleeve. He stands very
straight and looks as though he is holding back a fit of laughter.

On the right, a young woman of about twenty, strong jaw, very pale eyes, fair braids coiled under
a ribboned headdress. White collar embroidered with black zigzags, apron embroidered to match. She
is not smiling: she is looking at the sea, to the left of the lens, as though keeping watch on
something.
