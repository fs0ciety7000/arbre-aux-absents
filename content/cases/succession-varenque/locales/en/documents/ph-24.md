## Front

Large black-and-white print, sharp, taken under the covered playground of a high school: cast-iron
columns, a blackboard on which someone has chalked *4B — 1954–1955*. Twenty-four girls of thirteen
or fourteen in four rows, in pale round-collared smocks, the school badge on the pocket. On the
right, standing, a grey-haired woman teacher in a tailored suit, her arm stiff.

In the second row, second from the left: a girl with short, curly, light-brown hair and thick
eyebrows. Above the forehead, on the left, a white forelock, sharp-edged, two fingers wide. All the
others have their hair pulled back or held by a slide; she does not. She looks at the lens without
smiling, her chin a little raised.
