## AUBRELIEU CIVIL COURT — WARDS’ CHAMBER

### Judgment No. 877 — sitting of Thursday the seventeenth of September, nineteen hundred and forty-two

**Between:** Mr Marcel Daubry, aged forty-five, Commissioner of Registers for the province of the
Orvelle, and Mrs Blanche Varenque, his wife, aged forty-one, residing together at Aubrelieu,
8 avenue de la Charte, **petitioners**;

**And:** the Regency’s Wards’ Charity, Grisenoue home, represented by its administrator, who states
that she leaves the matter to the court.

**The Court,**

Whereas a child of the female sex, **born on 3 March 1942 at Grisenoue, of unknown parents**, a ward
of the Charity, was entrusted to the petitioners on 20 June 1942 and has lived in their home ever
since;

Whereas the petitioners, married these nineteen years and childless, give proof of good character
and conduct, of sufficient means and of an unreserved attachment to Velmarchois order; whereas the
male petitioner, in his official capacity, himself supplied the documents of the child’s civil
status;

Whereas full adoption is in the interest of the child and in that of the Nation;

**On these grounds,**

Pronounces the full **adoption** of the said child by Marcel Daubry and Blanche Varenque, his wife;

Rules that the child shall bear the given names **Lise**, Henriette, and the surname **Daubry**;

Rules that no filiation exists or shall exist for the said child other than that resulting from the
present judgment, and that no earlier document relating to her may be disclosed to anyone;

Orders that the present judgment be entered in the civil registers of Aubrelieu, where it shall
stand in place of a birth certificate.

::signature[The President of the Chamber — the clerk]
