## HERBARIUM — FERNS OF VELMARCHE

### Sheet 27

*Specimen: one complete fertile frond, 46 cm, fixed with nine strips of gummed paper.*

**Printed label, filled in with a pen:**

| Herbarium of C. Varenque, botanist | Moulinvert |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Royal fern, *osmunda regalis* |
| Common name | ::hand[ilv, in Ostrian] |
| Place collected | Bank of the Clairette, below the Moulin de la Sorbe, peaty soil |
| Date | 14 June 1871 |
| Collector | C. V. |
| Remarks | Fertile frond; upper pinnules bearing the sporangia in russet clusters. |

**Note in pencil, in the right-hand margin, with a simplified sketch of the frond:**

::hand[Ilv. — for the watermark, 1888. The word is Maaren's: where she comes from, it is the plant that grows back first when the heath has burnt. Keep the stem straight, eleven pairs of pinnules, no sporangia: it must show through the paper. — C. V.]

**Later note, in ink, in the same hand:**

::hand[Reproduced in the Flora of the Ferns of Velmarche, 1894, plate XII. — C. V.]
