*Hospital letterhead, a quick hand in ballpoint, a great many words underlined.*

**PORT-SÉNANE SEAMEN’S HOSPITAL** — Paediatric Department
Dr H. Marsal

Port-Sénane, 24 March 1968

Aunt Adé,

You have read Paul’s articles. Everyone has read them: they cut them out for me in the canteen,
they read them aloud to me in the lift, a patient’s mother asked me if I was “one of those Varenque
people”. Yes. I am one of those Varenque people.

I am not asking you to take sides. I am asking you to tell me whether it is true.

Papa supplied the paper for the grey booklets: that much I knew, everyone knew, he was tried for it
and acquitted all the same. But he says nothing about Odile. Thirty years he has said nothing about
Odile. Paul wrote “Gaspard Varenque” in full, and beside it he wrote “his sister”, and he had no
need to write the verb: everyone read it in between.

I wrote to him. He has not answered. He never answers. He has always been like that: when he doesn’t
answer, it is because he is right, and when he is right, he doesn’t answer.

I shall not set foot in Moulinvert until he has spoken. Tell him so if you like. Paul says I am
wrong to blame him and not the times. Paul may be right. I do my paediatric night shifts so as not
to think about it: children, at least, don’t lie about their fever.

With love all the same, and a great deal of it.

::signature[Hélène Marsal]
