## Front

Studio portrait, soft lighting, painted backdrop of a blurred garden. A woman of about forty sits
in an armchair; wavy hair shorter than the fashion of before, a beauty spot above the lip on the
left, a string of pearls. On her lap she holds a baby a few months old in a long gown and a tight
lace bonnet, from which not a single hair escapes. She is looking at the child, and at nothing else.

Standing behind the armchair, a man of about forty, very thin, fine moustache, a neat side parting,
heavy eyelids. Dark suit, a round badge in his buttonhole. His right hand rests on the back of the
chair, close to the woman’s shoulder without touching it; a signet ring gleams on his little finger.
He is the one looking at the lens.
