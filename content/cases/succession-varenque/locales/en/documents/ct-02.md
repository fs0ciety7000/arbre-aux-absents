## NORA ILVESS — FACES OF THE COAST

### Photographs 1946–1963 — Forty-seven plates

Galerie du Môle, quai du Môle, Port-Sénane — From 6 June to 26 July 1964 — With the support of the Town of Port-Sénane and the *Vel Taalik* press

*On the cover: “For É., 1962” (plate 47).*

---

### Preface

She came to Vellaak one winter when nobody came, and nobody asked her where she was from. On our coast, that is the greatest courtesy one can pay a person.

She opened a studio above the drying sheds, she learnt the names of the boats before the names of the people, and the people ended up climbing her stairs. They came for their weddings, for their dead, for a child who was going away. She took them as they were, that is to say better than they saw themselves. In Ostrian we say that a fern always grows back where the fire has passed. I know of no better definition of these photographs.

::signature[S. V., Vellaak, May 1964]
