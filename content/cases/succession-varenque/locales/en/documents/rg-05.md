## COMMUNE OF MOULINVERT — REGISTER OF BIRTHS

### Extracts for the years 1898, 1901 and 1908

*Registers with printed columns since 1890. Out of habit, the town clerk enters wives as “of no
occupation”, whatever their actual work.*

| Year | No. | Child | Born | Father | Mother |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1898 | 117 | Gaspard Anselme VARENQUE, male | 8 October 1898, at Les Tilleuls | Augustin Varenque, aged twenty-six, industrialist | Henriette Sorbelle, aged twenty-two, his wife, of no occupation |
| 1901 | 19 | Blanche Clémence VARENQUE, female | 16 February 1901, at Les Tilleuls | Augustin Varenque, aged twenty-nine, industrialist | Henriette Sorbelle, aged twenty-four, his wife, of no occupation |
| 1908 | 74 | Odile Marie VARENQUE, female | 27 June 1908, at Les Tilleuls | Augustin Varenque, aged thirty-six, Deputy for the Orvelle | Henriette Sorbelle, aged thirty-two, his wife, of no occupation |

The entries for 1898 and 1901 bear the father’s signature. The one for 1908 was registered by a
third party, “the father being detained at the Chamber”: Pierre Lhoste, foreman at the Varenque
Paper Mills, holding a power of attorney.

Certified true extracts.
