## TARGET CARD

Varenque collection, article 41, called the “blue register” in the inventory. Bound register, blue cloth cover, 22 × 34 cm, 96 leaves, the first 59 written on the front. Untitled. A single hand, in pen. Microfilmed on 12 September 1974, 35 mm, 98 frames (target, cover and leaves). Operator: Miss L. D.
