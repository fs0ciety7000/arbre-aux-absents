title: "Clandestine note, on cigarette paper"
author: "“F.”"

*A cigarette paper seven centimetres by four, written on both sides in pencil, in tiny, cramped
letters. Concertina folds; a brown stain in one corner. No date, no place. At the foot, a tiny
drawing of a fern stands in for a signature, followed by a letter.*

## Front

T. A. —

H. is carrying this. He is one of ours. Burn it if you like, but read it first, it’s the only one.

She was born on the 3rd, in the infirmary, at four in the morning, with a sea pounding the walls
like at the house on the cape. A girl. Small, but she cries well.

I named her **Élise**. I said it three times to the sister who writes the registers, so she would
write it. She wrote it.

They took her from me after **nine days**. I’d been told fifteen. They are taking her to **G.**, to
the Wards. They change the names there, everyone knows that.

## Back

She has **the forelock**. Like you, like me, like Grandmother. Very small, on the left. That, they
can’t change.

O. is well. I see him on Thursdays, across the yard; he pretends to laugh, for me. He doesn’t know
yet that she’s gone. I don’t know how to tell him with my hands. If you find her, tell her that her
father sings out of tune and that he is good.

Don’t come. Don’t ask M. anything, above all not M. Don’t ask anything of anyone in the family who
wears a uniform.

Find her.

::signature[(a fern drawn in pencil) F.]

::hand[In pencil, on the outer fold, in Adélaïde’s hand: Delivered to Les Tilleuls on 21 March 1942 by the Haask boy, from the lighthouse, back from Kessaar. No other.]
