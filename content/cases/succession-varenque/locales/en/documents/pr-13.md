**THE VELMARCHE EYE** — Illustrated weekly — No. 2908 — Saturday 4 October 1958 — 0.30 Fn

## BOOKS

### *Faces of the Coast*, by Nora Ilvess

*Thirty-six plates, a text of ten lines, and the north coast as no one had ever looked at it.*

There are books one opens to leaf through and closes two hours later without having turned the tenth page. **Faces of the Coast**, just published by *Vel Taalik*, is one of them. Yet it is only an album of photographs, and the author has given it no more than a ten-line preface, in Ostrian and in Velmarchois, which says in substance: “I have photographed only people who had something to hide, that is to say everyone.”

Almost nothing is known of **Nora Ilvess**. She is a **photographer from Vellaak**, of whom nothing at all is known before 1946, when she opened, above the drying sheds of the harbour, a small studio where fishermen come to have their portraits taken for their weddings and their dead. She has never exhibited in Aubrelieu. She has answered none of our letters. Her publisher tells us only that she “does not like questions, but is very fond of other people’s answers”.

### People, with the sea behind them

Her sitters are sailors, net-menders, children who are cold, a young lighthouse keeper on his gallery, old women setting on the water, on the Night of Lanterns, the lights they mean for their drowned. All of them look at the lens as one looks at someone one has known for a long time. None smiles to please. There is in these faces a patience, and in the way they are taken a tenderness, that are not learnt at school.

We reproduce Plate XII, one of the finest, simply titled **“The Gull, 1951”**: a fishing skipper of Vellaak, whom the whole harbour calls by that name, laughs at the tiller of his boat, cap pushed back, the gold ring of the coast’s seamen in his ear. One longs to ask him what he has seen. One feels he will not say.

The last plate is a self-portrait, the only one in the book. The author shows herself standing in her studio, camera in hand, at fifty or thereabouts. We expected an enigma; we find a woman at work. She is doubtless right: it is the only enigma that matters.

*Faces of the Coast*, by Nora Ilvess, published by *Vel Taalik*, Vellaak, 36 plates, 18 Fn. On sale at good booksellers and, in Aubrelieu, at the bookshop of the Palais des Chartes.
