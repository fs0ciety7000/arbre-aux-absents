::stamp[CITY OF AUBRELIEU — CIVIL REGISTRY — 1ST WARD]

## DEATH CERTIFICATE No. 160 — YEAR 1938

### Death of Mr Augustin Varenque

In the year nineteen hundred and thirty-eight, on the fourth of February, at three o’clock in the
afternoon, before us, assistant to the mayor, registrar by delegation, there appeared Joseph Mallet,
aged fifty-one, employed at the town hall, and Désiré Cornu, aged sixty, valet to the deceased, who
declared to us that on this day, at six o’clock in the morning, there died at his home, at
Aubrelieu, 6 quai des Chartes:

**Augustin Jacques Hippolyte Varenque**, aged sixty-six, **former Deputy** for the Orvelle,
chairman of the board of the Varenque Paper Mills, born at Moulinvert on the eleventh of January
eighteen hundred and seventy-two, son of the late Anselme Varenque and of the late Clémence
Aubertot, husband of Henriette Sorbelle.

The death was certified by Dr Morand: “apoplexy”.

Having heard this read over, the informants signed with us.

::signature[J. Mallet — D. Cornu — the assistant to the mayor]

::stamp[ENDORSED AS IN ORDER — B.R.R. — 7 FEBRUARY 1938]
