::stamp[AUBRELIEU OPERA — DIRECTOR MR FERNAND RAVENEL]

## THE LANTERN OF OSTRIE

### Lyric drama in three acts

Music by **Mr Aurèle Vasquin** — Libretto by Mr Gérard Hollain after a legend of the north coast

**FIRST PERFORMANCE**

Saturday 9 February 1929, at 20.00

Thereafter on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays

---

### Cast

| Role | Performer |
| --- | --- |
| **Elva**, daughter of the keeper of the light | **Mrs Blanche Aubertine**, soprano |
| Torrik, her betrothed, a fisherman | Mr Léon Aubriot, tenor |
| The keeper of the light | Mr Raymond Deshaies, bass |
| Old Sivva | Mrs Simone Varlet, contralto |
| The lifeboat coxswain | Mr André Courtil, baritone |

Chorus of the women and fishermen of the coast, sung in the Ostrian tongue. Orchestra of the Opera conducted by Mr Aurèle Vasquin.

Sets by Mr Bréval. Costumes from the Opera workshops.

---

Prices: from 2 florins (amphitheatre) to 25 florins (boxes). Booking open at the Opera box office, from 11.00 to 18.00, and at the Belaube Department Store, entertainments counter.
