## Envelope

An ordinary envelope of grey paper, addressed by typewriter: “Civil Re-registration Office —
Varenque file — Aubrelieu”. No sender. The flap has been stuck down again.

::stamp[VELMARCHE POSTS — VELLAAK — 4 X 74 — 16 H]

## Sheet

Half a sheet of typing paper, folded in four. Three lines typed with a black ribbon, rather worn.
The *e* key sticks: now and then the letter is missing and only a blank remains.

> Look for th  photographer of Vellaak.
>
> Sh  is not who you think.
>
> She n eds no pity, just a compl te file.

No signature. On the back, nothing.
