::stamp[NATIONAL ARCHIVES — PALAIS DES CHARTES — CONSERVATION WORKSHOP]

**Varenque collection** (testamentary gift of Miss Adélaïde Varenque, received 2 April 1974).
Summary inventory, drawn up in June 1974. 63 articles, 2.40 linear metres.

**Provenance.** House known as Les Tilleuls, Moulinvert: bureau in the drawing room (Arts 1 to 40),
trunk in the attic (Arts 41 to 63). The bundles have been kept in the order in which they were
found; the donor’s order is itself a piece of information.

**General condition.** Good for the most part. Rag papers from the Varenque Paper Mills, very
stable. A few documents on thin paper (cigarette paper, onionskin) require handling under glass.
Traces of damp on Arts 52 to 57 (trunk).

| Art. | Description | Dates | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Papers of Clémence Varenque: herbarium (ferns), 212 sheets, and notes | 1866–1931 | herbarium restored by the workshop in 1970–1971 |
| 3–19 | Correspondence received by Adélaïde Varenque, arranged by correspondent | 1883–1973 | good; a few documents in sleeves |
| 20–34 | Family photographs, prints and cabinet cards | 1870–1972 | good; two captions torn |
| 35–38 | Press cuttings collected by the donor | 1889–1974 | acidic newsprint, to be deacidified |
| 39–40 | School papers (girls’ school of Moulinvert) | 1899–1944 | good |
| 41 | Register with a blue cloth cover, 96 leaves: paper-mill accounts (known as the “blue register”) | 1944–1952 | good |
| 42–51 | Household accounts of Les Tilleuls | 1919–1973 | good |
| 52–57 | Ostrian correspondence (cards, letters, one printed sheet) | 1883–1972 | damp, to be flattened |
| 58–63 | Objects and miscellanea: announcements, seating plans, programmes | 1870–1974 | good |

**Inventory-maker’s remark.** Several letters in Arts 3–19 bear neither date nor place; the paper
sometimes makes it possible to date them. The watermarks have been noted and entered on the article
cards. The present inventory is not an analysis: it says where things are, not what they mean.
