## Front

Printed caption: AUBRELIEU — The Palais des Chartes, seat of the National Archives. — Belaube Editions, card No. 2204.

## Back

::stamp[VELMARCHE POSTS — AUBRELIEU-CHARTES — 22.4.1971 — 0.30 FN]

*Address:* Miss Adélaïde Varenque, Les Tilleuls, Moulinvert.

::hand[Dear Madam,]

::hand[Your mother's herbarium is restored. All two hundred and twelve sheets have been deacidified and the ferns fixed back one by one; the osmunda sheet, the one where your mother wrote “for the watermark”, took three weeks on its own. It is waiting for you at the workshop, or I will bring it to Les Tilleuls myself if you would rather not make the journey.]

::hand[You asked me, last time, whether I sing. No: it is my adoptive mother who sings. I have never been able to hold a note. I hold papers instead, which is something.]

::hand[Thank you for the tea, and for your questions. I am seldom asked such kind ones.]

::signature[Lise Daubry, conservator]

::hand[Conservation workshop of the National Archives, Palais des Chartes.]
