## MARRIAGE CERTIFICATE No. 31 — YEAR 1923

In the year nineteen hundred and twenty-three, on the twenty-second of September, at eleven o’clock
in the morning, at the Town House of Moulinvert, before us, mayor and registrar, there appeared:

**Marcel Léon Daubry**, bookkeeper at the Varenque Paper Mills, aged twenty-six, born at Saint-Ormel
on the fourteenth of November eighteen hundred and ninety-six, residing at Moulinvert, rue de la
Clairette, son, of age, of Léon Daubry, clerk of the Saint-Ormel court, and of Berthe Lamarre, his
wife, present and consenting;

and **Blanche Clémence Varenque**, pupil at the Aubrelieu Conservatoire, aged twenty-two, born at
Moulinvert on the sixteenth of February nineteen hundred and one, residing there at Les Tilleuls,
daughter, of age, of Augustin Varenque, chairman of the Varenque Paper Mills, and of Henriette
Sorbelle, his wife, present and consenting;

who, the banns having been published at Moulinvert and at Saint-Ormel without objection, each in
turn declared that they take one another as husband and wife. We have pronounced in the name of the
law that Marcel Daubry and Blanche Varenque are united in marriage. The spouses declared that no
marriage contract had been drawn up.

In the presence of Ernest Couvreur, aged fifty-five, chief bookkeeper of the Varenque Paper Mills,
and of Anatole Mérel, aged forty-one, singing master at the Aubrelieu Conservatoire, witnesses, who
have signed with the spouses, their parents and ourselves, after reading.

::signature[M. Daubry — Blanche Varenque]

::signature[L. Daubry — B. Daubry, née Lamarre — Aug. Varenque — H. Varenque, née Sorbelle]
