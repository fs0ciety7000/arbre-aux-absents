## BULLETIN OF ORDER

### Official Journal of the Regency of Velmarche — Thursday 6 November 1941 — No. 88

**CENSUS AND REMOVALS BUREAU — REMOVAL LIST No. 41**

Having regard to the Act of 2 February 1938 on the purity of the registers; having regard to the
records of the provincial Registers bureaux of the Orvelle and of Ostrie,

**Article 1.** — The persons named below are struck off the civil registers and **declared
absent**. Those whose destination is indicated shall be transferred there without delay.

| No. | Surname | Given names | Born, at | Description | Grounds | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | BRISSAC | Henri | 4 March 1890, Hautcorvin | schoolmaster | seditious talk | Kessaar |
| 2 | KESSVEL | Orrin | 20 June 1905, Vellaak | typesetter, Ostrian | forging false papers | Kessaar |
| 3 | LANDREAU | Simone | 11 October 1913, Port-Sénane | typist | possession of banned printed matter | — |
| 4 | MOORVIK | Jalmar | 7 February 1899, Laakvik | fisherman | smuggling struck-off persons | Kessaar |
| 5 | VARENQUE | Odile | 27 June 1908, Moulinvert | engraver | forging false papers | Kessaar |

**Article 2.** — In connection with the case known as the “Fern” network, dismantled on 19 October
1941, the woman Odile Varenque, engraver, and the man Kessvel, Orrin, typesetter, Ostrian, are
furthermore deprived of all right of correspondence. Their property is placed under sequestration.

**Article 3.** — The families of the absent are required to surrender their identity papers to the
Registers bureau of their province within eight days.
