## Cover

Art deco border, stylised gold waves on a midnight-blue ground. In the centre, a photographic
portrait in a medallion: a young woman of about twenty-five, in three-quarter profile, black hair in
a short bob, a long neck, a long jade necklace falling over a dark dress. Her slender hands are
folded beneath her chin.

**PORT-SÉNANE THEATRE — 1928 SEASON**

**Piano recital**

**IRÈNE CASTAGNE**

*Saturday 17 March, at 20.30 — in aid of the Seamen’s Widows’ Fund*

## Programme

### First part

- Aurèle Vasquin — *Three Seascapes*: I. The Golfe d’Ambre at Morning; II. Mists; III. The Lantern
- Hector Malbrune — *Sonata in C sharp minor*, known as the “Estuary”

### Interval

### Second part

- Anton Vasko — *Brénica Dances* (first performance in Velmarche)
- Three Ostrian sea songs, transcribed for piano by the performer
- Aurèle Vasquin — *Toccata of the Packet Boats*

## Notes

A daughter of Port-Sénane and a pupil at the Aubrelieu Conservatoire, where in 1921 she was
unanimously awarded the first prize, Mrs Irène Castagne returns to play in the town where she
learnt to love music listening to the sirens in the docks. The Aubrelieu critics have hailed “a
pianist who plays Vasquin the way one reads a love letter: that is to say, too fast and twice over”.

Concert grand by the house of Grimaudot, Port-Sénane. The theatre thanks Castagne Shipping and the
Orvelle Packet Company.
