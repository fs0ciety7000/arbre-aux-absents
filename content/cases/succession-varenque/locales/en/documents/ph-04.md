## Front

Grey phototype, slightly muddy, printed in a series. The Tennok lighthouse fills the whole right
side: white tower with a dark band, gallery, lantern. At its foot, the keeper’s house and the
lifeboat house; behind, the sea and a line of reefs.

In front of the door, five people pose:

- in the foreground, in the centre, a woman of about sixty, strong jaw, very pale eyes, grey braids
  worn as a crown, a collar embroidered with zigzags, a shawl round her shoulders; she holds a
  bunch of heavy keys;
- behind her, on the left, a man of about thirty, clean-shaven, weather-beaten, in a zigzag
  jersey; his left hand, resting on a boy’s shoulder, has only three fingers left;
- on the right, a woman of about thirty in round spectacles, a headscarf knotted at the nape,
  smiling broadly;
- in front, a boy of about ten, barefoot, who has moved (his face is a little blurred), and a girl
  of six or seven, very serious, a rag doll under her arm.

Caption printed at the bottom, in capitals:

**TENNOK — THE LIGHTHOUSE AND THE KEEPER’S FAMILY**

## Back

Printed postcard back: *POSTCARD — Correspondence — Address*. Publisher: *Orvelle Phototype
Works, Port-Sénane, “Our Coasts” series, No. 118*. A 10-sou stamp bearing the Grand Duke’s head,
uncancelled.

::hand[Dear Adé, here we are on sale at the post office for 10 sous apiece! The photographer made us keep still for a quarter of an hour in the wind; the big one moved all the same. The little ones send their love. Come this summer, the sea is mild. – T.]
