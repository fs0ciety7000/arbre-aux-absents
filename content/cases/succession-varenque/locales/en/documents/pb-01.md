## MÉRANDE SOAP

### “The Washerwoman”

**The soap for hands that work.**

*Pure, gentle, rich in lather. Won’t burn your linen, won’t burn your hands.*

With Mérande soap, the finest linen comes out whiter than the snow on the Corvins, and the washerwoman’s hands stay as soft as a duchess’s. Ask your grocer for it: insist on the brand with the washerwoman.

The large bar: 30 sous. — Toilet soap, box of three: 1 florin.

Silver medal at the Grand Exhibition of Aubrelieu, 1889.
