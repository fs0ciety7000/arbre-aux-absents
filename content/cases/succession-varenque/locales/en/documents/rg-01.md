## COMMUNE OF MOULINVERT — REGISTER OF BIRTHS

### Extracts for the years 1872, 1875 and 1879

*Registers bound in sheepskin, a town clerk’s hand, faded brown ink. The three entries were copied
out one after the other, each from the register of its own year.*

| Year | No. | Child | Born | Father | Mother | Witnesses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1872 | 4 | Augustin Jacques Hippolyte VARENQUE, male | 11 January 1872, ten o’clock at night, in the house known as Les Tilleuls | Anselme Varenque, aged twenty-five, papermaker, residing at Les Tilleuls | Clémence Aubertot, aged twenty-one, his wife | Jacques Varenque, miller, paternal grandfather; Hippolyte Aubertot, pharmacist at Aubrelieu, maternal grandfather |
| 1875 | 61 | Lucien Marie VARENQUE, male | 30 September 1875, six o’clock in the morning, at Les Tilleuls | Anselme Varenque, aged twenty-nine, papermaker | Clémence Aubertot, aged twenty-five, his wife | Pierre Lhoste, foreman; Étienne Rabier, sizer, both employed at the paper mill |
| 1879 | 88 | Adélaïde Clémence VARENQUE, female | 1 December 1879, noon, at Les Tilleuls | Anselme Varenque, aged thirty-three, papermaker | Clémence Aubertot, aged twenty-nine, his wife | Pierre Lhoste, foreman; Jean Bastide, schoolmaster |

All three children are entered as “son” or “daughter of Anselme Varenque, papermaker, and of
Clémence Aubertot, his wife”, the father being present and signing each time. In 1879 the registrar
added in his own hand, after the father’s signature: *“third child of this household”*.

Certified true extracts from the registers.

::signature[The Mayor of Moulinvert, registrar]
