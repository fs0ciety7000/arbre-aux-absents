## OFFICIAL TRANSLATION FROM THE THALBÈRE

*Translation made by Mrs Ivela Darin, sworn translator to the court of Brénica. The original text is attached.*

### Municipal Police of Brénica (Brenzac) — Aliens Department

Brénica, 3 February 1960.

Chief Inspector Ilko Varasin to the Director of Public Security of the Republic of Velmarche, at Aubrelieu, through the consulate of Velmarche at Brénica.

**Subject:** identity of the man **Marc Dobrin**, deceased at Brénica on 17 November 1959.

Dear Sir,

I have the honour to bring to your attention the following facts, which may be of interest to your department.

The man **Marc Dobrin**, describing himself as a **bookkeeper**, born by his own account at Saint-Ormel (Velmarche) in 1896, **arrived at Brénica in April 1952 without valid papers**. In 1953 he obtained a residence permit on the strength of a Velmarchois birth certificate that was plainly forged, which our services failed to recognise at the time. Since then he lived a very retired life, rue des Scieurs, employed as a bookkeeper at the Brénica Forestry Cooperative, whose managers describe him as punctual, polite and friendless. He died of a heart complaint at the hôpital de la Croix-de-Brénica. No one claimed the body. The *Brenzac Messenger* published an obituary notice at the request of his landlady.

The inventory of his effects, handed to the town hall for want of an heir, revealed:

- a gold **signet ring**, worn on the right little finger, engraved with the letter **D**;
- an account book kept in Velmarchois, in a very careful hand, recording each month since 1953 sums sent by money order to a lady at Bains-de-Mérande, with no sender’s name;
- a Velmarchois press cutting, much worn, showing a woman opera singer.

Lastly, the doctor who certified the death reports an old scar: the **right ear** is **notched** at the top of the outer ear, an old wound, long healed.

These details, taken together with the description circulated by your services in 1953 concerning the former Commissioner of Registers **Daubry**, wanted by the Velmarchois courts, lead me to believe that **Marc Dobrin may be the ex-commissioner Daubry**. I hold at your disposal the signet ring, the account book and a photograph of the deceased taken in 1957 for the renewal of his residence permit.

Yours faithfully, with the assurance of my highest consideration,

::signature[Ilko Varasin, Chief Inspector]
