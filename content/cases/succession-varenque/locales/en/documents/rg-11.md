::stamp[CITY OF AUBRELIEU — CIVIL REGISTRY — 2ND WARD]

## MARRIAGE CERTIFICATE No. 388 — YEAR 1934

In the year nineteen hundred and thirty-four, on the fourteenth of April, at half past ten in the
morning, at the town hall of Aubrelieu, before us, assistant to the mayor, registrar by delegation,
there appeared:

**Victor Sauge**, aviator, pilot with the Aéro-Velmarche company, aged twenty-seven, born at
Aubrelieu on the twelfth of May nineteen hundred and six, residing at Aubrelieu, chemin de la
Plaine-aux-Saules, son, of age, of Pauline Sauge, residing at Aubrelieu, present and consenting, and
of a father not named;

and **Ninon Brial**, costume maker at the Aubrelieu Opera, of age, born at Aubrelieu in nineteen
hundred and twelve, residing there, rue des Coulisses, daughter of Jules Brial, tailor, and of the
late Adèle Ranson, his wife;

who each in turn declared that they take one another as husband and wife. We have pronounced in the
name of the law that Victor Sauge and Ninon Brial are united in marriage.

In the presence of Albéric Morvan, aged forty, chief pilot of Aéro-Velmarche, and of Jules Brial,
father of the bride, witnesses, who have signed with the spouses and ourselves, after reading.

::signature[V. Sauge — N. Brial — P. Sauge]

::signature[A. Morvan — J. Brial — the assistant to the mayor]
