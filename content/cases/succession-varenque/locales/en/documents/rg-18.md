## MOULINVERT CEMETERY — PERPETUAL PLOT No. 112

### Varenque family vault (allée des Tilleuls, row 3)

*A leaf from the register of plots, kept by three successive keepers: three hands, three inks. Plot
acquired on 23 November 1919 by Mr Augustin Varenque, for himself and his family.*
