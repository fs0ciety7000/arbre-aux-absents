## OFFICIAL DIRECTORY OF PROFESSIONS

### Republic of Velmarche — 1972 Edition

*Liberal professions, regulated professions, press, technical trades and applied arts. Arranged by profession, then by locality, then in alphabetical order. The particulars are supplied by the persons concerned or by their professional bodies; the publisher accepts no responsibility for their accuracy.*

Price: 45 Fn. Kept up to date each year by loose-leaf inserts.
