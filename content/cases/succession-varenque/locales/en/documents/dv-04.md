::stamp[PAPER FROM THE VARENQUE PAPER MILLS — FERN 1950]

Mr Gaspard Varenque, Director of the Varenque Paper Mills;

Mrs Marcel Daubry, née Blanche Varenque;

her children;

Maître Armand Varenque, barrister;

Miss Hélène Varenque;

Miss Lise Daubry;

her grandchildren;

Miss Adélaïde Varenque, her sister-in-law;

The Sorbelle, Castagne and Daubry families and their connections,

regret to announce the death of

## Mrs Augustin Varenque, widow

### née Henriette Sorbelle

**Founding President of the Washerwomen's Charity**

who died piously on 8 December 1950, at her home in Moulinvert, in her seventy-fifth year.

The funeral will take place on Tuesday 12 December 1950, at ten o'clock, in the church of Moulinvert. Interment will follow in the family vault.

No flowers, by request. Donations will be gratefully received for the Washerwomen's Charity, 22 quai des Tanneurs, Aubrelieu.

*Les Tilleuls, Moulinvert.*
