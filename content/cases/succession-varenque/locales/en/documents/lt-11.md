*A docket from the Vellaak fish market, with printed columns (“species — weight — price”), its back
used for writing. Carpenter’s pencil, large letters. A smell of brine, according to the inventory
card.*

Vellaak, 21 February 1943

Madam,

The parcel has arrived at Vellaak. It coughs but it lives.

The keeper did what was needed, and so did the sea, for once: heavy weather the whole way across,
nobody about to watch what was being loaded. The parcel said nothing the entire crossing. It talks a
little now, at night, when it has a fever. I don’t repeat it.

I am keeping it warm at my place for as long as it takes. The boy thinks it’s family. He isn’t
wrong.

As for payment, it’s done, and it’s too much. I’ll hand the difference on to those who come
through after.

Burn this. You won’t, I know you.

::signature[Maask]
