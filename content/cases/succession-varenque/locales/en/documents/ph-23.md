## Cover

**VELMARCHE ANTENNA** — *the radio and television magazine* — No. 112 — week of 16 to 22 October
1954 — 0.30 Fn

Close-up, a photograph retouched in soft colours. A young woman of about twenty-five, blonde, her
hair swept up in a twist, a winged line of cat-eye eyeliner, pearl earrings, a Claudine collar. She
is smiling in front of a round-grilled studio microphone marked RTV.

In large letters: **MATHILDE, THE VOICE OF THE EVENING**

Subtitle: *Every evening at 20.00 she comes into 60,000 homes. Our announcer takes us behind the
scenes at the RTV.*

## In this issue

- Mathilde, the voice of the evening: “I have never said good evening the same way twice” (p. 4)
- This week’s programmes, radio and television (p. 8)
- Television comes to Port-Sénane: the transmitter on the cape is finished (p. 20)
- Radio serial: *The Wreckers of the Mer Grise*, episode 14 (p. 24)
- Viewers’ letters: “Why does the announcer never say her surname?” Mathilde Rouvière replies:
  “Because I have had two since my wedding, and at eight o’clock I only have time to say one.”
  (p. 30)
