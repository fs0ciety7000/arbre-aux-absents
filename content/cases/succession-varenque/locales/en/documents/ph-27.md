## THE BRENZAC MESSENGER — Brénica — 21 November 1959 — Deaths

### Mr Marc Dobrin

It is with sadness that we learn of the death, which occurred on Tuesday at the hôpital de la
Croix-de-Brénica, of Mr Marc Dobrin, bookkeeper at the Brénica Forestry Cooperative since 1953, at
the age of sixty-three.

Having come from Velmarche in the spring of 1952, like so many others at that time, Mr Dobrin had
found among us work, lodgings in the rue des Scieurs and, as he used to say, “rest”. His colleagues
salute a bookkeeper of rare exactitude, who never left a column without doing it over twice, and a
polite, reserved man who never spoke of his life before. He was known to have neither family nor
correspondence. On the little finger of his right hand he wore a signet ring engraved with a D, “D
for Dobrin”, he would reply to those who remarked on it, and he had a small nick in his right ear,
a souvenir, he said, of a farm dog.

The funeral took place on Thursday at the Brenzac cemetery, in the presence of his colleagues from
the Cooperative and of Mrs Mirela Vasko, his landlady. No heir being known, his effects have been
handed over to the town hall.

seen, its rim nicked. His right hand, resting on the lapel of his jacket, wears a signet ring on the
