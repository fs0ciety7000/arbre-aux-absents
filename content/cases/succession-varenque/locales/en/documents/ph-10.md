## Front

Grey phototype. The lifeboat, long and white, with ten oars, sits on its carriage at the mouth of
the stone boathouse; on the stem one can read *TENNOK*. In front, eight men in a row, cork
lifejackets laced over their oilskins, woollen caps.

In the centre, a step ahead of the others, the coxswain: in his forties, clean-shaven,
weather-beaten, eyes deep-set under his brows, a zigzag jersey under his open oilskin. He holds his
cap in his right hand; the left hangs at his side, and it is missing the ring finger and the little
finger.

In the background on the right, the lighthouse, white with a dark band.

Printed caption:

**THE TENNOK LIFEBOAT AND ITS CREW — COXSWAIN A. VARENQUE**

## Back

Printed back: *POSTCARD. Sold in aid of the Tennok lifeboat fund (Lights and Beacons, Mer Grise
station). 41 lives saved since 1906.* Publisher: *Orvelle Phototype Works, Port-Sénane, “Our
Coasts” series, No. 131*.

::hand[Bought at Tennok, August 1912. He told me the two fingers were the winch, not the sea: “the sea takes all or nothing”. A.]
