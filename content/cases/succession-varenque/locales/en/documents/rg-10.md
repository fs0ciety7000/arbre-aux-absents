::stamp[TOWN HOUSE OF TENNOK]

## BIRTH CERTIFICATE No. 5 — YEAR 1932

In the year nineteen hundred and thirty-two, on the nineteenth of September, at nine o’clock in the
morning, before us, mayor and registrar of the commune of Tennok, there appeared **Firmin
Varenque**, aged thirty-two, fishing skipper, born at the Cap de Tennok, domiciled at Vellaak, who
presented to us a child of the male sex, born the day before, the eighteenth of September, at eleven
o’clock at night, at the Cap de Tennok, in the house of his paternal grandmother, to the informant
and to **Hessa Draavik**, aged twenty-six, midwife, his wife, domiciled with him at Vellaak, and to
whom he declared that he gave the name of

**Tavik**.

The child was delivered by his mother herself, “there being no other midwife on the coast that
night”, as the informant insisted on having written down.

The said declaration and presentation were made in the presence of Ossak Draavik, aged forty-three,
fisherman at Vellaak, maternal uncle of the child, and of Lennart Haavik, aged fifty, shipwright at
Tennok, witnesses, who have signed with the informant and ourselves, after reading.

::signature[F. Varenque — O. Draavik — L. Haavik — the mayor]
