::stamp[CIVIL RE-REGISTRATION OFFICE — FILIATIONS DEPARTMENT — FILE CLOSED]

Aubrelieu, 30 June 1975

**Subject:** closure of file No. 74-0318 — Estate of Adélaïde Varenque.

The table of descent has been settled by the Commission. The re-registrations have been entered in
the registers of the communes concerned. The legacy has been divided. The documents of the Varenque
collection, all read, all referenced, have been returned to the conservation workshop, which has put
them back in new boxes.

It is customary, when a file is closed, for the rapporteur to query the Reader one last time on the
heading that opened it. The printout is attached below, just as the printer produced it.

> LX-2 READER — CENTRAL INDEXED COLLECTION — QUERY No. 75-11872
>
> HEADING REQUESTED: THE ABSENT
>
> RESULT: NO UNATTRIBUTED DOCUMENT.
>
> END OF LIST.

I have never seen the Reader give that answer. I had it checked by extension 212. Extension 212
told me, and I quote, that “the machine has nothing, and that is good news”.

The file is closed. Thank you.

::signature[R. Ferrandot]

::hand[P.S. — A postcard from Vellaak came for you this morning. I did not read it. Well, hardly.]
