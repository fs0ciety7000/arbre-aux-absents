## COMMUNE OF TENNOK — REGISTER OF BIRTHS

### Extracts for the years 1874, 1900 and 1903

*Thin registers: Tennok records scarcely more than four or five births a year. Ostrian names are
written without the feminine suffix, as is the custom in the registers.*

| Year | No. | Child | Born | Father | Mother | Informant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1874 | 2 | Aldrik VARENQUE, male | 5 May 1874, on the cape, in the keeper’s house | Firmin Varenque, aged twenty-four, second master mariner, away at sea | Maaren Tennok, aged twenty-two, his wife | Joren Tennok, keeper of the light, maternal grandfather |
| 1900 | 4 | Firmin VARENQUE, male | 3 March 1900, on the cape | Aldrik Varenque, aged twenty-five, fisherman | Tenna Orrvik, aged twenty-two, his wife, of no occupation | the father |
| 1903 | 6 | Selda VARENQUE, female | 9 July 1903, on the cape | Aldrik Varenque, aged twenty-nine, fisherman, crewman on the lifeboat | Tenna Orrvik, aged twenty-five, his wife, postmistress at Tennok | the father |

### Marginal notes

**In the margin of entry No. 2 of 1874 (Aldrik Varenque):** *“Died at sea, on duty, in the night of
21 to 22 November 1924, off the Cap de Tennok, as lifeboat coxswain at Tennok. Body not
recovered. Declaratory judgment of death by the Vellaak court of 10 March 1925, entered in the
register on 2 April 1925.”*
