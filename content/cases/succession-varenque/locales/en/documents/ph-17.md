::stamp[REGENCY OF VELMARCHE — B.R.R. — IDENTIFICATION SERVICE — CARD No. 41-2207]

## Photographs

Two prints pasted on, 4 × 5 cm, a graduated measuring rule behind. **Full face:** a man of about
thirty, tall (the top of his head almost touches the edge of the frame), hair cropped very short,
the left brow swollen. His mouth is half open; a corner is missing from an upper front tooth.
**Profile (right):** the same man; the hand holding the slate has black nails and fingertips, from
an ink that the depot soap has not shifted.

The slate reads, in chalk: *41-2207 — 24.10.41*.

## Description

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Surname | KESSVEL |
| Given names | Orrin |
| Born | 20 June 1905, at Vellaak (Ostrie) |
| Language | Ostrian (speaks Velmarchois) |
| Occupation | typesetter, formerly of *Vel Taalik* (banned sheet); latterly at the Lanvert printing works, Aubrelieu |
| Height | 1.86 m |
| Hair | fair (cropped at the depot), naturally curly |
| Distinguishing marks | broken upper front tooth; fingers stained with printer’s ink |
| Situation | living with the woman on the preceding card (41-2206), unmarried |
| Case | known as the “moulin de la Sorbe” case; presumed printer for the network |

## Decision

Removal proposed to the Council of Order. Transfer: Kessaar penal colony, men’s section, by the
first convoy. Observation: *Ostrian. Refuses to answer in Velmarchois.*
