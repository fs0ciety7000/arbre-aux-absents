## Front

Small square print with deckled edges, of the kind amateur cameras turn out. A wooden landing
stage, a rowing boat, the still water of a lake, alders, and in the background a chalet with a
fretwork wooden balcony.

In the stern of the boat, a man of about thirty-five in shirtsleeves, a thin moustache, the oars
crossed on his knees; he smiles with dimples, and his left eye turns slightly outwards.

In the bow, a woman of about thirty in a knotted turban, large hoops in both ears, thick black
eyebrows; she is holding by the sash of her dress a little girl of about three who is leaning over
to touch the water.

The little girl has a straight fringe and looks the lens full in the face, with the same slight
outward turn of the left eye as the man.
