::stamp[BRUNESSAU NOTARY’S OFFICE — COPY — FILED WITH ORIGINAL DEED 418]

**AUGUSTIN VARENQUE**
Deputy for the Orvelle — Les Tilleuls, Moulinvert

Moulinvert, 14 May 1918

To Maître Achille Brunesseau, notary at Saint-Ormel

Maître,

In my capacity as executor of the will of my late brother Lucien Varenque, who fell on 19 March
last, I have acquainted myself with the state of his affairs. It has come to my notice that you hold
in your office a deed deposited by him on 6 October 1917, concerning a child born in 1906 at
Aubrelieu.

I would ask you kindly to **take no further action** on this deposit, to forward it to no
registrar, and to issue a copy of it to no one.

I ask this in the interest of the family, and in that of the child himself, who bears his mother’s
name, an honest name of which he need not be ashamed, and who has no need whatever to bear a second
one for which he would certainly be reproached. My brother was an artist; he signed a great many
things on an impulse of the heart. I am charged with his affairs, not with his affairs of the heart.

The fees for safekeeping will be settled by me. I count on your discretion; you may count on my
gratitude.

I remain, Maître, yours faithfully,

::signature[A. Varenque]
