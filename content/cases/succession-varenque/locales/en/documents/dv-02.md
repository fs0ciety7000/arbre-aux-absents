## LES TILLEULS — 10 JUNE 1922

### Wedding banquet of Mr Gaspard Varenque and Miss Irène Castagne

*Card printed on fern paper from the Varenque Paper Mills. Left-hand leaf: plan of the top table, names in calligraphy in violet ink. Right-hand leaf: menu.*

---

### Top table (under the wisteria)

**Front-steps side, from left to right:**

- Mr Firmin Varenque, of Tennok
- Miss Blanche
- Mr Daubry
- Mr Augustin Varenque
- **Mr Gaspard Varenque**
- **Mrs Gaspard Varenque, née Irène Castagne**

**Garden side, from left to right:**

- Mr Albert Castagne
- Mrs Augustin Varenque
- Mrs Anselme Varenque
- Miss Adélaïde Varenque
- Miss Odile
- ::hand[a place with no name, marked with a small cross in pencil]

### Guests' tables

The Paper Mills table (Mr Vauquelin, chief engineer, and Mrs Vauquelin; the foremen) — The Port-Sénane table (the bride's family and friends) — The girls' school table (the schoolmistresses) — The children's table.

---

### MENU

Chicken consommé with asparagus tips

Clairette trout in brown butter

Saddle of Corvins lamb, new potatoes, garden peas from Les Tilleuls

Cheeses of the valley

“The Fern” set piece

Strawberries in Sorbe wine

**Wines:** Saint-Ormel white 1915 — Red from the Sorbe hillsides 1911, the Centenary year — Hautcorvin sparkling wine

Coffee — Liqueurs

---

::hand[On the back, in pencil, in Adélaïde's hand: the little cross is Lucien's place. Nobody sat there. Odile put her posy on the plate.]
