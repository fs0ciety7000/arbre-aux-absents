## Front

Silver gelatin print, black and white, 13 × 18 cm, white borders. A very old lady, sitting bolt
upright in a wing chair, near a window with misted panes. White hair braided into a crown. A beauty
spot on the right cheek. A silver fob watch pinned to a dark dress with a white collar. On her
knees, an open school exercise book; she is not looking at it. She looks at the lens the way one
looks at a pupil who has not learnt the lesson.

Typed caption pasted beneath the picture:

> **Moulinvert. — Miss Adélaïde Varenque is 90.** She whom three generations of Moulinvert women
> still call “Miss” celebrated her ninetieth birthday at Les Tilleuls, surrounded by her former
> pupils. Schoolmistress at the girls’ school from 1899 to 1944, she was its headmistress for nearly
> forty years. “I never left the school,” she tells us. “The school left me.” The *Gazette* offers
> her its respectful good wishes.
