*A sheet of cream laid paper, written in black ink in a close hand that does not slant. No date, no
place, no signature beyond an initial. The envelope has been kept.*

::stamp[VELMARCHE POSTS — VELLAAK — 3 IV 62]

A.,

Tell her nothing as long as we have no proof.

I know what you will answer: that the proof is the forelock. A forelock is proof for you and for
me; for a court, it is a whim of the hair. The Grisenoue papers burned, you know that as well as I
do. Whoever had them burned knew what he was doing.

If we tell her without proof, we give her a mother she can show to no one, and we take away the one
she has. The one she has brought her up. That is not nothing. I am not one of those women who take
back what was stolen from them by stealing in their turn.

I am writing to you on our fern: I have a few sheets of it left. When there are none, I shall write
to you on anything at all.

Take care of yourself. Take care of her from a distance.

::signature[N.]

::hand[P.S. — If one day someone comes looking, let them leave me absent, but let her be told.]
