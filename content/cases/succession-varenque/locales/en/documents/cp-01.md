## Front

Printed caption: AUBRELIEU — The place de la Charte and the Morel column. — Studio Lumen Phototype, No. 118.

## Back

::stamp[VELMARCHE POSTS — AUBRELIEU — 9 MAY 05 — 5 S.]

*Address:* Mrs Anselme Varenque, Les Tilleuls, Moulinvert, Clairette valley.

::hand[Dearest Mama,]

::hand[Lucien's poster is everywhere: on the columns, at the baker's, even on the cabs. You see nothing else, even beside Augustin's, which are twice as many and looked at half as much. Augustin is furious about it and Lucien delighted, which changes nothing whatever.]

::hand[His washerwoman has P.'s smile – you know which P. I shall say no more on an open card. Look at the column in the photograph: that is her.]

::hand[I come home on Thursday by the four o'clock ferry. Give Papa a kiss from me, and tell him I have found his ink.]

::signature[Adé]
