## LUCIEN VARENQUE (1875–1918)

### Poster artist — Retrospective

Galerie de la Charte, 4 place de la Charte, Aubrelieu — From 10 April to 15 May 1920 — Daily from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., Mondays excepted — Admission: 1 florin, in aid of the orphans of the War of the Passes

*On the cover: Self-Portrait in a Hat, 1910, oil on canvas (No. 1).*

---

### Foreword

Lucien Varenque died on the Col de Corvenne on 19 March 1918, painting rocks for the army. He had spent his life painting walls for passers-by. This exhibition would like to gather, for a few weeks, what he had scattered all over Velmarche: posters that were torn down, sketches he gave to whoever asked for them, canvases he never showed.

People thought him light-hearted. He was, in the way of those who carry a great deal. His friends know what his laughter hid; they will not say it here. Let the visitor simply look, at No. 1, at that face which stares at us sidelong, with the left eye that wanders off to look elsewhere: “my painter's eye,” he used to say, “the one that sees what the other won't.”

::signature[Émile Fontagne, for the friends of Lucien Varenque]

### Catalogue of works

**Paintings**

1. *Self-Portrait in a Hat*, 1910. Oil on canvas. Lent by Miss A. Varenque.
2. *Les Tilleuls in Winter*, 1898. Oil on canvas. Lent by Mrs Anselme Varenque.
3. *The Orvelle Bridge, Evening*, 1903. Oil on board. Private collection.
4. *Wash-house on the Orvelle, quai des Tanneurs*, 1904. Oil on canvas. Private collection.
5. *Rocks at the Pass, a Camouflage Study*, 1917. Distemper on tarpaulin. Lent by the Ministry of War.

**Posters and studies for posters**

6. *Mérande Soapworks — Fairy Soap*, 1904. Colour lithograph poster.
7. *Belaube Department Store — Christmas*, 1906. Colour lithograph poster.
8. *The Golfe d'Ambre — Orvelle Packet Company*, 1908. Colour lithograph poster.
9. *Varenque Paper Mills — The Paper That Does Not Lie*, 1909. Poster. Lent by the Varenque Paper Mills.
10. *Aubrelieu Opera — 1911 Season*, 1911. Lithograph poster.
11. *The Charter Centenary Celebrations*, 1911. Official poster.
12. *Bains-de-Mérande — Its Baths, Its Springs*, 1912. Lithograph poster.
13. *The Washerwoman*, 1905. Colour lithograph poster for the Mérande Soapworks. Lent by the Mérande Soapworks.
14. *The Washerwoman*, study for the poster (**Pauline S., model**), 1905. Charcoal and white chalk on buff paper. Private collection.
15. *The Washerwoman*, study of hands, 1905. Red chalk. Private collection.

**Drawings and sketchbooks**

16. *Rue des Tanneurs Sketchbook*, 1904–1906. Forty-two leaves. Lent by Miss A. Varenque.
17. *Children's Heads*, c. 1907–1912. Twelve pencil studies of the same little boy. Private collection.
18. *The Paper Aeroplane, for V.*, 1913. Pen and watercolour, with this inscription: “For my aviator, when he is grown up.” Private collection.
19. *Sketchbook from the Pass*, 1916–1918. Sketches of soldiers, mountains and rocks. Lent by Miss A. Varenque.
