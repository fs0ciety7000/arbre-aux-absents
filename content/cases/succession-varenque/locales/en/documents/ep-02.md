## Front

Colour postcard, a recent printing in slightly too gentle tints. The Tennok lighthouse at dusk,
white with a red band, the lantern lit. The sea is calm. On the gallery, tiny, someone is leaning
on the rail and watching the light.

Printed at the bottom: *TENNOK — The lighthouse, automated since 1972.*

## Back

Addressed: *To the rapporteur for file 74-0318, Civil Re-registration Office, Aubrelieu.*

Two hands. The first slanting, firm, in black ink; the second rounder, in blue ink, like someone
writing on one knee.

::hand[The Tennok light does not keep itself: someone has to look.]

::hand[Thank you for looking.]

::hand[— O. and É.]

::stamp[VELMARCHE POSTS — VELLAAK — 26 VI 75]
