## Front

Glossy black-and-white press print, 18 × 24 cm, traces of white gouache retouching on the sky. In
the foreground, a young man of about twenty-five, leaning one shoulder against the propeller of a
biplane. Leather helmet, flying goggles pushed up on his forehead, leather jacket open over a white
scarf. He is grinning, with two dimples; his left eye turns slightly outwards, which makes him look
at once at the photographer and at someone beside him.

On the cowling, a painted swallow and the name *L’Hirondelle*; on the fuselage, the registration
and the letters *AÉRO-VELMARCHE — AIRMAIL*. Behind a barrier, a blurred crowd in straw hats; on the
right, a mechanic wiping his hands.

## Back

Typed label from the photographic department, stuck on askew:

> **Aubrelieu–Kessaar–Aubrelieu mail flight.** The pilot Victor Sauge, aviator with
> Aéro-Velmarche, in front of *L’Hirondelle* at the Plaine-aux-Saules aerodrome, on his return from
> the first mail service across the Mer Grise (18 July 1931). Flying time: 6 hours 12 minutes. Mail
> carried: 1,400 items.
