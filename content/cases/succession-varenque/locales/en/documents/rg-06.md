::stamp[CITY OF AUBRELIEU — CIVIL REGISTRY — 4TH WARD (TANNEURS)]

## BIRTH CERTIFICATE No. 731 — YEAR 1906

In the year nineteen hundred and six, on the fourteenth of May, at ten o’clock in the morning,
before us, assistant to the mayor of the Fourth Ward, registrar by delegation, there appeared Joseph
Tissier, aged forty, tanner, residing at Aubrelieu, 16 rue des Tanneurs, a neighbour, who presented
to us a child of the male sex, born on the twelfth of this month, at five o’clock in the morning, at
his mother’s home, and to whom he declared that he gave the name of

**Victor**,

son of **Pauline Sauge**, aged twenty-one, laundress, residing at Aubrelieu, 14 rue des Tanneurs,
and of a **father not named**.

The child shall bear his mother’s name: **Victor Sauge**.

The said declaration and presentation were made in the presence of Marthe Bréval, aged thirty-four,
seamstress, and of Eugène Lacombe, aged fifty-eight, lodging-house landlord, both residing in the rue
des Tanneurs, witnesses, who have signed with the informant and ourselves, after reading. The
mother, confined to her bed, did not appear.

::signature[J. Tissier — M. Bréval — E. Lacombe — the assistant to the mayor]

*Marginal notes: none.*
