## Front

Warm-toned albumen print, somewhat faded, mounted on cream card with gilt corners. Studio set: a
painted backcloth of a balustrade and a potted palm, a fringed rug.

Standing on the left, a young man of about twenty-five, thick brown hair swept back, mutton-chop
whiskers trimmed short. A pale scar runs through his left eyebrow. A new frock coat, too stiff, a
watch chain across the waistcoat. He has laid his hand on the back of the armchair the way one lays
a hand on a machine one has just bought.

Seated in the armchair, a young woman of about twenty. Brown hair in bandeaux parted in the
middle; above the forehead, on the left, a distinctly white forelock. A pale dress with a high lace
collar, fastened by a small brooch in the shape of a fern frond. She holds a bouquet of ferns and
lily of the valley, and she is barely smiling, from the left corner of her mouth.

Printed in gold letters beneath the image: *Studio Lumen — Aubrelieu — Portraits & cabinet cards*.

## Back

Card printed with the studio’s device (a rising sun above a lens): *Studio Lumen, 7 rue des
Orfèvres, Aubrelieu. Negatives are kept. Fresh prints may be obtained at any time.*
