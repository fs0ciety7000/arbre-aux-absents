::stamp[CITY OF AUBRELIEU — CIVIL REGISTRY — 1ST WARD]

## MARRIAGE CERTIFICATE No. 212 — YEAR 1897

In the year eighteen hundred and ninety-seven, on the twenty-fourth of April, at half past eleven
in the morning, at the town hall of Aubrelieu, before us, assistant to the mayor, registrar by
delegation, there appeared:

**Augustin Jacques Hippolyte Varenque**, bachelor of laws, aged twenty-five, born at Moulinvert on
the eleventh of January eighteen hundred and seventy-two, residing there in the house known as Les
Tilleuls, son, of age, of Anselme Varenque, papermaker, and of Clémence Aubertot, his wife, present
and consenting;

and **Henriette Louise Sorbelle**, of no occupation, aged twenty, born at Aubrelieu on the
twenty-third of May eighteen hundred and seventy-six, residing there, quai des Chartes, daughter,
under age, of Édouard Sorbelle, banker, managing partner of the Sorbelle Bank, and of Louise
Ferrand, his wife, present, assisting and consenting;

who, the documents having been read and the banns published without objection, and having declared
to us that a marriage contract was drawn up on the twentieth of this month by Maître Gérard
Lassalle, notary at Aubrelieu, each in turn declared that they take one another as husband and
wife. We have pronounced in the name of the law that Augustin Varenque and Henriette Sorbelle are
united in marriage.

In the presence of Hippolyte Aubertot, aged seventy-seven, retired pharmacist, grandfather of the
groom; of Gustave Marchenoir, aged fifty-two, banker; of Jules Ferrand, aged forty-eight, judge of
the court of appeal, uncle of the bride; and of Antoine Belaube, aged forty-five, merchant;
witnesses of full age, who have signed with the spouses, their fathers and mothers and ourselves,
after reading.

::signature[A. Varenque — H. Sorbelle]
