## Front

Full-page photogravure, deep blacks, on heavy paper.

A woman of about fifty photographs herself in a large mirror with foxed silvering. She holds a
top-viewfinder camera against her chest and looks not at the lens but at her own reflection,
straight in the eye. Hair drawn back in a low bun; above the forehead, on the left, a white
forelock. An Ostrian zigzag jersey, sleeves pushed up. Her right hand rests on the shutter release,
its back turned towards the mirror: on it can be seen a broad, pale, shiny scar, like an old burn.

Behind her, in the mirror, a small-paned window looking onto a fishing harbour, drying racks, a
stretch of fjord. On the windowsill, a fern in a pot.

Caption printed beneath the plate:

*XXXVI. — Self-portrait. Vellaak, 1958.*
