## Front

Printed caption, in two languages: TENNOK — The lighthouse and the Dents. — TENNOK — *Tenn ta Kessdaan.* — Published by the Tennok Post Office.

## Back

::stamp[TENNOK POST OFFICE — THE POSTMISTRESS T. ORRVIK — 14 AUGUST 1930]

*Address:* Miss A. Varenque, headmistress of the girls' school, Moulinvert, Clairette valley.

::hand[Aunt Adé,]

::hand[Three weeks here and I never want to leave. I sleep in the room that was the woman keeper's, the one that looks out on the light. Firmin is teaching me to sail: I capsize with method. His Hessa laughs at my forelock; she says in Ostrian they call that a gull's feather and it brings boats luck. I believe her, she has brought half of Vellaak into the world.]

::hand[Cousin Tenna gives me the prettiest stamps and makes me taste the dried fish. Selda has lent me books in Ostrian that I cannot read. I am drawing the ferns on the heath for Grandmama.]

::hand[Don't tell Papa I smoke Firmin's pipe.]

::signature[Odile]
