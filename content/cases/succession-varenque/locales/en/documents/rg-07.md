## MARRIAGE CERTIFICATE No. 23 — YEAR 1922

In the year nineteen hundred and twenty-two, on the tenth of June, at ten o’clock in the morning, at
the Town House of Moulinvert, before us, mayor and registrar, there appeared:

**Gaspard Anselme Varenque**, engineer and papermaker, aged twenty-three, born at Moulinvert on the
eighth of October eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, residing there at Les Tilleuls, son, of age, of
Augustin Varenque, former Deputy, chairman of the Varenque Paper Mills, and of Henriette Sorbelle,
his wife, present and consenting;

and **Irène Castagne**, pianist, aged nineteen, born at Port-Sénane on the twenty-first of September
nineteen hundred and two, residing there, daughter, under age, of Albert Castagne, shipowner, and of
Hortense Delage, his wife, present, assisting and consenting;

the banns having been published at Moulinvert and at Port-Sénane without objection, and the
marriage being celebrated at the groom’s residence. They each in turn declared that they take one
another as husband and wife, and we have pronounced in the name of the law that Gaspard Varenque and
Irène Castagne are united in marriage.

In the presence of Émile Castagne, aged fifty, shipowner at Port-Sénane, uncle of the bride, and of
Léon Vauquelin, aged forty-four, chief engineer at the Varenque Paper Mills, witnesses, who have
signed with the spouses, their parents and ourselves, after reading.

::signature[G. Varenque — Irène Castagne]
