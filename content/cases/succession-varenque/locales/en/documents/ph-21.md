## Front

Black-and-white press print. A workshop under the eaves: wooden dress forms in half-sewn gowns, rolls
of fabric, a large cutting table, an iron on its stove.

Standing on a low dais, a woman of about forty in a trained stage gown embroidered with little
lanterns; short wavy hair, a beauty spot above the lip on the left, a string of pearls. She has her
arms raised so that the waist can be worked on, and she is talking, visibly, without pause.

Kneeling at her feet, a woman of about thirty-five in a knotted turban, large hoops in both ears,
thick black eyebrows; three pins between her lips, a pincushion strapped to her wrist. She does not
look up.

## Back

::stamp[AUBRELIEU OPERA — PRESS OFFICE — 1948 SEASON — FREE OF RIGHTS FOR PRESS USE]

Typewritten caption: *Revival of “The Lantern of Ostrie”. Mrs Ninon Sauge, head costume maker,
adjusts the gown of Mrs Blanche Aubertine.*

Typed text of the press release, pasted on:

> The Aubrelieu Opera revives *The Lantern of Ostrie* by Aurèle Vasquin on 4 December, in the sets
> and costumes of the 1929 première, restored by the house’s own workshops. The soprano Blanche
> Aubertine returns to the role that made her name. The costumes are by Mrs Ninon Sauge, costume
> maker at the Opera since 1932 and head costume maker since 1946.
