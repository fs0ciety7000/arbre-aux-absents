**THE HERALD** — Organ of Velmarchois Order — Sunday 11 October 1942 — 50 sous

## THE FAMILY PAGE

### Families of order welcome the wards

*This year the Wards’ Charity of the Regency has placed more than three hundred children in homes worthy of the Nation. The Commissioner of Registers sets the example.*

There are children whom the Nation did not choose and whom it does not abandon. They are the **wards** of Order: foundlings, children of failing families, children whose parents, by their crimes, have cut themselves off from the Velmarchois community. For them, in 1938, the Regency created the **Wards’ Charity**, whose home at Grisenoue, north of the capital, takes in, cares for and educates several hundred little Velmarchois every year.

The Charity keeps children no longer than it must. As soon as a sound home presents itself, it places them. The ward receives a surname, a Christian name of order if he has none that is fitting, and a family that will teach him what his parents could not. More than three hundred placements have been made since January: it is a record, and an honour for those **families of order** who have opened their doors.

### “She knew straight away who her mother was”

Among them, one of the most prominent in the capital. **The Commissioner and Mrs Daubry and their little Lise** received us in their flat on the avenue de la Charte, where the child, who arrived at the beginning of the summer at a few months old, already, by her parents’ admission, “lays down the law in the house”.

“We had been waiting for her for nineteen years,” Mrs Daubry told us, laughing. “She came all by herself, she knew straight away who her mother was.” The Commissioner of Registers, more reserved, was content to add: “A child is guilty of nothing. It is for us to see that he does not become so.” The judgment of adoption was handed down last month by the Aubrelieu court.

Little Lise, who had kindly agreed to wake up for our photographer, showed little interest in the interview and a great deal in our lady reporter’s hat.

### How to become a family of order

Velmarchois households married for at least three years, of good character and entered without reservation in the registers, may send their application to the Wards’ Charity of the Regency, Aubrelieu office, at the Citadel. A monthly allowance of 120 order florins is paid until the child comes of age.
