*Letterhead of the Hôtel des Thermes, Bains-de-Mérande, written in pencil on both sides. A draft:
crossings-out, fresh starts, one sentence begun three times. The letter as sent is not in the
collection.*

Bains-de-Mérande, July 1961, in the night

N.,

I saw her at Blanche’s farewell concert. ~~I think that~~ She has your forelock. She is nineteen.

She was turning the pages. Blanche sang the Lantern, as in ’29, a tone lower, and everyone wept for
Blanche. I was watching the small hand that turned the pages ~~and which~~ at the right moment,
always a little ahead, the way you did when you read over my shoulder.

She hides the forelock under a ribbon. ~~Like~~ Blanche says she has been hiding it for two or
three years, since the boys began to look. Blanche says “my daughter” and she is right to say it.
~~I have not~~ I said nothing. I did not move. An old woman crying at a farewell concert – nobody
looks at her.

~~What should I do~~ ~~Tell me what I should do~~ Tell me what you want. Not what is right: what
you want.

::hand[Across the margin: do not write the given name. Write nothing that has a name.]

A.
