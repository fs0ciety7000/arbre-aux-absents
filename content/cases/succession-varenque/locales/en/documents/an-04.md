## REGISTER OF LIGHTS

### Light No. 7 — Cap de Tennok

**Station:** first-order lighthouse, cylindrical masonry tower, white with a red band, height 31 metres. White light, group flashing in threes, period 15 seconds, range 22 nautical miles. First-order lens from the Hautcorvin Glassworks (1889), replacing the apparatus of 1851. Fog signal. Lifeboat house at the foot of the cape. Charted danger: the Dents de Tennok reef, 1.2 nautical miles to the north-west.

**First lit:** 1 November 1850.

### Service record of the permanent keepers

| Period | Permanent keeper | Remarks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1850–1883 | Joren Tennok | First keeper. Died in service, March 1883. |
| 1883–1912 | **Mrs Maaren Varenque, née Tennok** | Daughter of the above. Appointed on a provisional basis on 14 March 1883, no other candidate being available; given a permanent post on 1 January 1884 by ministerial decision. First woman keeper of a first-order light. Retired on 31 December 1912. |
| 1912–1950 | Jorre Haask | Formerly assistant keeper (1905–1912). Appointed on 31 December 1912. Retired on 8 December 1950. |
| 1950–1972 | **Tavik Varenque** | Descendant of Joren Tennok. Appointed on a provisional basis on 9 December 1950, aged eighteen; given a permanent post on 1 July 1951. Last permanent keeper. |

### Final entry

**Light automated on 1 June 1972.** Electric supply by submarine cable, remote watch from the Vellaak station. The post of permanent keeper is abolished from that date. The keeper's quarters are left at the disposal of the last holder of the post, free of charge and for life, at his request.
