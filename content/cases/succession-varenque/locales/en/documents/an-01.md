## DIRECTORY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY OF VELMARCHE

### Forty-third year — 1906 — Grand Duchy of Velmarche, Aubrelieu and the provinces

*Containing: the addresses of all tradesmen, manufacturers, craftsmen and members of the liberal professions in the Grand Duchy, arranged by profession and by locality; the list of the streets of Aubrelieu with their residents; the rates of the Posts and the telegraph; fairs and markets.*

Price: 8 florins in paper covers, 10 florins bound in cloth.
