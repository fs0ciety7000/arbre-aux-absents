## The Velmarche Eye — No. 2511 — 21 April 1951 — 20 order florins

### THE EYE’S SOCIAL DIARY — The wedding of the year

On Saturday Moulinvert saw the wedding the whole capital had been talking of since the winter: that
of Maître Armand Varenque, barrister at the Aubrelieu Bar, son of the industrialist Gaspard
Varenque, and Miss Mathilde Rouvière, the well-known RTV announcer, whose smile viewers wait for
every evening before the news bulletin.

The bride wore an ivory faille gown with a Claudine collar, of a simplicity that surprised and
delighted. The groom, true to his reputation, wore a bow tie, even, we are assured, during Mass. The
reception was held at Les Tilleuls, the family home, beneath the lime trees in question, which were
not yet in flower but had been asked to make an effort.

Among the guests were a good many figures from the Bar and from Velmarche Radio-Television, as well
as the eldest guest of all, the schoolmistress of Moulinvert, who pointed out to the bride that
people on television do not articulate properly.

The young couple will settle in Aubrelieu. Miss Rouvière, who will henceforth be Mrs Varenque, has
reassured her admirers: she will go on wishing them good evening.

### Photograph

On the forecourt of a church, under a shower of rice. A young man in his twenties with black hair,
big ears, a cleft chin, morning coat and bow tie, laughing as he shields himself with one hand. On
his arm, a young blonde woman with her hair swept up in a twist, a winged line of cat-eye eyeliner,
pearl earrings, a dress with a Claudine collar and a short veil.
