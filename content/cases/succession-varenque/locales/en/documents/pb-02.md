## ELECTIONS TO THE CHAMBER OF DEPUTIES

### Orvelle Constituency — Poll of Sunday 14 May 1905

**ELECTORS OF THE ORVELLE!**

## VOTE AUGUSTIN VARENQUE

### Candidate of the Liberal Union

**A man of the valley, for the valley.**

Born at Moulinvert, son and grandson of millers and papermakers of the Clairette, **Augustin Varenque**, thirty-three years of age, read law at Aubrelieu and has worked for ten years at the Moulinvert Paper Mills, the foremost paper manufactory in the Grand Duchy, which keeps six hundred families of our constituency in bread.

He asks for your votes to become your **Deputy**, and he tells you what he will do:

- **A bridge over the Clairette** below Moulinvert, promised to you these thirty years.
- **Electric light** in every township of the valley before 1910.
- **Protection for Velmarchois labour** against foreign paper and cloth.
- **One more school** in every canton, and better-paid teachers.
- **The relief fund** for sick workers, extended to their widows.

*“I promise only what I can deliver.”*

Electors, on 14 May you will choose between speeches and work.

**VOTE, AND GET OTHERS TO VOTE, FOR AUGUSTIN VARENQUE!**
