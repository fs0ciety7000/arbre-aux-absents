## BULLETIN OF ORDER

### Official Journal of the Regency of Velmarche — Saturday 19 February 1938 — No. 7

*A page printed in two columns on the Bulletin’s grey paper, in close-set type. Price per issue:
twenty order sous.*

**CENSUS AND REMOVALS BUREAU — REMOVAL LIST No. 3**

**(Journalists and contributors to sheets in the Ostrian language)**

Having regard to the Act of 2 February 1938 on the purity of the registers; having regard to the
decree of 3 February 1938 banning sheets published in the Ostrian language; on the report of the
Census and Removals Bureau,

**Article 1.** — The persons named below are struck off the civil registers of Velmarche and
**declared absent**. They may neither contract, nor own property, nor bear witness, nor be entered
in any register whatsoever. Registrars shall enter the note “struck off” in the margin of their
certificates.

| No. | Surname | Given names | Born, at | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | AASVEL | Mette | 3 May 1897, Tormaas | correspondent |
| 2 | DAAREN | Vilmo | 12 January 1880, Vellaak | translator |
| 3 | KESSMAR | Holdar | 30 August 1871, Vellaak | printer and manager |
| 4 | ORRMAAS | Liva | 18 April 1909, Vikmaar | correspondent |
| 5 | VARENQUE | Selda | 9 July 1903, Tennok | woman editor of the banned sheet *Vel Taalik* |
| 6 | VELDRIK | Tormen | 2 November 1876, Vellaak | editor-in-chief of the banned sheet *Vel Taalik* |

**Article 2.** — The sheet known as *Vel Taalik*, edited by the man Tormen Veldrik and the woman
Selda Varenque, is dissolved. Its presses, type and archives are seized for the benefit of the
Bureau.

**Article 3.** — Any person who harbours, employs or corresponds with an absentee renders himself
liable to the penalties laid down in Article 11 of the aforementioned Act.

::signature[For the Regent and by delegation, the Head of the Census and Removals Bureau]
