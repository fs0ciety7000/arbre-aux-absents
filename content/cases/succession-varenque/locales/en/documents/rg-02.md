## MARRIAGE CERTIFICATE No. 3 — YEAR 1873

*A large leaf from the register, Velmarchois text on the left, Ostrian text on the right, in two
different hands. The Ostrian text is written in the spelling used before the reform of 1898.*

### Velmarchois text

In the year eighteen hundred and seventy-three, on the twenty-first of June, at eleven o’clock in
the morning, before us, Mattis Vaaskel, mayor and registrar of the commune of Tennok, there appeared
publicly at the Town House:

**Firmin Varenque**, second master mariner with the Orvelle Packet Company, born at
Moulinvert on the seventeenth of August eighteen hundred and forty-nine, residing at Port-Sénane,
son, of age, of Jacques Varenque, miller at Moulinvert, consenting by the attached deed, and of the
late Rose Méral, his wife;

and **Maaren Tennok**, called in the Ostrian tongue *Maaren Tennokessa*, of no occupation, born at
the Cap de Tennok on the second of April eighteen hundred and fifty-two, daughter, of age, of Joren
Tennok, keeper of the light at Tennok, here present and consenting, and of the late Ilka Venn, his
wife;

who have asked us to proceed to the celebration of their marriage, the banns having been published
at Tennok and at Port-Sénane without objection. The documents having been read, and the parties
having each declared in their own tongue that they take one another as husband and wife, we have
pronounced in the name of the law that they are united in marriage.

In the presence of Hubert Davennes, aged thirty-one, first officer of the steamer *Orvelle*, and of
Kaarel Vennik, aged forty, fisherman at Tennok, witnesses, who have signed with the spouses, the
bride’s father and ourselves, after reading.

### Ostrian text

*Tennok, 21.6.1873. Maaren Tennokessa, Joren Tennok-tiira, ta Firmin Varenque, vel-vaard, Moulinvert-sonn, ommen kaavel tenn ta vel ees. Draa ta orr, ommen aar.*
