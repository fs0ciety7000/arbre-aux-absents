## Front

Black-and-white print, sharp, tightly framed. A small post office with panelled walls. Behind a
counter with a brass grille, a woman of about fifty, round spectacles, freckles, a dark headscarf
knotted at the nape, black lustre oversleeves on her forearms. She has a broad smile and a rubber
stamp in her raised hand, as though about to cancel the lens. On the lapel of her bodice, a
mourning band.

Behind her: numbered sorting pigeonholes, a pair of scales, a Posts calendar open at the month of
May, a telegraph under its cover.

Caption printed in the margin of the print:

*Our Rural Post Offices — Tennok post office (Mer Grise). The postmistress, Mrs Tenna Orrvik, in
post since 1901, also runs the telegraph and the savings bank.*
