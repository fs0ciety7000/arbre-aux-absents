## What the Reader is

The **LX-2 Reader** is a query terminal connected to the **Central Indexed Collection** of the
National Archives: a magnetic tape on which the indexers at the Palais des Chartes have entered, for
each document, its **headings**. A heading is the name of a person, place, organisation or event,
or a subject word, as it is written in the document.

The Reader does not read documents. It reads what indexers have noted about them. It does not
guess, it does not assume, it has no imagination; that is its chief virtue.

## Querying

1. Type **one heading** on the keyboard, then press the SEND key.
2. The screen displays the list of matching references; the printer runs off a slip.
3. Hand the slip to the duty archivist: the documents are brought to your office, in original or on
   microfiche.

The Reader ignores capitals, accents and punctuation: *Sénane*, *SENANE* and *senane* are, to it,
the same heading. On the other hand, it knows only **the exact term**. A typing error, a synonym or
a circumlocution returns nothing. “The old lady’s house” is not a heading. “Moulinvert” is.

## What does not work

- The Reader does not complete names. If you know only the initial of a given name, search for
  something else: the surname, the place, the firm.
- It does not translate. Ostrian terms are indexed as they are written in the documents; you must
  have read them to search for them.
- It will never tell you that a person does not exist. It will tell you that it has nothing. That is
  not the same thing; in archives, it is in fact the opposite.

## Restricted collections

Under Article 9 of the Act of 14 May 1973, the collections of the Census and Removals Bureau, the
Kessaar penal colony and the Wards’ Charity are released only **on request by name**: given name
and surname of the person concerned, in that order, exactly. A query by place or by subject returns
the press and the ordinary collections, never those registers. The Reader will then display
“RESTRICTED COLLECTION — REQUEST BY NAME REQUIRED”. This is not a fault.

## In case of difficulty

The printer jams about once a day, always just when one is in a hurry. Do not pull on the paper.
Call extension 212.
