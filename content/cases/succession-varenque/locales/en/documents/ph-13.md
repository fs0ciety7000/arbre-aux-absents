## VEL TAALIK — the Messenger of the Sea

**Bilingual Ostrian–Velmarchois newspaper — Vellaak — founded 1898 — No. 1912 — March 1936 — 15 sous**

### Vel Taalik ta Vellaak — The Messenger and its home

On the occasion of our 1912th number, our readers have asked us what it looks like, the house that
brings them the news of the coast every Saturday in both their languages. Here it is. It fits into
two rooms above the cod-drying shed on the jetée Est; you can hear the gulls there better than the
telephone, and the smell, we are told, can be recognised on the paper as far away as Aubrelieu.

In it you will find a treadle press of 1902, which works; a case of Ostrian type cast specially,
with the double *aa* and *ee* that no foundry in the capital sells; a woman editor; a typesetter;
and a cat with no name, because nobody could agree on one.

The editor, Selda Varenque, was born at the Tennok lighthouse. A journalist on *Vel Taalik* since
1925, she has been in charge of the paper since 1931. She writes the articles, checks the
advertisements, translates the official notices, answers the post, and smokes. She asks us to make
it clear that the paper holds no political opinion, and that it stands for schooling in Ostrian,
free fishing and the right of women to keep a lighthouse.

The typesetter, O. Kessvel, is from Vellaak. He sets a page in two hours in both languages, sings
out of tune in Ostrian as he does so, and has never let a misprint through, save one, in 1934,
which we shall not repeat.

### Photograph

A low-ceilinged workshop cluttered with reams of paper. Standing on the left, a woman of about
thirty, hair in a short bob, freckles, a man’s jacket with the sleeves rolled up, thin spectacles on
the end of a chain; she is reading a proof, an unlit cigarillo between her lips. On the right, in
front of a type case, a tall man of about thirty, curly fair hair under a flat cap, his fingers
blackened with ink; he has looked up and is laughing towards the lens, which shows a broken front
tooth.

Caption, in Ostrian and then in Velmarchois:

*Selda Varenque ta O. Kessvel, Vel Taalik.*

*Selda Varenque, editor, and the typesetter O. Kessvel, in the newspaper’s workshop.*
