letter is missing and only a blank remains. Signature and postscript by hand.*

Vellaak, 3 July 1972

Dear Adélaïde,

Th  Tennok lighthouse has been coming on by itself since 1 June. An engineer from Port-Sénane came
to fit a clock, a relay and a gr y box that goes tick; he shook Tavik’s hand, he said “there you
are, a free man”, and he left before nightfall so as not to se  the first time.

Tavik won’t leave the k eper’s house. He says the light doesn’t need him any more, but the birds
do. He has kept the r gister of the light, with his drawings in the margins; he rereads it in the
evenings the way others reread their l tters. I go up to see him on Sundays. We don’t talk much. It
is very restful.

Here, *Vel Taalik* prints nine hundred copies. At the café they call me “the doyenne”, which is a
polite way of saying I am not dead yet.

The photographer is well. She asks after you, as always, without seeming to.

With lov .

Selda Varenque

::signature[Selda]

::hand[P.S. The machine still eats its e’s. I shan’t buy another: it went into exile with me.]
