**THE BRENZAC MESSENGER** — Velmarchois edition — Brénica, Saturday 19 November 1960

## DEATH NOTICES

**TOMIN** (Vesko). — The Tomin and Karvelin families regret to announce the death of Mr Vesko Tomin,
wheelwright, at Brénica on 14 November, in his eightieth year. The funeral took place on Thursday.

**DALVARIN** (Danela). — We are asked to announce the death of Danela Dalvarin, engraver, formerly of
the Hautcorvin Glassworks, who passed away at Dalvica on 11 November, in her seventy-third year. She
rests in the cemetery of Dalvica, above the Dalva. No flowers and no speeches, by her wish. From her
son, Andrin Dalvarin.

**RUZELIN** (Mila). — Mr Petko Ruzelin and his children thank all those who shared their grief at
the death of Mrs Mila Ruzelin, née Vasarin.
