**Technical note No. 76-04**

Aubrelieu, 26 January 1976

**To:** reading room; Civil Re-registration Office, Filiations Department (for the rapporteurs).
**Subject:** transfer of the collections of the upper Orvelle valley to the Central Indexed
Collection.

**1. What has been transferred.** Since 19 January, the LX-2 Reader also searches:

| Batch | Collection | Medium | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hautcorvin and Vernezac Municipal Archives (births, marriages, deaths, indexes) | microfilm 1975 | loaded |
| 1 | Press: *The Corvins Sentinel*, *The Aubrelieu Courier* (issues cited in the file) | microfilm | loaded |
| 1 | Vèze customs, Vernezac post (records 1920–1960) | originals | loaded |
| 1 | Committal registers of the Kessaar penal colony, Nos 1 to 6 | originals | loaded, **restricted** |

| 2 | Papers of Honoré Tessonnal, deposited at the Office by his notary (January 1976) | originals | pending |
| 3 | Photographs, negatives and documents handed in during the inquiry | originals | pending |

Batches 2 and 3 will be loaded as the Commission stamps the first sets of the table. The archivist
does not bring up the next boxes until the previous ones have gone back down. The computing
department can do nothing about it: the rule is the archivist’s.

**2. Indexing rules specific to these collections.**

- **Thalbère personal names**: indexed under their spelling in the Velmarchois civil register, the
  only legal form. Given names in their Thalbère form, which appear in certain sworn translations,
  are not indexed.
- **Thalbère places**: indexed under both names, Thalbère and Velmarchois, which lead to the same
  documents: Dalvica / Dalvezac; Brénica / Brenzac. There is no point in searching for both.
- **Prisoner numbers**: committal numbers are indexed without the leading zero. A prisoner number
  written with four digits in a register is requested from the Reader without the zero.
- **Register forms**: a name entered in list order (SURNAME Given name) returns the public documents
  in which it appears in that form. It never returns the restricted collections.
- **Restricted collections** (Act of 14 May 1973, Art. 9): the Reader returns a restricted document
  only on an exact given name and surname, in the usual order. Initials, nicknames, names in religion
  and prisoner numbers return what they return; they do not open the restricted collections.

**3. Warning.** The 1975 microfilms were made in a hurry. A few frames are blurred; two reels were
mounted backwards and then corrected. If a document seems to be missing, ask for it by its reference
before concluding that it does not exist.
