## Front

Print, 9 × 13 cm, on matt paper with a crinkle-cut edge, slightly yellowed. A wooden house with a
balcony, geraniums in pots; summer.

In front of the door, an old woman and a man of about forty. She is small and upright, a white
plait coiled into a crown; her eyebrows have stayed dark, straight and thick; an apron over a black
dress. He is thin, grey at the temples, his hair parted at the side; black eyebrows, straight and
thick, almost joined; deep-set dark eyes; a dimple in his left cheek. A loupe hangs from a cord on
his jacket. His left hand rests on the old woman’s shoulder, and the back of that hand is crossed
by a broad, shiny scar.

Behind the plank fence, half hidden by a pot of geraniums, a woman of about forty with thick
eyebrows, a scarf knotted over her head, is looking into the camera.
