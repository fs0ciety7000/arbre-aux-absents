*A sheet of squared notebook paper, in pencil, folded in eight; traces of soot along the folds. A
quick, heavy hand with an uneven slope, Hautcorvin school capitals: the bar of the T drawn in a
single straight stroke, detached from the stem.
Handed to the Office by Mrs Colombe Brossal at her hearing of 9 February 1976, “for the
handwriting”.*

::hand[Colo,]

::hand[I didn’t set the fire. I was at number two till midnight and the fire started in the store, at the other end of the hall. Ask Mallet what he saw. Ask him, not the lieutenant.]

::hand[Tell Papa I never left the furnace. Tell him too that I won’t come down as long as they keep saying what they’re saying.]

::hand[Don’t try to find out where I am. The bearer can be trusted. Burn this.]

::hand[B.]

---

::hand[Document received 9.2.76. Mrs Brossal states: “He told me to burn it. I didn’t burn it.” — F. O.]
