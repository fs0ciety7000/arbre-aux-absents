## Front

Colour print, 13 × 18 cm, its colours faded and drifting towards magenta. The high street of
Vernezac under snow, banners, plank stalls, smoke from roasting chestnuts.

On the left, under a banner reading *Ski School — Vernezac*, a young, tanned instructor with
sticking-out ears, a striped bobble hat, glacier goggles pushed up on his forehead, the school’s
red jacket. In the middle, in front of the Guides’ Company stall, a man with a black beard, a
woollen hat, an ice axe on his shoulder; on his jacket, a badge with the number 31. On the right, at
the Posts stall, a young woman with backcombed hair and butterfly glasses, in a uniform jacket, a
pencil in her hand, is selling illustrated envelopes.

In front of the stall, a woman of about thirty with black hair cut short, a lock lifting in a
cowlick in the middle of her forehead, pale eyes, a dimple in her left cheek; a horn whistle hangs
at her neck. Next to her, a man of nearly sixty in civilian clothes, very tall, with sticking-out
ears, leaning on a stick.

In the baker’s window, on the right, the reflection of a woman with a straight fringe holding a
reflex camera up to her face.
