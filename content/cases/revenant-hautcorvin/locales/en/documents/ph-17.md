## Negatives

A strip of two 6 × 6 cm negatives in a glassine sleeve; the Office has had two proof prints made
from them.

**Pose 1, full face** (the one used for the photograph attached to the petition). A thin man of
about sixty, his white hair cropped short. His eyebrows, still black, are straight and thick, almost
joined; his eyes are dark and deep-set; a dimple hollows his left cheek. A dark suit too wide at the
shoulders, a tie; an engraver’s loupe hangs from a cord on his lapel.

**Pose 2, right profile** (not chosen by the client). The same man. The close crop bares the ear:
the lobe does not hang, it is attached to the cheek. He had raised his left hand to his collar just
as the shutter clicked; the back of that hand is crossed by a pale scar.

## Sleeve

::hand[9.1.76 — Mr Tessonnal, for the Office. Full face: two prints, paid. Profile: not chosen.]

On the back of the sleeve, in a different ink:

::hand[He said it was the studio his mother used to bring him to as a boy. He knew the way to the darkroom. — S. Brossal]

*Registry note: negatives handed in to the Office on 17 February 1976, on requisition, by Mrs Sabine
