## Front

Grey-brown print, 13 × 18 cm, the corners dog-eared, a crease running across it. A garden under a
vine arbour, a long wooden bench, two iron chairs. Eight people.

Seated in the middle of the bench, an elderly man with a white chin-curtain beard, small round
spectacles pushed up on his forehead; beside him, a grey-haired woman, a round brooch of gilded
glass at her collar. Sitting in the grass at their feet, two children: a girl of about ten with very
pale eyes, and a boy of five in a smock, a lock of hair standing up in a cowlick in the middle of
his forehead. Both have a dimple in the left cheek.

Standing behind the bench, from left to right:

- a man of about thirty-five, short moustache, a cowlick in the middle of his forehead, a
  glassworker’s canvas cap pushed back, his huge hands clasped in front of him;
- a young woman whose long neck is set off by a velvet ribbon, with very pale grey eyes, under a
  large hat with a flat-laid feather;
- a nun in a white cornette with broad wings, steel spectacles, a dimple in her left cheek, a cross
  of blue glass on her breast;
- a man of about thirty, hair cut en brosse, thin moustache, iron-rimmed spectacles, a dimple in his
  left cheek; a round lens maker’s loupe hangs from a cord over his waistcoat.
