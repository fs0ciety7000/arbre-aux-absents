## ENGRAVED GLASSWARE OF VERNEZAC

### Catalogue for the year 1925

*All our pieces are mouth-blown in our Vernezac furnace hall and wheel-engraved in our workshop, on
lead crystal of our own making. None is moulded. No two are alike: that is their fault and their
worth. Each engraved glass bears under the foot the mark of the workshop, an ember in a circle.*

*The shaped pieces are blown by Mr H. Tessonnal, master glassmaker, and by the glassblowers of the
hall. The pieces of the “Laurier” series and orders bearing a monogram are engraved by Mrs D. Dalvarin,
engraver, head of the workshop.*

### Gilding

Gold lines and gilt monograms are executed in our gilding workshop at Hautcorvin, according to the
tradition of the firm.

### Optics

*On the back page.* The Hautcorvin Glassworks have calculated, cast and cut lighthouse lenses for the
Lights and Beacons Service since 1887. Our references: the Tennok lens, a first-order apparatus,
1889, gold medal at the Aubrelieu Exhibition. Estimates on request, at the head office.
