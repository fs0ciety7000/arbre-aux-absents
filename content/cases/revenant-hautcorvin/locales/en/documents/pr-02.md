**THE CORVINS SENTINEL** — Weekly of the upper valley — Hautcorvin — Saturday 22 April 1916 —
5 sous

## THE THALBÈRES ESCORTED BACK TO THE PASS

### The decree of 20 April applied throughout the upper valley

The decree of 20 April on Thalbère subjects in the frontier provinces has been applied without delay.
From Thursday evening, the constables went from house to house in Hautcorvin, in Vernezac and in the
hamlets, list in hand; those concerned had twenty-four hours and one bag apiece. The convoys went up
to the Col de Corvenne by road, under escort, and the barrier closed behind them.

We shall not dispute a measure which a state of war demands, and which our readers, who watch the
carts of the fort go by every day, understand better than anyone. We shall say only what we saw.
This expulsion empties our streets of people we knew by name: a cobbler from the rue Haute, two
woodcutters, a wet nurse, and the engravers whom the Hautcorvin Glassworks brought over from Brenzac
ten years ago. There were tears on the doorstep of more than one Velmarchois house.

At the barrier, on the far side, in Thalberie, families were waiting with carts. There was no
incident of any kind. An officer said to us: “That isn't war. That's administration.”
