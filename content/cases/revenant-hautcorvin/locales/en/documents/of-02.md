::stamp[CIVIL RE-REGISTRATION OFFICE — REGISTRY — RECEIVED 12 JAN. 1976 — No. 76-0012]

**REPUBLIC OF VELMARCHE — CIVIL RE-REGISTRATION OFFICE**

**Form R-4. Petition for the re-registration of a living absentee**
*(Civil Re-registration Act of 14 May 1973, Art. 4)*

*This form is intended for persons struck off the registers between 2 February 1938 and 11 April
1952, still living, who were not re-registered in 1953. It is completed by the claimant in person,
at the registry, and signed before the clerk. Identity is admitted only on two concordant documents
and after portrait identification (Art. 4, para. 3).*

---

**I. Declared identity**

Surname: ::hand[TESSONNAL]

Given names: ::hand[Bastien]

Born: ::hand[14 May 1910] at: ::hand[Hautcorvin]

Son of: ::hand[Honoré Tessonnal, master glassmaker] and of: ::hand[Eugénie Ravenel, milliner]

Occupation on the date of removal: ::hand[night foreman at the Vernezac furnace hall (Hautcorvin Glassworks)]

Last address before removal: ::hand[Vernezac, hall lodgings]

**II. Removal and detention**

Removal list (number, date): ::hand[No. 9, Bulletin of Order of 26 March 1938]

Ground entered on the list: ::hand[“criminal blaze”. I did not set the fire.]

Place of detention, prisoner number: ::hand[Kessaar, No. 0214, cell 14]

**III. Circumstances that prevented re-registration in 1953** *(state briefly)*

::hand[I was recorded as dead in error on 9 March 1944 in the Kessaar infirmary. The man who died that day was Andrin Dalvarin, my cellmate, No. 1171. I took his name and his number because I was due to be tried in April for sabotage. I have lived under his name ever since. Released on 11 April 1952 under that name, re-registered under that name at Vernezac on 3 June 1953. I did not dare become myself again while my father was alive. My father died on 19 December.]

**IV. Present address**

::hand[Dalvica (Dalvezac), Dalva valley, Thalberie, care of Mrs Jadela Rovin. In Aubrelieu: Hôtel du Grand-Cerf, rue des Chartes, room 9.]

**V. Enclosures**

::hand[One photograph (Studio Orlhac, Hautcorvin, 9 January 1976). Re-registration card in the name of Andrin Dalvarin, Vernezac, 3 June 1953.]

::hand[No document in the name of Bastien Tessonnal: I have none left.]

**VI. Witnesses the claimant asks to have heard**

::hand[None. Those who knew me are dead, or will not want to recognise me.]

**VII. Persons with an interest in the petition** *(Art. 6: heirs, beneficiaries, children)*

::hand[My sister Colombe Brossal, Hautcorvin. My daughter, Agnès Neviot, nurse, Vernezac, born 14 February 1939.]
