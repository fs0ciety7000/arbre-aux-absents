## Photographs

Two prints pasted side by side, 4 × 5 cm each, in front of a graduated height gauge. **Full face:**
a man of about thirty with black hair cut short, whose hairline across the forehead is even. Dark
eyes under straight, thick eyebrows, almost joined above the nose; a dimple in the left cheek. He
does not lower his eyes. **In profile (right):** the same man; the earlobe does not hang, it is
joined to the cheek. Both hands rest flat on the number slate; the backs of the hands are clean,
without a mark.

The slate reads, in chalk: *41-1987 — 25.9.41*.
