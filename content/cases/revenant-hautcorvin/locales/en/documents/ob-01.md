*Footed crystal cup, 31 cm high, blown in two gathers, the foot added. On the wall, wheel-engraved,
the Regent’s profile within a laurel wreath of forty-two leaves, twenty-one to a branch, the veins
picked out with the point. Under the foot, point-engraved: “Hautcorvin Glassworks, 1941”. On the
bowl, on the reverse of the profile: “To His Highness the Regent, Hautcorvin, 7 September 1941”. An
old chip on the edge of the foot.*

## Detail photographs (1941)

*Two prints stapled to the record. The first, enlarged six times, shows the left branch of the
wreath. The second, enlarged twenty times, shows the space between the seventh and eighth leaves:
between two veins, three signs less than a millimetre high, separated by dots, which the naked eye
takes for a vein of the laurel:*

**B · T · 38**

## Conservation notice (1976)

Regency exhibit, transferred to the National Archives in 1953 with the seals of the Census and
Removals Bureau. Released to the Civil Re-registration Office on 19 February 1976 for file 76-0012,
then entrusted to the Museum of the Fire Arts for expert examination. Condition: intact, apart from
the chip on the foot, which predates the seizure according to the record.
