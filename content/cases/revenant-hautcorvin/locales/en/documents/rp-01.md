::stamp[REGENCY OF VELMARCHE — GUARD OF ORDER — HAUTCORVIN LIEUTENANCY]

**REPORT No. 37-311**

**Subject:** blaze in the furnace hall of the Hautcorvin Glassworks, at Vernezac, night of 27 to 28
November 1937.

**To:** Provincial Command of the Guard of Order, Aubrelieu. Copy: Aubrelieu public prosecutor's
office.

---

**I. The facts.** On Saturday 27 November 1937, at about 23.40, a blaze broke out in the furnace hall
of the Hautcorvin Glassworks, at Vernezac. Alerted at 0.15 by the foreman Lavergne, we arrived on the
scene at 1.30 with four guards. The fire, fought by the night staff and the inhabitants, was brought
under control at about 4.00. The eastern part of the hall (stores) is destroyed, the roof fallen in;
the furnaces are spared.

**II. Findings.** According to the first witnesses heard, the fire is thought to have started on the
furnace side, where the night shift was working.

**III. Persons.**

1. The night foreman **Bastien Tessonnal**, glassblower, son of the master glassmaker, known as a ringleader of the walkout of June 1936, disappeared during the night. He was not found at his lodgings. The two apprentices of his shift state that he left the furnace “to go to the fire” and that they did not see him again.
2. Mr **Aimé Brossal**, company secretary, heard on the 28th at his home in Hautcorvin, states that he left the hall at eight o'clock in the evening and did not return. His servant confirms this.
3. The night watchman **Toussaint Mallet**, badly burnt on the hands while fighting the fire and taken to the Hautcorvin hospice, could not be heard. The watchman's log, consulted on the spot, contains nothing of use for the evening.
4. The engraver Dalvarin Andrin, who lodges at the hall, states that he saw nothing and does not know where the night foreman is.

**IV. Conclusions.** The blaze appears to be of criminal origin. The night foreman Tessonnal, whose
hostility to the management has been notorious since the walkout, is being sought. We propose that
his description be circulated to the constabulary posts and the customs, and that he be entered on
the next removal list.

Hautcorvin, Monday 29 November 1937.

::signature[R. Saunier, lieutenant, commanding the Hautcorvin Lieutenancy]
