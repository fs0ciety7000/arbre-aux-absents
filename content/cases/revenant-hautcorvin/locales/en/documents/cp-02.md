## Front

Photo postcard from before 1910, deckled edges, top corner broken off. A white-sand quarry cut into
the slope, men in shirtsleeves, shovel or pick in hand, in front of tip-carts. In the foreground, the
broadest of them, horseshoe beard and bowler hat, leans on his shovel with a left hand that is missing
two fingers. Beside him, a woman in a black bonnet with strings, iron-rimmed spectacles and a leather
bag slung across her, sits on a mule. A little apart, a clean-shaven young man, in a felt hat with a
feather, a coiled rope across his chest, looks at the camera.

Printed caption: *VERNEZAC — The Combe-aux-Sables. The quarrymen of Mr Barthélemy Neviot, supplier of
white sand to the Glassworks.*

## Back

Card unstamped, no address, slipped into a book. In thick pencil, in a large hand that presses hard:

::hand[Rémi —]

::hand[The sheep are getting across fine. Your sister has the knack. Burn this.]

::hand[Your old man, No. 7.]

Below, in blue ink, in another hand, more recent and careful:

::hand[Handwriting of Jules Neviot, my father-in-law, 1939. He didn't burn it, nor did Rémi. — O. N., 1972]
