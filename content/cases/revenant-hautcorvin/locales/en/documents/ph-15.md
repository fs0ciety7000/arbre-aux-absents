## Front

Print, 18 × 24 cm, taken outdoors: the stone steps of Hautcorvin town hall, one June morning. Six
people on the steps.

The bride and groom in the middle. He, in his thirties, already thinning on the crown, pale eyes
behind spectacles, a dimple in his left cheek, a bow tie. She, twenty-four, a straight fringe,
shoulder-length hair, rings in her ears; her smile is crooked, like someone who would rather be on
the other side of the camera.

One step higher, a young woman with pale eyes and the dimple, and a man with a short beard and
half-moon spectacles. Below, a woman of about fifty dressed all in black, grey eyes, a dimple, a
glass brooch with the [[dove|colombe]]; beside her, an old man with a white mane leaning on a glassblower’s
pipe.

Printed in the margin: *Mr Hugues Brossal, engineer at the Glassworks, and Miss Sabine Orlhac, of
Studio Orlhac.*
