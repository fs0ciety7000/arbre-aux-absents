## Front

Brown-toned print, 13 × 18 cm, spotted with damp in one corner. A low room lit by a row of
windows. Three benches each carry a small copper wheel mounted on a lathe, under a lamp with a
sheet-iron shade; jars of emery, glasses waiting on a shelf. Two men in shirtsleeves, bent over
their wheels, do not look up.

In the foreground, in three-quarter view, a young woman of about twenty-five holds a cup against
the wheel, both hands flat on the glass. Very dark eyes under straight, thick eyebrows, almost
joined; a plait coiled into a low crown; an embroidered scarf knotted at the nape. She wears the
engravers’ long leather apron, which bulges at the waist a little more than it should.

Beneath the image, in white ink on the black of the print: *Hautcorvin Glassworks — Vernezac
engraving workshop — Miss D. Dalvarin, engraver.*
