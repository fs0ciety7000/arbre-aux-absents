## Front

Print, 13 × 18 cm with a white margin, the usual size for class photographs. A classroom: a
cast-iron stove, a wall map of Velmarche, a blackboard on which someone has chalked *14 June 1947*.
Twenty-one pupils in three rows, the little ones seated at the front, the big ones standing behind.

On the right, standing, the schoolmistress: about thirty, low chignon, round spectacles, grey
overall, a stick of chalk between her fingers.

In the front row, third from the left, a girl of eight with black hair cut short, very pale eyes, a
dimple in her left cheek; a lock of hair stands up in a cowlick in the middle of her forehead. At
the end of the same row, the smallest, aged five, who would not let go of her slate. In the back
row, the tallest of the boys is looking out of the window.
