## I. Birth of Andrin Dalvarin — entry No. 31, year 1912

In the year nineteen hundred and twelve, on Monday the second of September, at four o'clock in the
afternoon, before us, mayor of the commune of Vernezac, registrar, there appeared **Annette Neviot,
née Combal**, midwife, holding a diploma, aged sixty-one, residing at Vernezac,

who presented to us a child of the male sex, born this day at six o'clock in the morning at his
mother's home, rue du Four, in this commune, to whom she declared she gave the name of **Andrin**;

son of **Danela Dalvarin**, engraver at the Hautcorvin Glassworks, aged twenty-four, a Thalbère
subject, born at Dalvica (Thalberie), residing in this commune, daughter of **Dranko Dalvarin**,
charcoal burner at Dalvica;

**father not named**.

In the presence of: Barthélemy Neviot, quarryman, sixty-five, and Germain Ardouin, innkeeper,
fifty-seven, residing in this commune, who have signed with the informant and us, the mother having
been unable to appear.

::signature[A. Neviot — B. Neviot — G. Ardouin — the Mayor of Vernezac]

*Marginal note:* “Struck off the registers (Bulletin of Order No. 85, list No. 40, 16 October 1941).
Re-registered at Vernezac on 3 June 1953, record No. 7 of the re-registrations.”

## II. Vernezac parish — register of baptisms, 1912, fol. 23

On Sunday the eighth of September nineteen hundred and twelve, we, Mathurin Pradeil, parish priest of
Vernezac, baptised **Andrin**, born on the second of this month, son of Danela Dalvarin, engraver, of
this parish, the father unknown.

The godfather was **Léonce Tessonnal**, lens maker at the Hautcorvin Glassworks; the godmother,
**Marguerite Tessonnal, in religion Sister Agathe**, Hospitaller nun at Hautcorvin; who have signed
with us.

::signature[L. Tessonnal — Marguerite Tessonnal, Sr Agathe — M. Pradeil, parish priest]
