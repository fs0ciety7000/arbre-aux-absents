## II. St Éloi's parish, Hautcorvin — register of baptisms (extracts)

*Parish register, kept by the parish priest; deposited in the municipal archives with the registers
of catholicity in 1968, microfilmed in 1975.*

**1906, fol. 31.** On Sunday the twenty-ninth of July nineteen hundred and six, we, the undersigned
parish priest, baptised **Colombe**, born on the twenty-third of this month, daughter of Honoré
Tessonnal, master glassmaker, and of Eugénie Ravenel, his wife, of this parish. Godfather: Gustave
Ravenel, hatter at Aubrelieu, uncle of the child. Godmother: Joséphine Pélissier, widow Ravenel, her
maternal grandmother.

::signature[G. Ravenel — J. Ravenel née Pélissier — H. Tessonnal — G. Lacoste, parish priest of Saint-Éloi]

**1910, fol. 22.** On Sunday the twenty-second of May nineteen hundred and ten, we, the undersigned
parish priest, baptised **Bastien**, born on the fourteenth of this month, son of Honoré Tessonnal,
master glassmaker, and of Eugénie Ravenel, his wife, of this parish.

Godfather: **Évariste Tessonnal**, director of the Glassworks, paternal grandfather of the child.

Godmother: **Clotilde Mervaux**, married name Tessonnal, paternal grandmother of the child.

::signature[É. Tessonnal — C. Tessonnal née Mervaux — H. Tessonnal — G. Lacoste, parish priest of Saint-Éloi]

*In the margin, in the priest's hand:* “The child screamed all through the rite. The grandfather
says it is a good sign for a glassblower.”
