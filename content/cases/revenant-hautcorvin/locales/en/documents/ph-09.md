## Photographs

Two prints pasted side by side, 4 × 5 cm each, in front of a graduated height gauge. **Full face:**
a man of about thirty, his head freshly shaved; at the roots of the hair, the clipping reveals a
whorl in the middle of the forehead. The eyes are very pale, almost white on the print; a dimple in
the left cheek, a graze on the cheekbone. The parted lips show a chipped front tooth. **In profile
(right):** the same man; the ear, laid bare by the clipping, has a free lobe, hanging loose below
the point where it joins the head. In the foreground, his right hand holds the number slate: on the
back of the hand, a broad, shiny burn scar.

The slate reads, in chalk: *38-0377 — 24.5.38*.

## Decision

Struck off, absconded, recaptured. Transfer: Kessaar penal colony, by the first convoy.
