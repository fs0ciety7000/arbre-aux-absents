*Two documents stapled together. The original: a sheet of blue paper written in Thalbère, violet
ink, a slanting hand with looped Ts; envelope “Mr Honoré Tessonnal, master glassmaker, Hautcorvin
(Velmarche)”, Brénica postmark, July 1953. The sworn translation, typed, made at Brénica in
February 1976 at the Office’s request.*

## The original (first line)

“*Onoré, nosarin dorvel ad ila. Ne sanevi. Ja ne loram : sela tinin.*”

## Sworn translation

::stamp[BRÉNICA COURT — SWORN TRANSLATOR — THALBÈRE / VELMARCHOIS]

I the undersigned, Tarko Senavin, sworn translator to the Brénica court, certify that what follows is
a faithful and complete translation of the letter written in Thalbère that was presented to me,
signed “Danéla”, sent from Dalvica to Mr Honoré Tessonnal in the month of July 1953.

*Spelling: the author signs in the Thalbère way, Danéla; the Velmarchois civil register writes
**Danela Dalvarin**.*

> Dalvica, 9 July
>
> Honoré,
>
> Our son has come back from the island. You have known since the telegram; I am writing it to you
> because he took back his name on 3 June, at Vernezac, before the mayor, and now it is written
> down somewhere.
>
> When he came over the pass last year, on foot, with a canvas bag and nothing in it, he was not yet
> forty and you would have said sixty. He slept two days and two nights; on the third day, he asked
> for a wheel. His left hand is burnt, from over there, from the lime kiln. He engraves all the same.
> The Brénica Glassworks are taking him on at St Martin’s.
>
> He talks about your Bastien at night, in his sleep, as if he were in the room. By day he does not
> speak of him. He went to tell the girl at the refuge, three weeks ago; he came back without a word.
>
> He does not know. He still believes what he was made to believe, that his father is your brother,
> who died at the pass. I will not tell him: that is for you. I carried him, I fed him, I took him to
> the border twice; it is not for me to give him a father at forty.
>
> You blew a cup for him, once, and I engraved it. Make something with your hands again.
>
> D.

*Translator’s note.* “Our son” renders *nosarin*, literally “the one of our house”; the word is used
only of a child, never of a parent or a servant. “The island” renders *ila*, with no name: the
author does not name Kessaar.

Brénica, 6 February 1976. Fees paid by the Office.

::signature[T. Senavin, sworn translator]
