## COMMUNE OF VERNEZAC — REGISTER OF BIRTH CERTIFICATES

### Extracts for the years 1847 to 1914 (Neviot family)

*Small stitched registers, laid paper, several clerks' hands; microfilmed in 1975. The marginal
notes are reproduced in the last column.*

| Year | No. | Child | Born | Father | Mother | Marginal notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1847 | 9 | Barthélemy NEVIOT, male | 9 March 1847, at the hamlet of Les Scies | Mathieu Neviot, woodcutter | Rose Fabre, his wife | Married at Vernezac on 12 May 1874 to Annette Combal. |
| 1851 | 22 | Annette COMBAL, female | 3 July 1851, in the village | Pierre Combal, clog maker | Marie Teyssier, his wife | Married at Vernezac on 12 May 1874 to Barthélemy Neviot. |
| 1877 | 4 | Jules NEVIOT, male | 14 January 1877, in the village | Barthélemy Neviot, quarryman at the Combe-aux-Sables | Annette Combal, midwife, his wife | Married at Vernezac on 6 May 1905 to Philomène Ardouin. |
| 1880 | 6 | Philomène ARDOUIN, female | 22 January 1880, at the Auberge des Guides | Germain Ardouin, innkeeper | Céleste Mazet, his wife | Married at Vernezac on 6 May 1905 to Jules Neviot. |
| 1908 | 17 | Rémi NEVIOT, male | 17 March 1908, at the Auberge des Guides | Jules Neviot, guide | Philomène Ardouin, innkeeper, his wife | Married at Vernezac on 15 September 1934 to Odette Lamberge. |
| 1914 | 25 | Lucie NEVIOT, female | 8 April 1914, at the Auberge des Guides | the same | the same | Married at Vernezac on 12 April 1947 (see the register of marriages). |

Jules is registered as “son of Barthélemy Neviot, quarryman, and of Annette Combal, midwife, his
wife”, the mother having filled in the notice of birth in her own hand. Rémi and Lucie are registered
as “children of Jules Neviot, guide, and of Philomène Ardouin, innkeeper at the Auberge des Guides,
his wife”.

Certified true extracts.
