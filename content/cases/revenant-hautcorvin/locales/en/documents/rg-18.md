::stamp[HAUTCORVIN GLASSWORKS — VERNEZAC HALL — NIGHT WATCHMAN'S LOG]

## Rounds register, November 1937

*Board-covered exercise book with numbered leaves, one leaf per night and per watch, pencil. The
handwriting of Toussaint Mallet, night watchman, careful and slow.*

### Leaf 56 — Saturday 27 November, 18.00 to 22.00

| Time | Round | Remarks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18.00 | coming on duty | T. Mallet. |
| 18.30 | gate | Day shift going out. |
| 20.05 | hall office | Mr Brossal leaves the office, by the gate, in his motor car. |
| 20.30 | furnaces | Furnace one banked. At number two, the night foreman and two lads, gathering for bottles. |
| 21.30 | packing store | Nothing. Doors locked. |

### Leaf 57 — torn out

*Only the stub remains, torn close to the stitching. From the heading of the following leaf, it
covered the watch from 22.00 to midnight.*

### Leaf 58 — Sunday 28 November, 0.00 to 4.00

*Leaf almost blank. In raking light, the lines written in pencil on the torn-out leaf, which lay on
top of it, can be read as indentations:*

> 22.00 — round of the furnaces, nothing.
>
> 22.40 — number two is working, the night foreman is there.
>
> 23.10 — M. B. in the packing store. Says he is looking for a file.
>
> 23.3… — (illegible)

*The only writing visible on the leaf, at the bottom, in the same hand, very shaky:*

::hand[28 Nov., 6.00. Hands burnt. Fire in the store, not the furnaces.]
