## COMMUNE OF HAUTCORVIN — REGISTER OF BIRTH CERTIFICATES

### Extracts for the years 1848, 1856, 1880, 1883 and 1885

*Registers bound in sheepskin, a town clerk's hand, faded brown ink; microfilmed in 1975. The
entries have been copied out one after another, each from its own year's register; the marginal
notes are reproduced in the last column.*

| Year | No. | Child | Born | Father | Mother | Marginal notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1848 | 51 | Évariste TESSONNAL, male | 11 April 1848, seven o'clock in the morning, rue Haute | Joseph-Marie Tessonnal, thirty-eight, glassworks master | Hortense Lavalade, his wife | Married at Hautcorvin on 23 April 1878 to Clotilde Mervaux. Died 7 January 1921. |
| 1856 | 19 | Clotilde MERVAUX, female | 2 February 1856, noon, rue des Doreurs | Prosper Mervaux, twenty-nine, glass gilder at the Glassworks | Louise Bardin, his wife | Married at Hautcorvin on 23 April 1878 to Évariste Tessonnal. Died 30 March 1930. |
| 1880 | 112 | Honoré TESSONNAL, male | 15 August 1880, four o'clock in the afternoon, rue Haute | Évariste Tessonnal, thirty-two, director of the Hautcorvin Glassworks | Clotilde Mervaux, twenty-four, gilder, his wife | Married 12 May 1904 to Eugénie Ravenel. Died 19 December 1975. |
| 1883 | 37 | Marguerite TESSONNAL, female | 6 March 1883, five o'clock in the morning, rue Haute | the same, thirty-four | the same, twenty-seven | Died 2 November 1964. |
| 1885 | 71 | Léonce TESSONNAL, male | 20 June 1885, eleven o'clock at night, rue Haute | the same, thirty-seven | the same, twenty-nine | Died for Velmarche on 2 October 1916 (entered in the register on 30 October 1916). |

Honoré, Marguerite and Léonce are all three registered as “children of Évariste Tessonnal, director
of the Glassworks, and of Clotilde Mervaux, gilder, his wife”, the father present and signing each
time. In 1885 the registrar added after the father's signature: *“The informant asked that Léonce be
written with the accent.”*

Certified true extracts from the registers.
