*Small notebook with a black oilcloth cover, 9 × 14 cm, in pencil: tally marks, dates, a few words.
Lucie Neviot's handwriting. The registry has listed it under the name Mr Sarrade gives it, the “sheep
notebook”.*

**On the flyleaf:** “B. — 14.2.39” and, below, a small cowlick of hair drawn in a single stroke.

## 1938

- 17.9 — 2 sheep. Papa in front, me behind. Snow at the Pas.
- 30.9 — 3. An old man who wouldn't let go of his suitcase.
- 12.11 — 2. Done before the heavy snow.

## 1939

- January — I don't go up any more. Papa alone.
- June — Going up again. 4.
- 9.7 — six sheep for Dalvica. A family. The little girl was crying, Papa carried her as far as the boundary stone.

## 1944

- 12.2 — Papa. The guards fired. The six got through.
- 28.3 — 4. V. was at the cross. V. looked the other way.

## 1947

- 12.4 — Nothing this month. Married.

## 1952

- 11.4 — They're opening the island. B.?
- 20.4 — 1. The last. A hundred and forty-two. We're closing.
