## I. Hall staff register (extracts)

*Folio register, one line per worker, kept by the hall office; entries added in ink by several hands
over the years. The “Occupation” column gives the trade for which the worker is paid; the “Posts”
column gives grades and duties.*

| Works No. | Surname and given names | Joined | Occupation | Posts, bonuses and notes | Left |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 87 | DALVARIN Danela | 1906 | glass engraver (trained at Brénica) | Head of the engraving workshop from May 1921. | Escorted back to the pass 21.4.16; reinstated May 1921; expelled 8.7.38 (decree on aliens). |
| 341 | TESSONNAL Bastien | 1926 | apprentice glassblower (1926); glassblower, full grade (1929) | Night foreman of the hall from 1935 (grade, night bonus). 1st prize for blowing, St Éloi competition 1935. | Struck from the roll 28.11.37. |
| 356 | DALVARIN Andrin | 1926 | apprentice engraver (1926); glass engraver (1931) | 1st prize for engraving, St Éloi competition 1935. | Struck from the roll 25.9.41. |

## II. Engraving workshop pay book (extracts)

*Head of workshop: Mrs D. Dalvarin (from 1921). One line per payment; the description column is in
the cashier's hand.*

| Month | Payee | Description | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| September 1912 | D. D. | monthly supplement, on Mr Honoré's orders | 40 fl. |
| May 1916 | D. D. | monthly supplement, on Mr Honoré's orders; held in reserve, the payee being at Dalvica, beyond the pass | 40 fl. |
| May 1921 | D. D. | monthly supplement, on Mr Honoré's orders | 55 fl. |
| January 1930 | D. D. | monthly supplement, on Mr Honoré's orders | 75 fl. |
| December 1937 | D. D. | monthly supplement, on Mr Honoré's orders | 90 fl. |
| June 1938 | D. D. | monthly supplement, on Mr Honoré's orders; final payment | 90 order fl. |
| July 1938 | — | ::hand[Stopped. Payee expelled. — A. B.] | — |

*Microfilming note (1975):* the supplement is entered without a break from September 1912 to June
1938, including from 1916 to 1921, while the payee was away.
