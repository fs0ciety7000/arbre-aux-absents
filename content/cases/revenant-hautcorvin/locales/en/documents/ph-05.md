## Front

Small matt print, 9 × 12 cm, deckled edges, very contrasty. A sloping clearing in a fir forest; in
the background, a charcoal burner’s stack, round as an oven, smoking from two vents, and a log cabin
roofed with bark.

In front of the stack, three people. A man of about sixty in a felt cap with a rolled brim, a
drooping moustache, straight, thick eyebrows that almost meet above the nose; the soot stays in his
wrinkles like a pen drawing. He holds a charcoal burner’s long pole. A young woman, her plait coiled
into a low crown, an embroidered scarf knotted at the nape, has the same straight, dark eyebrows as
he does. She keeps her hand on the shoulder of a child of four in short trousers and clogs, who is
already frowning, under a straight fringe, with eyebrows too big for him.

At the foot of the print, a line in ink, in Thalbère.

## Back

In pencil, in a notary’s hand:

::hand[“Dalvica, 1916 — Dranko, Danela, Andrin.” (Translation of the caption on the front, which is in Thalbère. — J. E.)]
