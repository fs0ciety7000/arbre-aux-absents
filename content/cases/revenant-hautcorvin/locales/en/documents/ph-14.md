## Front

Print, 18 × 24 cm, taken in the studio in front of a painted backcloth: a balustrade, an Italian
garden. Nine people.

In the centre, the bride, twenty-four, in a high-necked dress, very pale eyes, a dimple in her left
cheek. The groom, about thirty: a short beard, half-moon spectacles, a three-piece suit, a signet
ring on his little finger. On his left, a man of about sixty with the same strong nose as his, a
white moustache, a wing collar, a watch chain.

To the bride’s right, a woman of about fifty, grey eyes, a dimple, a glass brooch engraved with a
[[dove|colombe]]; near her, a man of about sixty with a greying pencil moustache, slicked hair, his right
eyebrow raised. In the second row, a young man of about twenty-seven, already thinning on the crown,
spectacles, bow tie, a dimple in his left cheek; and an old man with a white mane and bushy
eyebrows, leaning on a glassblower’s long pipe. Two bridesmaids at the couple’s feet.

## Back

::stamp[STUDIO ORLHAC — HAUTCORVIN — NEGATIVE No. 7720]

Pasted on the back, a cutting from *The Corvins Sentinel* of 28 June 1958:

> **Announcements.** — On Saturday 21 June, at Hautcorvin, the marriage took place of our colleague
> Miss Isaure Brossal, journalist on our valley pages, daughter of Mr Aimé Brossal, company
> secretary of the Glassworks, and of Mrs Brossal, née Tessonnal, to Mr Julien Escande, notary’s
> clerk, son of Maître Léon Escande, notary at Hautcorvin. The staff of *The Sentinel* offer the
> young couple their warmest good wishes. (Photograph: Studio Orlhac.)
