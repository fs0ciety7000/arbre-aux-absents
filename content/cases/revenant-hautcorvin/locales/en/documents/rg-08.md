## Register of birth certificates — year 1939, entry No. 6: Agnès Neviot

In the year nineteen hundred and thirty-nine, on Tuesday the fourteenth of February, at eleven
o'clock in the morning, before us, mayor of the commune of Vernezac, registrar, there appeared
**Jules Neviot**, guide, aged sixty-two, residing at Vernezac, maternal grandfather of the child,

who presented to us a child of the female sex, born this day at five o'clock in the morning, at the
Auberge des Guides, in this commune, to whom he declared he gave the name of **Agnès**;

daughter of **Lucie Neviot**, aged twenty-four, keeper of the refuge de l'Ourse, residing at the said
refuge and, in winter, at Vernezac;

**father not named**.

In the presence of: Rémi Neviot, guide, thirty, uncle of the child, and Joseph Teyssier, sexton,
sixty, residing in this commune, who have signed with the informant and us.

::signature[J. Neviot — R. Neviot — J. Teyssier — the Mayor of Vernezac]

*Endorsement:* “Seen and entered in the provincial index of births, in accordance with Article 3 of
the Act of 2 February 1938. The Delegate of the Orvelle Registers Bureau.”

---

*Marginal note (1947):* “On 12 April 1947, at his marriage to the mother, Victorin Sarrade, customs
officer, declared that he did not acknowledge the child, the mother opposing any acknowledgement.”
