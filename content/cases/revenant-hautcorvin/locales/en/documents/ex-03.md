## Method

We compare the right ear, the one part of the face that age does not deform: it lengthens a little,
it does not change its pattern. Enlargements to the same scale (height of the ear brought to 10 cm).
Plate attached: three ears side by side.

## Findings

| Feature | I (1938) | II (1941) | III (1976) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lobe | **free**, rounded, clear groove below the attachment | **attached**, merging into the cheek | **attached**, merging into the cheek |
| Antihelix | single branch, arched | **forked in a Y**, upper branch thick | **forked in a Y**, upper branch thick |
| Rim (helix) | evenly rolled | small notch in the upper third | **same notch**, at the same point |
| Tragus | prominent, pointed | low, rounded | low, rounded |
| Relative height | medium | tall | tall (lengthened with age) |

## Conclusion

The claimant's ear (attached lobe, Y-shaped antihelix) is that of card 41-1987. It is not that of
card 38-0377 (free lobe).

Three concordant and independent features between II and III, one of them an accidental
peculiarity (the notch in the helix); three discordant features between I and III. Under the rules of
the service, identity is established between II and III; it is excluded between I and III.
