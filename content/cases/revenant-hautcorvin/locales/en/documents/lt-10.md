*A sheet of squared paper, ink, the enormous, shaky writing of someone who has trouble holding a
pen, several blots; envelope “Mrs Colombe Brossal, director, at the Glassworks, Hautcorvin —
personal”. Enclosed by Mrs Brossal in her envelope of 1958 (Escande office).*

::hand[Vernezac, 18 May 1952]

::hand[Madam,]

::hand[I am writing to you because now we can. I write on my own, it takes time, my hands are still the way you know since the fire. Excuse the writing.]

::hand[On the evening of 27 November 1937, at eleven and a bit, I saw Mr Brossal in the packing store. He had a lamp. He told me he was looking for a file. I put it down in my log the way I put everything, the time and the name, and I carried on with my round. At half past, your brother was at number two with his lads, blowpipe in hand, he shouted at me not to fall asleep. Ten minutes after, the fire was in the store, not at the furnaces.]

::hand[The next day, the page wasn’t there any more.]

::hand[The lieutenant told me to keep my mouth shut or go and join your brother. I was sixty and my hands were in bandages. I kept my mouth shut. I ask your pardon for your brother. I heard from people that he died out there.]

::hand[I am speaking now because I can. I won’t tell anyone but you, because it’s your firm and your husband, and it’s for you to decide.]

::hand[Toussaint Mallet]

::hand[former night watchman at the hall]
