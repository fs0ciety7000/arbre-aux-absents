**THE CORVINS SENTINEL** — Weekly of the upper valley — Hautcorvin — Saturday 20 December 1975
— 0.40 Fn

## HONORÉ TESSONNAL, THE LAST MASTER

### The master glassmaker of the Hautcorvin Glassworks died yesterday, in his ninety-sixth year

There was still, last winter, a man in Hautcorvin who could tell, from the colour of a gather alone,
whether the furnace had been properly tended in the night. That man died yesterday morning, Friday,
in the house in the rue Haute where he was born. **Honoré Tessonnal** was ninety-five. He was the last
**master glassmaker** in the valley to have learnt the trade at the blowpipe, before the machines.

The son of Évariste Tessonnal, who made Hautcorvin crystal known far beyond our passes, he had entered
the hall at fourteen as a boy, carrying the blowpipes. He kept from it his way of speaking to the
young workers: few words, and the movement shown twice. When his father died, in 1921, the board
offered him the management of the firm. He refused it. “I am a glassmaker,” he said at the time, “not
a bookkeeper.” He stayed in the hall, chaired the board and left the running of the business to
others. Until he was eighty he could be seen of a morning walking between the Vernezac furnaces, hands
behind his back, saying nothing when all was well.

He was eight in 1889, when his father's glass won its medal at the exhibition; he used to tell how he
had been allowed that day to hold the blowpipe for one minute, and had not wanted to give it back. The
valley will remember him so. It will remember too that he never spoke of the blaze of 1937, nor of
what the war cost his house.

He leaves his daughter, **Mrs Colombe Brossal**, director of the Glassworks; his grandchildren,
**Mr Hugues Brossal**, engineer at the Glassworks, and Mrs Isaure Escande, our contributor, who
writes these lines; his son **Bastien Tessonnal**, absent since 1938, of whom he would never allow it to be said in
his presence that he was dead. Only this autumn he was heard asking at the door whether Bastien had
come home. He had been a widower since 1931.

The funeral will be held on Tuesday 23 December, at ten o'clock, in the parish church of Hautcorvin.
The family asks that no flowers be sent. The Glassworks will close the Vernezac furnaces for the
duration of the service.

I. B.

### Our photograph

Last night, in front of the Tessonnal house in the rue Haute, where the valley came to pay its
respects to the master. In the foreground, on the doorstep, Mrs Colombe Brossal, in a black coat
fastened with a brooch of glass engraved with a [[dove|colombe]], white hair cut in a bob,
heavy-framed spectacles; on her right, Mr Hugues Brossal; on her left, Mrs Isaure Escande. Behind them,
the old hands of the hall, bareheaded in the snow. In the background, near the street lamp, a young
woman has stayed alone, apart, seen from behind, a scarf over her hair; no one named her.
