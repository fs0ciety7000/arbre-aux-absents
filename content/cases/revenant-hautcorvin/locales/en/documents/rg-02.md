## REGISTER OF MARRIAGE CERTIFICATES — YEAR 1878

### Entry No. 14

In the year eighteen hundred and seventy-eight, on Tuesday the twenty-third of April, at ten o'clock
in the morning, before us, mayor of the commune of Hautcorvin, registrar, at the Town House, there
appeared publicly:

**Évariste Tessonnal**, director of the Hautcorvin Glassworks, aged thirty, born in this commune on
the eleventh of April eighteen hundred and forty-eight, residing in the rue Haute, son of full age of
the late Joseph-Marie Tessonnal, glassworks master, who died in this commune on the ninth of June
eighteen hundred and seventy-four, and of Hortense Lavalade, his widow, of independent means, here
present and consenting;

and **Clotilde Mervaux**, glass gilder, aged twenty-two, born in this commune on the second of
February eighteen hundred and fifty-six, residing in the rue des Doreurs, daughter of full age of
Prosper Mervaux, glass gilder, and of Louise Bardin, his wife, here present and consenting;

who have required us to proceed to the celebration of the marriage intended between them, the banns
of which were published in this commune on Sundays the seventh and fourteenth of April, without
objection.

The future spouses have declared that a marriage contract was drawn up on the twentieth of April
before Maître Hilaire Escande, notary at Hautcorvin.

After reading out the documents and the chapter of the Civil Code on the rights and duties of
spouses, each of them having declared their wish to take the other as husband and as wife, we have
pronounced, in the name of the law, that they are united in marriage.

In the presence of: Anatole Mervaux, gilder, uncle of the bride; Casimir Roux, pharmacist; Eustache
Garrigou, foreman at the Glassworks; Gustave Lafon, schoolmaster; all of full age and domiciled in
this commune, who have signed with the spouses, the parents and us, after reading.

::signature[É. Tessonnal — C. Mervaux — H. Lavalade, widow Tessonnal — P. Mervaux — L. Bardin]

::signature[A. Mervaux — C. Roux — E. Garrigou — G. Lafon — the Mayor]

*Marginal note:* “Marriage dissolved by the death of the husband, which occurred at Hautcorvin on
7 January 1921.”
