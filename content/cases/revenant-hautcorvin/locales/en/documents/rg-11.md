## BULLETIN OF ORDER

### Official Journal of the Regency of Velmarche — Saturday 26 March 1938 — No. 13

*Page printed in two columns on the Bulletin's grey paper, close-set type; p. 88. Price of this
issue: twenty order sous.*

**CENSUS AND REMOVALS BUREAU — REMOVAL LIST No. 9**

Having regard to the Act of 2 February 1938 on the purity of the registers; having regard to the
report of the Hautcorvin Guard of Order of 29 November 1937; on the report of the Census and Removals
Bureau,

**Article One.** — The individuals named below are struck off the civil registers and **declared
absent**. Those for whom a destination is given shall be transferred there without delay.

| No. | Surname and given names | Born, at | Occupation | Grounds | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | AUZOLLE Clément | 3 February 1894, Aubrelieu | printer | printing seditious sheets | Kessaar |
| 2 | CASTANIER Marthe | 18 November 1901, Saint-Ormel | schoolmistress | remarks against the Regent | — |
| 3 | LABORIE Étienne | 7 June 1886, Port-Sénane | docker | refusal of the service of order | Kessaar |
| 4 | MAZERAN Paul | 2 April 1908, Hautcorvin | post office clerk | diversion of correspondence of order | Kessaar |
| 5 | TESSONNAL Bastien | 14 May 1910, Hautcorvin | glassblower | criminal blaze at the Hautcorvin Glassworks | fugitive — to be sought |

**Article 2.** — The man Tessonnal, a fugitive, is notified to the Guard of Order and to the customs
posts of the frontier provinces. Whosoever gives him shelter or helps him across the frontier falls
under Article 11 of the Act.

**Article 3.** — The families of the absent are required to surrender their identity papers to the
Census and Removals Bureau within eight days.

::signature[For the Regent and by delegation, the Head of the Census and Removals Bureau]
