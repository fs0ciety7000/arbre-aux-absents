## RECORD OF RE-REGISTRATION OF A STRUCK-OFF PERSON, LIVING AND PRESENT

### No. 7 — Vernezac, Wednesday 3 June 1953

*Printed form of the Provisional Government's order on the re-registration of struck-off persons
living and present, filled in on the typewriter by the town clerk. Identity photograph stapled at the
top right: a man of about forty, thin, grey at the temples. Handwritten statement and signature of
the person appearing, in ink; the statement begins with a capital T whose crossbar closes in a loop
to the left.*

Before us, mayor of the commune of Vernezac, registrar, assisted by the town clerk, there appeared in
person a man who requested his re-registration in the civil registers under the name of **Andrin
Dalvarin**, and declared:

| Heading | Statement |
| --- | --- |
| Surname | DALVARIN |
| Given name | Andrin |
| Born, at | 2 September 1912, at Vernezac (entry No. 31) |
| Parentage | son of Danela Dalvarin, engraver; father not named |
| Occupation | glass engraver |
| Removal | Bulletin of Order No. 85, list No. 40, 16 October 1941 |
| Detention | Kessaar penal colony, No. 1171, from 18 October 1941 to 11 April 1952 |
| Residence | Dalvica (Thalberie), at his mother's |

**Description** (taken by the clerk): height 1.72 m; hair black, greying; eyebrows black, straight,
thick; eyes dark brown; dimple in the left cheek; burn scar on the back of the left hand.

**Witnesses.** There appeared with him: Ernest Lavergne, former foreman at the Hautcorvin Glassworks,
sixty-six, and Marius Bonnafous, glassblower, forty-two, both residing at Vernezac, who declared that
they recognised him “as Andrin Dalvarin, the son of the woman engraver, whom they saw working in the
hall until 1941”.

Having seen the birth certificate and the removal list, no objection having been formed, we
pronounced the re-registration. A note to this effect shall be made in the margin of the birth
certificate. A re-registration card (No. 7) has been handed to the person appearing.

*Statement in the hand of the person appearing:* ::hand[The whole of the foregoing is true.]

::signature[Andrin Dalvarin]

::signature[E. Lavergne — M. Bonnafous — the Mayor of Vernezac]
