## Record of opening (1976)

::stamp[OFFICE OF MAÎTRE JULIEN ESCANDE, NOTARY AT HAUTCORVIN]

In the year nineteen hundred and seventy-six, on Thursday the twelfth of February, at the office,
we, Maître Julien Escande, notary at Hautcorvin, successor to Maître Léon Escande, upon the
requisition of the Civil Re-registration Office dated 10 February (file 76-0012), in the presence of
Mr Fernand Ollier, investigating clerk, opened the **sealed envelope** deposited at the office on
14 October 1958 by Mrs Colombe Brossal, née Tessonnal, bearing the superscription: “To be opened only
if my brother is accused again.”

The three seals of red wax, stamped with a dove for Colombe, were found intact. Mrs Brossal, having been
notified, did not appear; she let it be known that she “objects to nothing”.

The envelope contained the following documents, which we have numbered:

1. A letter from Mrs Brossal to Maître Léon Escande, dated 14 October 1958 (below).
2. Rider No. 3 to L’Égide blaze policy No. 18-447, dated 6 October 1937, raising the sum insured on the Vernezac hall from 1,200,000 to 3,500,000 florins, signed “A. Brossal, company secretary”.
3. Two issue slips from the general store, October 1937: “paraffin, two twenty-litre cans, for the packing store”, initialled “A. B.”.
4. A receipt from the Vèze Garage, Vernezac: “petrol and paraffin, paid cash, 26 November 1937, Mr Brossal”.
5. A letter from Mr Toussaint Mallet to Mrs Brossal, dated May 1952, filed separately in the Office’s file.

Duly recorded, read over and signed.

::signature[J. Escande, notary — F. Ollier, investigating clerk]

## Mrs Brossal’s letter (1958)

*Pearl-grey writing paper, no letterhead. Black ink, an even hand, not one crossing-out.*

Hautcorvin, 14 October 1958

Maître,

I am handing you this closed envelope. I ask you to keep it in your safe, to open it only if my
brother is accused again, and, if he is no longer accused, to burn it unread at my death.

In it you will find what I found in the safe of my husband, Mr Aimé Brossal, the day after his
funeral, beneath the title deeds of the firm, together with a letter I received six years ago and
did not answer.

I accuse no one. My husband is dead, and he was a good father to my children. I do not want my
children to read this. But my brother died at Kessaar branded a fire-raiser, and if anyone, one day,
lays it at his door again, I want it known that I knew.

I beg you not to reply, and never to speak to me of it.

::signature[Colombe Brossal, née Tessonnal]
