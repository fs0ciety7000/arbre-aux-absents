## I. Register of marriage certificates — year 1928, entry No. 41

In the year nineteen hundred and twenty-eight, on Saturday the sixth of October, at eleven o'clock in
the morning, before us, mayor of the commune of Hautcorvin, registrar, at the Town House, there
appeared publicly:

**Aimé Brossal**, company secretary of the Hautcorvin Glassworks, aged twenty-nine, born at Aubrelieu
on the seventeenth of February eighteen hundred and ninety-nine, residing at Hautcorvin, place du
Marché, son of full age of Edmond Brossal, wine merchant, and of Berthe Lagarde, his wife, residing at
Aubrelieu, here present and consenting;

and **Colombe Tessonnal**, of no occupation, aged twenty-two, born in this commune on the
twenty-third of July nineteen hundred and six, residing in the rue Haute, daughter of full age of
Honoré Tessonnal, master glassmaker, and of Eugénie Ravenel, milliner, his wife, here present and
consenting;

who, the banns having been published at Hautcorvin and at Aubrelieu without objection, have required
us to proceed to the celebration of their marriage. Each of them having declared their wish to take
the other as husband and as wife, we have pronounced, in the name of the law, that they are united in
marriage.

In the presence of: Raoul Brossal, barrister at Aubrelieu, brother of the groom; Gustave Ravenel,
hatter at Aubrelieu, uncle of the bride; who have signed with the spouses, the parents and us, after
reading.

::signature[A. Brossal — C. Tessonnal — E. Brossal — H. Tessonnal — E. Tessonnal née Ravenel]

::signature[R. Brossal — G. Ravenel — the Mayor]

*Marginal note:* “Marriage dissolved by the death of the husband, which occurred at Hautcorvin on
3 September 1958.”
