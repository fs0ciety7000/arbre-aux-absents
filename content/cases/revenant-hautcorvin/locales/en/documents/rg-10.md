## I. Register of marriage certificates — year 1947, entry No. 4

In the year nineteen hundred and forty-seven, on Saturday the twelfth of April, at eleven o'clock in
the morning, before us, mayor of the commune of Vernezac, registrar, at the Town House, there
appeared publicly:

**Victorin Sarrade**, customs officer, posted to the Vèze post, aged thirty-two, born at Hautcorvin
on the fourteenth of January nineteen hundred and fifteen, residing at the customs barracks of
Vernezac, son of full age of Auguste Sarrade, roadman, deceased, and of Marthe Bonnet, his widow,
residing at Hautcorvin, consenting;

and **Lucie Neviot**, keeper of the refuge de l'Ourse, aged thirty-three, born in this commune on the
eighth of April nineteen hundred and fourteen, daughter of full age of the late Jules Neviot, guide,
and of Philomène Ardouin, innkeeper, his widow, here present and consenting;

who have required us to proceed to the celebration of their marriage, and have been united in the
name of the law.

**Declaration of the husband.** Called upon in accordance with the law concerning the child born to
the wife before the marriage, Agnès, born at Vernezac on the fourteenth of February nineteen hundred
and thirty-nine, the husband declared that he did not acknowledge her, the wife opposing any
acknowledgement. A note to this effect has been made in the margin of the child's birth certificate.

In the presence of: **Rémi Neviot**, guide, brother of the bride; **Achille Fauvel**, Sergeant at the
Vèze customs; who have signed with the spouses, the mother of the bride and us, after reading.

::signature[V. Sarrade — L. Neviot — Ph. Neviot née Ardouin — R. Neviot — A. Fauvel — the Mayor]

*Endorsement:* “Seen. The Delegate of the Orvelle Registers Bureau.”

## II. Register of birth certificates — year 1948, entry No. 52

On Friday the third of December nineteen hundred and forty-eight, at eight o'clock in the evening,
there was born at Vernezac **Pierre Sarrade**, son of Victorin Sarrade, customs officer, and of Lucie
Neviot, refuge keeper, his wife. Informant: the father, who signed with us.

::signature[V. Sarrade — the Mayor of Vernezac]
