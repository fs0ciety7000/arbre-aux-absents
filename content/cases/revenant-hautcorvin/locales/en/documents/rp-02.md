## CLAIMS DEPARTMENT — ASSESSOR'S REPORT

| Heading | Particulars |
| --- | --- |
| Loss | No. 37-1142, night of 27 to 28 November 1937 |
| Insured | Hautcorvin Glassworks (head office at Hautcorvin) |
| Risk | Vernezac furnace hall, buildings and stock |
| Policy | No. 18-447; rider No. 3 of 6 October 1937 |
| Assessor | Léopold Mazeline, the Company's approved assessor |

**1. Instructions.** To determine the origin and circumstances of the loss, to assess the damage,
and to state whether the cover applies.

**2. Findings** (visits of 3, 4 and 9 December 1937).

The fire started in the **packing store**, at the eastern end of the hall, where the straw, wood wool
and crates are kept. This store lies forty metres from the furnaces, from which it is separated by
the firebreak wall of 1902, which is still standing. Furnaces No. 1 and No. 2 are intact; the deepest
burning is on the floor of the store, over an area of six metres by four, with greasy rings and a
smell of paraffin still noticeable on the 9th. A twenty-litre can, distorted by the heat, was found
beneath the debris. The store has had electric lighting since 1925; no paraffin is used there.

**3. Cover.** The sum insured on the hall, 1,200,000 florins since 1931, was raised to
**3,500,000 florins** by rider No. 3 of 6 October 1937, that is seven weeks before the loss, signed
on behalf of the insured by Mr A. Brossal, company secretary. I estimate the value of the hall and
stock at 1,400,000 florins.

**4. Witnesses.** The night watchman Mallet, seen on 9 December at the Hautcorvin hospice, his hands
bandaged, would make no statement in the presence of the guard attending him.

**5. Conclusions.** The origin of the loss is not accidental. Nothing connects the seat of the fire
with the furnace station. The amount of the rider, in relation to the value of the risk, calls for
explanations from the insured. I propose that settlement be suspended and that a judicial inquiry be
requested.

Aubrelieu, 14 December 1937.

::signature[L. Mazeline, approved assessor]
