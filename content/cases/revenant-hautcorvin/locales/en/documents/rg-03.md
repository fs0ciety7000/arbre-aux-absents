## REGISTER OF MARRIAGE CERTIFICATES — YEAR 1904

### Entry No. 22

In the year nineteen hundred and four, on Thursday the twelfth of May, at eleven o'clock in the
morning, before us, mayor of the commune of Hautcorvin, registrar, at the Town House, there appeared
publicly:

**Honoré Tessonnal**, master glassmaker at the Hautcorvin Glassworks, aged twenty-three, born in this
commune on the fifteenth of August eighteen hundred and eighty, residing in the rue Haute, son of full
age of Évariste Tessonnal, director of the Glassworks, and of Clotilde Mervaux, gilder, his wife, here
present and consenting;

and **Eugénie Ravenel**, milliner, aged twenty, born in this commune on the ninth of November
eighteen hundred and eighty-three, residing in the rue Haute, at the sign of “Ravenel, Millinery”,
daughter under age of the late Aristide Ravenel, hatter, and of Joséphine Pélissier, his widow,
milliner, here present and consenting;

who, the banns having been published without objection, have required us to proceed to the
celebration of their marriage. Each of them having declared their wish to take the other as husband
and as wife, we have pronounced, in the name of the law, that they are united in marriage.

In the presence of: Gustave Ravenel, hatter at Aubrelieu, brother of the bride; Mathilde Corbin,
milliner, rue Haute, friend of the bride; witnesses of full age; and of **Léonce Tessonnal**, lens
maker at the Glassworks, younger brother of the groom and under age, present at the entry; who have
signed with the spouses, the parents and us, after reading.

::signature[H. Tessonnal — E. Ravenel — É. Tessonnal — C. Tessonnal née Mervaux — J. Ravenel née Pélissier]

::signature[G. Ravenel — M. Corbin — L. Tessonnal — the Mayor]

*Marginal note:* “Marriage dissolved by the death of the wife, which occurred at Hautcorvin on
4 April 1931.”
