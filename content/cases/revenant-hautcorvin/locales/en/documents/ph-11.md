## Front

Print, 18 × 24 cm, pale grey, slightly overexposed by the snow: patches of it remain on the steps of
Vernezac church. Thirteen people on the forecourt.

In the centre, a very tall man in customs uniform, short moustache, sticking-out ears, his kepi
under his arm. On his arm, a woman of about thirty in a light-coloured costume, a black plait
brought forward over her shoulder; a horn whistle hangs at her neck, over her blouse. In front of
them, a girl of eight in a white bridesmaid’s dress, black hair cut short, a lock standing up in a
cowlick in the middle of her forehead, holds the bouquet upside down.

On the left: an old woman with white hair, a bunch of keys at her belt, even on that day; a man of
about forty, short beard, weathered face, badge No. 19 pinned to his lapel; a woman in her best
dress, low chignon, round spectacles; a boy of about ten and a girl of five, hand in hand.

On the right: a second customs officer of about forty-five, bushy moustache, braided kepi on his
head; two young people in their Sunday best; two old people leaning on each other.
