## Item 1 — Letter of denunciation (22 September 1941)

*Letterhead paper of the Hautcorvin Glassworks, management, Hautcorvin. Typewritten, signed in ink.*

Hautcorvin, 22 September 1941

To the Commissioner of Registers for the province of the Orvelle,

Sir,

I have the honour, and the painful duty, to bring the following facts to your attention.

The Regent's Cup, presented by our firm to His Highness on 7 September, during his visit to
Hautcorvin, was engraved in our Vernezac workshop by one Andrin Dalvarin, engraver, son of a Thalbère
subject expelled in July 1938. Having had occasion to examine under a loupe the photographic proofs
of the piece, taken before it was handed over, I noticed in the laurel wreath, on the left-hand side,
certain marks which do not appear on the approved design and which I can interpret in no other way
than as an insult.

I feel bound to add that this engraver was, in 1937 and 1938, the intimate of the fire-raiser
Tessonnal, struck off, to whom, it is said, he carried provisions during his flight.

Our firm, which has the honour of working for the Regency, could not cover up such conduct. I remain
at your disposal and ask you to treat this letter as confidential.

I have the honour to be, Sir, with my entire devotion to the Order,

::signature[A. Brossal, company secretary of the Hautcorvin Glassworks]

## Item 4 — Report of the Provincial Registers Office (26 September 1941)

**PROVINCIAL REGISTERS OFFICE OF THE ORVELLE — FOR THE CENSUS AND REMOVALS BUREAU**

**Report No. 41-1987/4. Subject: the Cup affair; one DALVARIN Andrin, engraver.**

1. The Cup, withdrawn from the Regent's household on 24 September, was placed under seal (41-1987-1) and examined under a loupe. In the wreath, on the left-hand side, between the seventh and eighth leaves, three tiny marks cut with the point: “B. T. 38”, which the department interprets as the initials of the struck-off Tessonnal and the year he was struck off.
2. Arrested on the evening of the 24th at Vernezac (anthropometric record card 41-1987, made out on the 25th), the engraver admits to having engraved them. He states: “He is my cousin. His name is no longer written anywhere. It had to be written somewhere.” He would add nothing further.
3. The engraver was the intimate of the struck-off fire-raiser (report of the Hautcorvin Guard of Order, 22 January 1938).
4. Proposal: removal for insulting the Regent and for collusion with a struck-off person; transfer to Kessaar. Mr A. Brossal's letter is placed on file; there is no need to hear him.

::signature[The Deputy Provincial Commissioner of Registers for the Orvelle]
