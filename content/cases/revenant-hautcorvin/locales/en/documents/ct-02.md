## BRÉNICA GLASSWORKS

### *Vernadra Brénica* — Catalogue 1974

*Glass blown and engraved by hand, at Brénica, at the foot of the passes. Our house engraves glass as
it was engraved in the upper Orvelle valley before the war: with the copper wheel, with emery, and
with the point to finish.*

### Our engravers

*Full-page photograph.* A thin man of about sixty, seated at the engraving wheel, three-quarter view,
a tumbler raised to eye level against the light of a window. White hair cropped short, black
eyebrows, straight and thick, a dimple in the left cheek. A loupe hangs from a cord over his leather
apron. His left hand, which holds the glass, bears a broad pale patch across the back.

Caption: *Mr A. Dalvarin, master engraver, at the wheel. Trained at Vernezac, in the former workshop
of the Hautcorvin Glassworks; with us since 1953.*

Beneath, in smaller type: *Mrs Tesela Korin, engraver, and the apprentice Bralko Senin.*

---

*On the back page.* Brénica Glassworks, rue des Scieurs, Brénica (Brenzac). Visits to the workshop on
Saturday mornings. Dispatched to Velmarche by the pass road.
