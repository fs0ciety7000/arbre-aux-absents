*Tumbler of blown white glass, 11 cm high, wheel-engraved all the way round: a notch between two
ridges, which everyone in Vernezac recognises as the Pas de l’Ourse; a zigzag path, with a small
cross at the third bend; a she-bear and her cub climbing towards the notch. Below the rim, in
copperplate engraved with the point:*

**“To the glassblower’s daughter — 14.II.1959”**

*No signature, no workshop mark. The base shows signs of wear: the glass has been used.*

## The packaging (kept)

A cardboard box wrapped in brown paper and tied with string. Address in block capitals, in grease
pencil: “MISS AGNÈS NEVIOT — VERNEZAC — VELMARCHE”. Thalbère stamps; Brénica postmark of 10 February
1959. No sender’s name, no letter in the box.

## Loan record (1976)

::stamp[CIVIL RE-REGISTRATION OFFICE — REGISTRY — DOCUMENT ON LOAN]

Engraved glass lent to the Office by Mrs Agnès Neviot, nurse, on 11 February 1976, at her hearing at
the Hautcorvin hospice, “so you can look at it, not so you can keep it”. To be returned to the lender
after examination by the Museum of the Fire Arts.

::signature[F. Ollier, investigating clerk]
