## Front

Small print, 6 × 9 cm, deckled edges, folded once in half and then flattened: the fold runs under
the chin. A young woman of about twenty stands on the threshold of a stone hut, one hand on the
door frame. Dark eyes, a single black plait brought forward over her shoulder, a roll-neck woollen
jumper; a horn whistle hangs from her neck on a lace. She is laughing at someone standing beside
the photographer, whom we cannot see.

Above the door, the painted board of the refuge de l’Ourse; in the foreground, a bench, a pair of
wooden skis leaning against the wall, a bucket.
