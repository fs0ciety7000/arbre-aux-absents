## BULLETIN OF ORDER

### Official Journal of the Regency of Velmarche — Thursday 16 October 1941 — No. 85

*Page printed in two columns on the Bulletin's grey paper; p. 671. Price of this issue: forty order
sous.*

**CENSUS AND REMOVALS BUREAU — REMOVAL LIST No. 40**

Having regard to the Act of 2 February 1938 on the purity of the registers; having regard to the
records of the Registers Bureau of the province of the Orvelle;

**Article One.** — The individuals named below are struck off the civil registers and **declared
absent**. Those for whom a destination is given shall be transferred there without delay.

| No. | Surname and given names | Born, at | Occupation | Grounds | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | BERTHOMIEU Gaston | 9 January 1897, Aubrelieu | bookbinder | possession of banned printed matter | Kessaar |
| 2 | DALVARIN Andrin | 2 September 1912, Vernezac | glass engraver | insult to the Regent; collusion with a struck-off person | Kessaar |
| 3 | FORESTIER Germaine | 14 May 1915, Port-Sénane | shorthand typist | seditious remarks | — |
| 4 | ROUQUETTE Arsène | 30 July 1890, Saint-Ormel | shoemaker | harbouring a struck-off person | Kessaar |

**Article 2.** — The objects used in committing the insult are confiscated and placed among the
exhibits.

**Article 3.** — The families of the absent are required to surrender their identity papers to the
Registers Bureau of their province within eight days.

::signature[For the Regent and by delegation, the Head of the Census and Removals Bureau]
