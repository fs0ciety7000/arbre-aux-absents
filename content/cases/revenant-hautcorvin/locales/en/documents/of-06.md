::stamp[CIVIL RE-REGISTRATION OFFICE — REGISTRY — RECEIVED 7 OCT. 1974 — No. 74-0962]

**Petition for the restoration of parentage** *(Civil Re-registration Act of 14 May 1973)*

*Handwritten letter, two sheets of squared paper, blue ink.*

::hand[Hautcorvin, 3 October 1974]

::hand[Dear Sir,]

::hand[My name is Agnès Neviot. I was born on 14 February 1939 at Vernezac. I have been a nurse at the Hautcorvin hospice since 1960 and I live at Vernezac, with my stepfather.]

::hand[I am the daughter of Lucie Neviot, keeper of the refuge de l’Ourse, who died on 23 January 1969, and of Bastien Tessonnal, glassblower at the Vernezac hall, struck off in 1938, died at Kessaar, I believe.]

::hand[On my birth certificate, where the father should be, there is an empty line. I read in the newspaper that your law was made to fill in empty lines. I would like his name to be written on mine.]

::hand[I have only one letter from my mother, written for me a little before she died; she was too ill to hold the pen, so my cousin wrote it for her, and she took it down as my mother dictated. I am keeping it. I will show it to whoever asks me for it, but I will not trust it to the post.]

::hand[I know other things were said, in the village. My mother told me this one. My mother did not lie: she kept quiet, and that is not the same thing.]

::hand[I ask for nothing but a name. I do not need money, I have a trade.]

::hand[Yours respectfully,]

::signature[Agnès Neviot]

---

**Inquiry note** *(typed sheet, stapled to the petition)*

::stamp[FILIATIONS DEPARTMENT — INQUIRY — 74-0962]

**Claimant:** Agnès Neviot, born 14 February 1939 at Vernezac, nurse.
**Subject:** restoration of paternal parentage (alleged father: Bastien Tessonnal, glassblower).

**Searches made.**

1. Birth certificate (Vernezac, 14 February 1939): “daughter of Lucie Neviot, refuge keeper; father
   not named”. No acknowledgement.
2. The alleged father appears on Removal List No. 9 (1938). Kessaar committal register (restricted
   access, at the Office’s request): died in the penal colony on 9 March 1944. Death not entered in
   the civil register.
3. The claimant produces no document. The mother’s letter, which she mentions, has not been
   submitted.
4. Witnesses: the mother is deceased. The committal register names a single cellmate of the alleged
   father who is still living, to the Office’s knowledge: A. Dalvarin, residing at Dalvica
   (Thalberie).

**Action taken.** Wrote on 14 November 1975 to the only cellmate identified, A. Dalvarin, Dalvica
(Thalberie), through consular channels, asking for his testimony. No reply as of 1 December.

**Opinion.** One possible document, none produced. The Act requires two concordant documents
(Art. 6). Suspended for want of documents.

::signature[J. Ramade]

J. Ramade, executive officer, Filiations Department, 2 December 1975

::hand[Joined to 76-0012 on 2 February 1976. The claimant in that file declares himself to be the father. — R. F.]
