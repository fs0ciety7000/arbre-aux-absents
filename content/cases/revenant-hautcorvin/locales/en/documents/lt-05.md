*Envelope of grey paper, addressed in pencil; across it, a violet stamp. Inside, a single sheet.
According to the registry inventory, the envelope had remained unopened: it was opened on
23 February 1976 at Vernezac town hall, in the presence of Mr V. Sarrade.*

## The envelope

Mr Bastien Tessonnal, prisoner

Kessaar penal colony

(island of Kessaar, via Port-Sénane)

::stamp[ADDRESSEE ABSENT — ACT OF 2 FEBRUARY 1938, ART. 6 — RETURN TO SENDER]

*On the back:* “L. N., refuge de l’Ourse, Vernezac”.

## The letter

::hand[Vernezac, 20 February 1939]

::hand[B.,]

::hand[She was born on the 14th, at five in the morning, while it was snowing. I am calling her Agnès. She has your cowlick, the very same, in the middle of the forehead. The woman who was helping me tried to smooth it down with water; it sprang back up on its own.]

::hand[I did not give your name at the town hall. Papa registered her as “father not named”. It is not to disown you. It is so that they do not come for her too.]

::hand[Papa still goes up, even in the snow. He says the mountain is kind this winter, that it lets people through.]

::hand[I have the glass tear. I am not breaking the tail.]

::hand[We don’t know if we are allowed to write to you. I am writing anyway. If they give it to you, answer me with one word, any word at all.]

::hand[L.]
