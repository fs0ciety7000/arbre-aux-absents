## I. Register of marriage certificates — year 1934, entry No. 9

In the year nineteen hundred and thirty-four, on Saturday the fifteenth of September, at ten o'clock
in the morning, before us, mayor of the commune of Vernezac, registrar, at the Town House, there
appeared publicly:

**Rémi Neviot**, mountain guide (Vernezac Company, badge No. 19), aged twenty-six, born in this
commune on the seventeenth of March nineteen hundred and eight, son of full age of Jules Neviot,
guide, and of Philomène Ardouin, innkeeper, his wife, here present and consenting;

and **Odette Lamberge**, schoolmistress at the Vernezac school, aged twenty-one, born at Aubrelieu on
the fifth of November nineteen hundred and twelve, residing in the schoolhouse lodgings, daughter of
full age of Albert Lamberge, post office clerk, and of Jeanne Rivière, his wife, residing at
Aubrelieu, consenting by deed received on the third of September;

who have required us to proceed to the celebration of their marriage, and have been united in the
name of the law.

In the presence of: Jules Neviot, guide, father of the groom; Clément Bousquet, headmaster of the
Vernezac school; who have signed with us, after reading.

::signature[R. Neviot — O. Lamberge — J. Neviot — Ph. Neviot née Ardouin — C. Bousquet — the Mayor]

## II. Register of birth certificates (extracts)

**1936, No. 21.** On Tuesday the second of June nineteen hundred and thirty-six there was born at
Vernezac **Gilles Neviot**, son of Rémi Neviot, guide, and of Odette Lamberge, schoolmistress, his
wife. The father being out on a climb at the Pas, the birth was registered on the fourth of June by
the midwife, in the presence of the mother, **Mrs Odette Neviot**, schoolmistress at the Vernezac
school, who signed.

**1941, No. 30.** On Wednesday the twentieth of August nineteen hundred and forty-one there was born
at Vernezac **Brigitte Neviot**, daughter of Rémi Neviot, guide, and of Odette Lamberge,
schoolmistress, his wife. Informant: the father.

*Endorsement (1941):* “Seen and entered in the provincial index of births. The Delegate of the
Orvelle Registers Bureau.”

Certified true extracts.

::signature[The Mayor of Vernezac, registrar]
