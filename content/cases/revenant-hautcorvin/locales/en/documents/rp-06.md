*Item released to the LX-2 Reader on the request by name “Bastien Tessonnal”. BRR file 38-0377.*

**CENSUS AND REMOVALS BUREAU — CENTRAL DIRECTORATE, AUBRELIEU**

**NOTE No. 44-0412**

Aubrelieu, Monday 14 February 1944

**Subject:** file 38-0377 — the struck-off Bastien Tessonnal, held at Kessaar under No. 0214.

Pursuant to the decree of 3 February 1944 on attacks against undertakings working for the Order,
the blaze in the furnace hall of the Hautcorvin Glassworks (night of 27 to 28 November 1937) is
reclassified as **sabotage**.

Prisoner 0214 will be transferred to Aubrelieu by the Kessaar launch, to be tried by the Court of
Order at its April 1944 session. The Kessaar registry has been notified.

::signature[For the Director of the Bureau, the head of the files section]
