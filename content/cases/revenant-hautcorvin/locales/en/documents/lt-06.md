## Front (printed)

::stamp[KESSAAR PENAL COLONY — ADMINISTRATION — CANTEEN]

**CANTEEN VOUCHER**
Value: **2 sous**. Not refundable. Not transferable.

Prisoner No.: ::hand[0214]   Cell: ::hand[14]

*Any prisoner who gives or receives a voucher shall be punished by loss of canteen privileges.*

## Back (in pencil)

::hand[L.,]

::hand[I won’t get out of here. The doctor says nothing, which means there is nothing to say. Don’t cry, you always cried too late anyway.]

::hand[Andrin will bring you this when they let him go. They will let him go, him. Believe him as you would believe me. He knows everything: I told him everything, two and a half years of it, at night, the refuge, the path, the cross at the third bend, the glass tear, what you said the first time. Don’t be angry. Someone had to know, and he forgets nothing.]

::hand[He has my face, don’t be fooled: he has his mother’s eyes. When he is on your doorstep, look at his eyes.]

::hand[He says the little one was born on 14 February, that she is called Agnès, that she has the cowlick. I counted on my fingers ten times. I know. ~~Tell her that~~ Tell the little one her father didn’t set the fire. That it was Mr ::redacted[7] who ~~did~~ — no. Mallet knows. Let her ask Mallet, when all this is over. Just tell her that her father didn’t set the fire, and that he was laughing.]

::hand[Keep the glass tear. Don’t break it by the tail.]

::hand[B.]
