::stamp[CIVIL RE-REGISTRATION OFFICE — FILIATIONS DEPARTMENT — FILE CLOSED]

Aubrelieu, Friday 19 March 1976

**Subject:** closure of file No. 76-0012 and of joined file No. 74-0962.

The Commission ruled on Monday 15 March, on your report. It followed your conclusions; the decision
is on file, with the table, and I shall add nothing to it. The notes have been entered in the
registers of Hautcorvin and Vernezac. Maître Escande has received a copy of the decision, for the
estate of Mr Honoré Tessonnal. The documents on loan have been returned to their owners, the glass
wrapped in three layers of tissue paper, as its owner had asked.

It is customary, when a file is closed, for the rapporteur to query the Reader one last time on the
name that opened it. I did so in your place this morning, because you were already on the next one.
The slip is attached just as the printer ran it off.

> LX-2 READER — CENTRAL INDEXED COLLECTION — QUERY No. 76-02231
>
> HEADING REQUESTED: BASTIEN TESSONNAL
>
> — REMOVAL LIST No. 9 — 26 MARCH 1938
>
> — RE-REGISTRATION COMMISSION — DECISION OF 15 MARCH 1976
>
> END OF LIST.

Two lines. There was only one on 2 February. The second does not say everything; the registers never
say everything. It says at least that someone looked.

The file is closed. Thank you.

::signature[R. Ferrandot]

Rosine Ferrandot, head of the Filiations Department
