**MEDICAL EXPERT REPORT**

*Commissioned by order of the rapporteur in charge of file 76-0012 of the Civil Re-registration
Office, dated 6 February 1976, at the request of the objecting party, to examine the claimant and
answer the following questions: distinguishing marks on the hands; state of the teeth; colour of the
eyes; shape of the ear; height; hairline; apparent age.*

I, the undersigned, Dr Noémie Castel, forensic physician, senior demonstrator at the Institute of
Forensic Medicine of the University of Aubrelieu, examined the claimant on Tuesday 10 February 1976,
from nine o'clock until a quarter past ten, on the Institute's premises, in the presence of Mr F.
Ollier, investigating clerk.

The subject submitted to the examination without difficulty. He took off his gloves in front of me,
saying: “You – you're a doctor.”

## Findings

| Heading | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Apparent age | sixty to sixty-eight |
| Height | 1.71 m, barefoot. Slight thoracic kyphosis. |
| Build | thin; muscle mass of the forearms well preserved |
| Right hand, back | **no scar**. Skin supple, pigmentation even, hair present, no trace of a graft or of a burn, however old. |
| Left hand, back | **deep burn scar**, oval, 6 × 4 cm, from the wrist to the base of the third and fourth metacarpals; skin white, shiny, contracted, adherent to the deeper tissues. Between **twenty and forty years** old. |
| Hands, palms | fine, symmetrical calluses on the pads of the right thumb and index finger, a callused groove on the outer side of the right middle finger: prolonged use of a tool held between the fingers. No band of callus across the palm. |
| Teeth | natural, worn, incomplete at the back. **Upper central incisors intact**, no crack, no repair, no crown. |
| Eyes | iris **dark brown**, uniform, no heterochromia. Early arcus senilis. |
| Ears | earlobe **attached** on both sides; antihelix forked in a Y on the right. |
| Hair | white, cut to about 5 mm; frontal hairline even, **no cowlick or whorl**. |
| Other | a single dimple in the left cheek, permanent. |

## Discussion

1. *On the hands.* A deep burn leaves a permanent scar; it does not fade in forty-nine years. The
   one on the left hand is twenty to forty years old; I cannot be more precise. No scar, old or
   recent, on the back of the right hand.
2. *On the eyes.* I am told that the subject puts the difference in colour down to age. In the adult,
   the iris does not darken with age; if anything it tends to grow paler. A light grey iris at
   twenty-eight does not turn dark brown at sixty-five.
3. *On height.* The difference of three centimetres from a description taken in 1938 is not
   conclusive: settling of that order is commonplace at this age, especially after years of hard
   labour.
4. *On the teeth.* A chipped incisor does not mend itself; it is treated, and the treatment shows.
   There is nothing of the kind here.

## Conclusion

I describe a man. I express no opinion on his name.
