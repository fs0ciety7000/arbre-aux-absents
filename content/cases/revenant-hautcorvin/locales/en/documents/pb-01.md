## VERNEZAC

### its guides — its slopes — its refuge de l'Ourse

*Three leagues from Hautcorvin by the Vèze road, at the foot of the Monts Corvins.*

**In winter.** Marked runs and a ski tow above the village. Qualified instructors. Snow Festival on
the third Sunday of February: torchlit descent, toboggan race, dance at the Auberge.

**In summer.** Mountain climbs with the Company's guides, numbered badges since 1898. The refuge de
l'Ourse, five hours' climb, just below the Pas: twenty bunks, hot soup, a view over two countries.

*Medallion, photograph:* a woman of about fifty, a long plait over her shoulder, a horn whistle
hanging round her neck, smiles on the threshold of a stone hut.

**Refuge kept from June to September by Mrs Sarrade.**

*At the foot of the poster, in small type:* Hotels and rooms in private houses: apply to the town
hall. The post leaves Vernezac every day by road, and Aubrelieu by the mail plane. Photograph by
Studio Orlhac.
