Newspaper cutting, 11 × 15 cm, cut out with scissors, halftone screen visible. A very old man,
seated, in three-quarter view: a white mane swept back, bushy eyebrows, a single dimple in the left
cheek. His two hands, enormous, mottled with old burns, rest on a glassblower’s blowpipe that he uses
as a walking stick.

Caption printed beneath the photograph: *Mr Honoré Tessonnal, master glassmaker, in 1965. (Photo:
Studio Orlhac.)*

On the back, the foot of a column of classified advertisements from *The Corvins Sentinel* of
20 December 1975, and, in ink, in a notary’s hand:

::hand[Enclosed with my letter of 29 Dec. — J. E.]
