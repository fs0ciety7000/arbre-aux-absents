## Front

Photo postcard, grey print, somewhat faded. The Tennok lighthouse seen from below, white with a red
band, on its rocky headland. At the top, behind the panes of the lantern, the figure of a slim man,
in profile, bending over the optical apparatus; the sun catches his round spectacles.

Printed caption: *CAP DE TENNOK — The lighthouse.*

## Back

*Address:* Mr Honoré Tessonnal, master glassmaker, Hautcorvin Glassworks, Hautcorvin.

::hand[Monday 18 Dec.]

::hand[Dear old chap,]

::hand[Here since 12 November for Papa's lens; I shan't leave before February. The new burner hisses, the lens doesn't shift by a hair – it stands straighter than the two of us. Christmas on the cape. The keeper is a woman and knows more than the firm's lens maker; I pretend to teach her things, she pretends to listen.]

::hand[That's me, up there, in the lantern. The photographer at the post office says I'm a good likeness from behind.]
