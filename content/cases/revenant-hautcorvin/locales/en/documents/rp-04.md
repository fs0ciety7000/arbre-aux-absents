## RECORD OF ARREST

### No. 38-27

In the year nineteen hundred and thirty-eight, on Saturday the twenty-first of May, at a quarter past
eleven at night, we the undersigned, **Achille Fauvel**, sergeant, and **V. Sarrade**, officer, of
the Vèze customs post, on night patrol on the Pas de l'Ourse path, report as follows.

A hundred paces below the gap, at the foot of the scree, above the iron cross on the third hairpin, we
caught sight of two persons climbing towards the frontier without a light. On being challenged, the
man stopped and raised his arms. **A woman who was with him fled towards the frontier** across the
scree; she could not be overtaken in the dark and has not been identified.

The man stated that his name was **Tessonnal Bastien**, born on 14 May 1910 at Hautcorvin. We
recognised him as the man struck off under List No. 9 (Bulletin of Order of 26 March 1938), glassblower
at the Hautcorvin Glassworks, wanted for the blaze in the hall.

| Description | Observed |
| --- | --- |
| Height | 1.74 m |
| Hair | chestnut, a cowlick in the middle of the forehead |
| Eyes | grey |
| Distinguishing marks | shiny burn scar on the back of the right hand; chipped upper left front tooth; dimple in the left cheek |
| Effects | a mountain rucksack (bread, bacon, a rope, an unlit lamp), a knife |

The accused made no statement. He asked only “that no one go looking for the woman, who has nothing to
do with it”.

He was handed over on 22 May at six o'clock to the Hautcorvin Guard of Order (Lieutenant Saunier),
against a receipt.

Having heard this read over, the accused signed with us.

::signature[A. Fauvel, sergeant — V. Sarrade, officer]

::signature[B. Tessonnal]

*The accused's signature in pencil, pressed hard; the T of “Tessonnal” is crossed with a short straight
