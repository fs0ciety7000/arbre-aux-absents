Lower half of a printed Velmarche Posts form, cut with scissors, quite straight. On the front, all
that remains of the print is the bottom of a box and the words *“Form No. 698 — write nothing below
this line”*. Below the line, in pencil, in careful capitals, by someone anxious not to have the
handwriting recognised:

::hand[ASK THE VERNEZAC POST OFFICE HOW HE SIGNS HIS TELEGRAMS.]

No date, no signature. The back is blank.

*Ordinary envelope, addressed in the same capitals:* ::hand[CIVIL RE-REGISTRATION OFFICE — AUBRELIEU — THE REVENANT’S FILE]

::stamp[VELMARCHE POSTS — VERNEZAC — 30 I 76 — 17 H]
