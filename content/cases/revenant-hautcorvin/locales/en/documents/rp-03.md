**SURVEILLANCE REPORT No. 38-019**

**Subject:** Refuge de l'Ourse (commune of Vernezac). Search for the fugitive Tessonnal.

We, Hippolyte Rascol, guard, on daytime watch since 8 January on the path to the Refuge de l'Ourse,
above Vernezac, report as follows.

1. The refuge, closed to climbing parties in winter, is nonetheless occupied by the keeper, the girl Neviot Lucie, twenty-three, who looks after the Company's wood and blankets there.
2. The man Dalvarin Andrin, engraver at the Glassworks, son of the foreign woman they call the Thalbère woman, goes up every Saturday after pay, carrying a sack, and comes down on Sunday evening. Questioned on 15 January at the second hairpin, he stated that he “takes up wood and flour for the keeper, who is alone up there”. It is common knowledge in the village that he is keeping company with the Neviot girl.
3. No trace of the fugitive Tessonnal in the vicinity. A search of the refuge on the 15th produced nothing. The tracks in the snow around the refuge are those of two persons, sometimes three.
4. The refuge is one hour from the Pas de l'Ourse. Clandestine crossings suspected: the engraver, who speaks Thalbère and whose family is on the other side, could be acting as a border runner.

We propose that the watch be maintained until the thaw.

Vernezac, Saturday 22 January 1938.

::signature[H. Rascol, guard]

::hand[Watch the Thalbère. Do not intervene before the thaw. — Lt R. S.]
