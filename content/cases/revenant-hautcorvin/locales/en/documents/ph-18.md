## Front

A proof print, 18 × 24 cm, made by the Office from an intact 13 × 18 cm glass plate. In front of the
mouth of Furnace No. 2, its cast-iron door raised, two young men stand full length, side by side,
shoulder to shoulder, well lit by two studio lamps. The same oval face, the same build; each has a
dimple in his left cheek.

**On the left**, a young man of about twenty-five holds his canvas cap in his hand; a lock of hair
stands up in a cowlick in the middle of his forehead. His very pale eyes look almost white on the
print. He is smiling, his lips parted over a chipped front tooth. His right hand holds a blowpipe
upright, like a rifle on parade, and the back of that hand gleams with a broad scar. A ribbon
rosette is pinned to his vest.

**On the right**, a young man a little younger, with black hair whose line across the forehead is
even, with no cowlick. His eyes are dark, under straight, thick eyebrows, almost joined. He wears
the engravers’ leather apron; a loupe already hangs from a cord on his chest. He holds in both
hands, flat, a cup engraved with a stag among fir trees, and his hands, on the glass, are unmarked.
The same rosette.
