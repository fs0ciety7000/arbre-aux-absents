## Front

Albumen print mounted on cream card, 18 × 24 cm, with the blind stamp of Studio Lumen. An exhibition
stand hung with dark velvet; two glazed display cases full of necklaces, brooches and blocks of polished
amber; on the pediment of the set, an emblem in relief, a crowned fork. In front of the cases, three
people pose standing: a moustached man in a frock coat, a pale tiepin; a young woman in a high-collared
dress, her hand on the shoulder of a little boy in a sailor suit.

Beneath the image, printed on the mount: “Grand Exhibition of Aubrelieu, 1889 — Luxury Industries
Section — Stand No. 214”.

## Back

In ink, in a slanting hand:

::hand[“Mr and Mrs B. Garnaud and their son. Silver medal. July 1889.”]

In pencil, later: “Company album, No. 2.”
