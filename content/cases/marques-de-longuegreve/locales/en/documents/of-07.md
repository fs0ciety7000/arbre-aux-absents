Bains-de-Mérande, 28 September 1977

Dear Sir,

I saw the State Lands notice about the island of Longuegrève in the paper. I am not from the island and
I claim nothing. But my great-aunt, **Sidonie Brémoy**, was schoolmistress on Longuegrève from 1886
until the evacuation. She married Benoît Lestaud out there, from the Longuegrève light; everyone called
her Mrs Lestaud, and on the island, “the mistress”. She died at our house in 1950.

She left three or four **chapel notebooks** – that is what we called them at home – written in her own
hand, close-written, with names and dates. There is also a school register, some letters and some
photographs. My mother used to say they must never be thrown away and never be shown. My mother is
dead, and I believe the time has come to show them.

I will bring them to anyone willing to hear me. I do not travel much any more; if someone from your
office can come to Bains-de-Mérande, I am at home every day, at 12 chemin des Salines.

Yours respectfully,

::signature[Ginette Sollier]
