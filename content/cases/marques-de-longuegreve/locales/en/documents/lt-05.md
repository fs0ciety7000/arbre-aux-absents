::stamp[LHERMET — NOTARIES AT PORT-SÉNANE — TESTAMENT DEPOSITED ON 14 FEBRUARY 1924]

*A sheet of laid paper, written entirely in the testator's hand, in careful, separate letters, like a
man who learned late.*

This is my testament.

I leave the Amber Company, its shares, its workshops and its shores, to my son **Léandre**, who already
runs it better than I do.

I leave to my wife **Amélie**, née Ferlay, the house on the quay and the bookshop, which is hers in any
case.

I ask to be buried in Port-Sénane cemetery, facing the gulf, with my mark under my name.

Safe-deposit box No. 108 at the Savings Bank is not mine. What is in it belongs to the four marks. Let
it be opened only for them. My son has nothing to take from it and he knows it.

Done at Port-Sénane, Thursday 30 June 1921.

::signature[B. Garnaud ::mark[lg-portrait-480ba1]]

*Notary's note, 14 February 1924:* “Opened and described. The residuary legatee, Mr Léandre Garnaud,
director of the Company, states that he does not know the contents of the safe-deposit box and does not
wish to know them.”
