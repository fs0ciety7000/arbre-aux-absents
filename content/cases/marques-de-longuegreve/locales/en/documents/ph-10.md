## Front

Print 18 × 24 cm, baryta paper, very sharp. In front of the plank wall of a boatshed, a man and a woman
hold four oars upright, blades in the air, the way flags are presented. On the flat of each blade, at
hand height, a mark carved with a knife, which the print makes legible; from left to right: ::mark[lg-portrait-84365f] (dated “1872” with a hot iron), ::mark[lg-portrait-ed16c7], ::mark[lg-portrait-9cb0f2], ::mark[lg-portrait-d18c08].

On the right, on a workbench, three objects laid side by side for the photograph, each marked:

| Object | Mark |
| --- | --- |
| A wooden spindle | ::mark[lg-portrait-3f8d58] |
| A round bread stamp, in boxwood (mark in relief) | ::mark[lg-portrait-ff741e] |
| A netting needle, in boxwood | ::mark[lg-portrait-7f010c] |

The man, in his forties, has very curly black hair going grey. The woman, older, with very pale eyes,
holds one of the oars against her.

## Back

In pencil, in S. Marest's hand:
