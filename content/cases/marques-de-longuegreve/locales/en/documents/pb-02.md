## Coastal lines from Port-Sénane

| Packet | Line | Sailings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Orvelle* | Port-Sénane – Aubrelieu (along the coast) | Tuesday and Friday, 7.00 |
| *Duchesse-d'Ambre* | islands of the gulf, passengers and mail: Tennok, Saint-Aubert, **call at Longuegrève on the 1st and the 15th** | according to the tide |
| *Sainte-Aude* | goods, northern islands | on request |

*The Longuegrève call is made in the roads, by the island boat. In bad weather it is cancelled without
notice. Passengers are requested to present themselves one hour before the tide.*
