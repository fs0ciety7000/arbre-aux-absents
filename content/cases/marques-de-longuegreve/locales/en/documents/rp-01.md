::stamp[ESTUARY DISTRICT — EPIDEMICS DEPARTMENT — CERTIFICATE]

*A typed sheet, white paper without letterhead, signature in ink.*

I, the undersigned, Cyprien Pélissart, doctor of medicine, medical officer for epidemics of the
Estuary district, certify that I visited **this day** the island of Longuegrève, a section of the
commune of Port-Sénane, and observed there:

1. the existence of an outbreak of **typhoid fever**, characterised by several cases of continued
   fever among the children and the old;
2. **two deaths** that have occurred since the month of December, attributable to the same cause;
3. the pollution of the island's only well, whose water is cloudy and of suspicious smell;
4. the general unhealthiness of the dwellings, built of wood and thatch, without ventilation, and
   which cannot be disinfected.

Consequently, I consider that the population cannot remain on the island without danger to itself
and to the coast, and I recommend:
- the **evacuation** of all the inhabitants at the earliest opportunity;
- the **destruction by fire** of the dwellings and the contaminated objects;
- a prohibition of residence until further notice.

Done at Port-Sénane, Friday 13 March 1908.

::signature[Dr C. Pélissart]

*On the back, in blue pencil, in the commissioner's hand:* “Received on the 13th in the evening.
Order to be made on Monday. — E. P.”
