## Front

Gelatin silver print, 18 × 24 cm, pasted into the album. An open-air wedding, in front of a row of trees.
In the centre, the bride in white holds a bouquet; beside her, the groom, very curly black hair. Around
them, nine guests in two rows. A young woman holds a swaddled infant in her arms. Beneath the image, a
caption in ink, washed out by damp: all that can still be read is “…pril 1913” and “Hamelot”.

## In pencil, on the album page

In Victoire Cormaud's hand:

::hand[“Our wedding, at the Cabanes. Lazare and me. His parents, Noël and Honorine; my mother. Gatien and Léontine with little Félix, who was one. Clément, who laughed the whole time.”]

*Investigator's note, 12 October 1977:* “Album handed over by Mr Marin Cormaud. The names are not
placed on the image.”
