*Plate II. Tracings made on the island, Saturday 29 August 1964, westerly wind. Oak crosses, marks
carved with a knife under the crosspiece. The names are those Mrs Cormaud spoke in front of each cross
(tape LG-64-05): by given name or nickname, as people were called on the island.*

## Longuegrève cemetery

Fourteen legible crosses, one of them a cenotaph. The crosses of the heads of the roll (Gervais
Goémard, Clovis Oyard, Siméon Cormaud, Lubin Lestaud) and of their wives are grouped by house, from the
west wall towards the sea.

*Notes.* Nos 9 and 10: a single grave, “Siméon's two boys, drowned together in the westerly gale”.
Nos 13 and 14: bar alone, no head, “the people of the *Aurore*”, five dead under two crosses. Two deal
crosses against No. 3, illegible: “my father, and little Anne” (Mrs Cormaud). No tracing.
