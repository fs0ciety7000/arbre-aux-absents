::stamp[CIVIL RE-REGISTRATION OFFICE — FILE 77-0874 — OBJECT DESCRIBED AT A HEARING]

*Investigator's description, 12 October 1977.*

Grey granite lintel, 1.10 m by 0.30 m, laid on two trestles in the yard of the Friendly Society's shed.
In the middle of the face, cut in intaglio:

::mark[lg-portrait-fdaeee] “1890” “S.B.”

Mr Marin Cormaud states: “The lintel of the light house, Benoît Lestaud's and the mistress's. They cut
it the year they married: his mark, her initials, because she could write. In 1908, the mistress had it
taken out the day before the commission's fire; six men to carry it to the boat. The constables let
them.”
