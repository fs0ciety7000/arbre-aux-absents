## Longuegrève evacuated: the fever

*An island given back to the birds. — Sixty-one persons taken in at the Cabanes d'Ambre.*

Last Thursday, 2 April, the constabulary carried out the complete evacuation of the island of
Longuegrève, a section of our commune, in execution of an order of Commissioner Pontcarré.

It is known that Dr Pélissart, medical officer for epidemics, had established on 13 March, in a health
certificate in due form, the existence on the island of an outbreak of **typhoid fever** that had
already caused two deaths. The well water was tainted. There was no other course to take.

The inhabitants, rough folk attached to their rock, at first made difficulties. One old man refused to
go aboard and gave in only the next day. On Saturday the houses, a seat of contagion, were destroyed by
fire.

We must here salute **the generosity of Mr Garnaud, shipowner**, who has placed his houses at the
Cabanes at the families' disposal free of charge, and promised work to all at his amber workshop. Our
photographer was on the jetty when the boat arrived (see our picture).
