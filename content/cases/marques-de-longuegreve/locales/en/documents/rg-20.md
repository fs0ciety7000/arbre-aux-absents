**Folio 41. — Safe-deposit box No. 108 (large size, lower strongroom).**

| Heading | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Date of rental | Saturday 6 June 1908 |
| Renter | Mr B. Garnaud, merchant, Hôtel Garnaud, quai du Môle |
| On behalf of | ::mark[lg-portrait-480ba1] *(drawn by the renter, in ink, in the box)* |
| Term | one hundred years, until 5 June 2008 |
| Rent | 600 fl., paid in advance, in cash |
| Opening | “reserved to the bearers of the marks below, or to their beneficiaries; failing them, to the public authority” |
| Marks | ::mark[lg-portrait-9e0e20] ::mark[lg-portrait-aa93f2] ::mark[lg-portrait-5ce728] ::mark[lg-portrait-8084e7] *(drawn by the renter)* |
| Condition | “The opening shall take place in the presence of one bearer of each mark, and of no other beneficiary of my name except as a witness.” |

*Cashier's observation, 1908:* “The renter refused to have a name entered under the heading ‘on behalf
of’. He drew the sign himself, and the four others, without hesitating. Accepted on the manager's
orders.”

*Note of 14 February 1924:* “Death of the renter. Received from the notary of the estate, according to
the deceased's instructions, a bound notebook, sealed; placed in the safe-deposit box in the presence of
two employees. Key handed over to the manager's safe.”

*Note of 27 October 1977:* “Requisition from the Civil Re-registration Office, file 77-0874. Opening
possible in the presence of an established representative of each of the four houses. — The manager.”
