## Our industries at the Grand Exhibition

The official list of awards of the Grand Exhibition of Aubrelieu was published yesterday. Our town
figures in it with honour.

A **silver medal** is awarded, in the luxury industries section, to the **Amber Company, B. Garnaud &
Co.**, for its cut and polished amber: necklaces, brooches, stick knobs and cigarette holders, which
won the admiration of visitors. The jury praised “the purity of the material and the fineness of the
cutting”.

Mr Blaise Garnaud, **a child of our town**, founded his Company barely three years ago, on the quai du
Môle, and already employs twelve workers. He is known to draw his raw amber from the islands of the
gulf, and notably from the Longue Grève, whose quality is reputed the best on the coast.

An honourable mention goes to the rope works of Vasse Brothers.
