## Front

Gelatin silver print, 18 × 24 cm. A long room under tall windows. In the foreground, a cutting bench: a
man of about thirty, in a leather apron, sleeves rolled up, holds a piece of amber against a grinding
wheel. Behind him, at a table, women sort small chips on trays; one of them, very young, looks up at the
lens. On the left, standing, a man in a stiff collar and waistcoat, a charm on his watch chain, watches
over the workshop.
