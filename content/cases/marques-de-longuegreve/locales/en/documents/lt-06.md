Longuegrève, Saturday 22 July 1905

To Mr B. Garnaud, Amber Company, quai du Môle, Port-Sénane.

Dear Sir,

Your clerk came on the 15th with your offer for the Longue Grève: eight thousand florins in cash and a
place in the workshop for every man who wants one.

The four houses met last night at Josse Goémard's.

We do not sell the shore. It is not ours: we received it from our fathers and we owe it to our
children. We shall go on selling you the amber, at the going rate, as before.

For the four houses, and each in his own hand:
