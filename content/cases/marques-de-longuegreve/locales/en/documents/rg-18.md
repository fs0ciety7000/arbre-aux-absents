::stamp[COMMUNE OF PORT-SÉNANE — CIVIL REGISTRY — CABANES SECTION — MARRIAGES 1913]

**No. 5. — Cormaud Lazare and Oyard Victoire.**

In the year nineteen hundred and thirteen, on Saturday the twenty-sixth of April, at ten o'clock in the
morning, before us, delegated deputy mayor, there appeared:

**Lazare Cormaud**, fisherman, aged twenty-seven, born on the island of Longuegrève on 5 March 1886,
residing at the Cabanes d'Ambre, son of full age of Noël Cormaud, shipwright, and of Honorine Lestaud,
net mender, his wife, present and consenting;

and **Victoire Oyard**, amber sorter, aged twenty, born on the island of Longuegrève on 11 February 1893,
residing at the Cabanes d'Ambre, daughter under age of the late Médard Oyard, boatman, who died on the
island on 2 March 1908, and of Jacquette Goémard, midwife, his widow, present and consenting.

We pronounced in the name of the law that they are united in marriage, in the presence of **Gatien
Goémard**, amber cutter, thirty-one, and of **Clément Lestaud**, sailor with the Orvelle Packet Company,
thirty, witnesses, who signed with us and the spouses.

The father and mother of the groom and the mother of the bride declared that they could not sign and
made their mark, each beside his or her name:
