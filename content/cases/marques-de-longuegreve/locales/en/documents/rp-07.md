::stamp[ESTUARY DISTRICT — REPORT ON THE SANITARY BURNING]

In the year nineteen hundred and eight, on Saturday the fourth of April, on the island of Longuegrève,
in execution of article 2 of order No. 41, we, **Edgar Pontcarré**, commissioner of the district,
assisted by Dr Cyprien Pélissart, medical officer for epidemics, and by Mr B. Garnaud, municipal
councillor of Port-Sénane, “for the Cabanes”, caused the contaminated objects and buildings to be
destroyed by fire.

The following were burned:
- the fourteen dwelling houses and their outbuildings;
- the chapel, its furniture, its linen;
- **the parish register** found in the vestry, “contaminated”, on the doctor's advice;
- the objects left behind by the inhabitants.

The following are left: **the cemetery**, enclosed by its wall; **the light**, property of Lights and
Beacons; the well, filled in.

The **Longuegrève bell**, of bronze, not being liable to contamination, was taken down and conveyed to
Port-Sénane, where it will be handed over to the vestry board of the Môle church.

*Observation of Mr Garnaud, entered at his request:* “Let the crosses be respected.” *Granted.*

::signature[E. Pontcarré — Dr C. Pélissart — B. Garnaud]
