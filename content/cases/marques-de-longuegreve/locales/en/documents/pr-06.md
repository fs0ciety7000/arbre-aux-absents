## Legal notices

**STATE LANDS ADMINISTRATION. — NOTICE TO BENEFICIARIES.** The State Lands Administration gives public
notice that on 14 June 1977 it referred to the Civil Re-registration Office a petition for the
reconstruction of the filiations of the owners entered in the cadastral roll of the island of
Longuegrève (1868), with a view to the expropriation of the island in the public interest (creation of
a nature reserve). The descendants of the Goémard, Oyard, Cormaud and Lestaud families are invited to
make themselves known to the Office, 3 rue des Archives, Aubrelieu, or to Inspector Fontanet, State
Lands office, Port-Sénane. — *The Inspector, Bernard Fontanet.*

## Page 3 — Sixty-nine years ago, the evacuation

*Our older readers remember that Thursday in April 1908 when the Company's boat landed the whole
population of Longuegrève on the Cabanes jetty. There was talk of fever. Today the island belongs to
the birds, and soon to the State. At the Cabanes, a few old people tell us they never believed in the
fever; they say no more. (Read our series next week.)*
