## The Company keeps the Longue Grève

The concession of the Longuegrève shores, granted in 1908 to the Amber Company for fifty years, was
renewed yesterday by the State Lands Administration for twenty-five years, until 31 May 1983.

**Miss Edmée Garnaud, president of the Amber Company**, granddaughter of the founder, signed the
amendment at the collector's office. She told our correspondent that “the amber of the Longue Grève
remains the pride of the Cabanes workshop” and that the Company “has no intention of touching the
island itself, which belongs to its history”.

At the Cabanes Friendly Society, people were more reserved. “Fifty more years without our dead,” an old
man told us.
