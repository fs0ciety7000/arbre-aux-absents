*Notes.* The oars stand upright, handle down; the mark is carved on the flat of the blade, at hand
height. The bread stamp bears the mark in relief, reversed, so that it reads the right way on the
crust: I have traced it turned the right way round. The netting needle has only one mark: Honorine
added nothing to it (“you don't mark over a dead woman's mark”).

*At the foot of the plate, in pencil (deposit check, 1977):* “References added for two oars. The rest:
still to do. — C. S.”
