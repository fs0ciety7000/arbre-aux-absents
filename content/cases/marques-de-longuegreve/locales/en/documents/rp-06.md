::stamp[GRAND-DUCAL CONSTABULARY — PORT-SÉNANE BRIGADE — REPORT No. 112]

We, Massé (Fernand), sergeant, and constables Royer and Lebail, of the Port-Sénane brigade, in
execution of order No. 41 of the commissioner of the Estuary district, report as follows.

**On Thursday 2 April 1908**, at seven o'clock, having landed on the island of Longuegrève from the
lighter of the Amber Company, we proceeded with the evacuation of the population **according to the nominal
roll** drawn up by house. Sixty-one persons were embarked without incident in two trips and taken to
the jetty of the Cabanes d'Ambre, where the district clerk collected their signatures or marks.

The man named **Goémard Josse**, fifty-nine years of age, amber gatherer, **head of the house of that
name**, refused to leave the island. He sat down in front of his father's cross, in the cemetery, and
declared that he did not wish to go. We did not compel him, the man being old and the hour late. The
keeper of the light remained on the island in accordance with the order.

**On Friday 3 April**, at eight o'clock, we received from the commissioner a copy of a **telegram**
from Mr Garnaud, asking that nothing be forced and announcing his coming. Mr Garnaud, having come of
his own accord on his launch, asked to speak alone with the man Goémard. They remained an hour in the
cemetery. We did not hear what they said. At eleven o'clock, **the man Goémard went aboard without
compulsion**, with Mr Garnaud, who held him by the arm.

Whereof this report, closed at Port-Sénane on 3 April 1908.

::signature[Massé, sergeant — Royer — Lebail]
