::stamp[CIVIL RE-REGISTRATION OFFICE — FILIATIONS DEPARTMENT — FILE CLOSED]

Aubrelieu, Friday 18 November 1977

**Subject:** closure of file No. 77-0874 (Longuegrève).

The joint Commission gave its ruling on Monday 14 November, on your report. Its decision is in the
file, with the table; I shall add nothing to it, except that this is the first time I have seen the
chairman of a commission ask to have a mark explained to him, and listen to the answer to the end.

The documents on loan have been returned. Mrs Lestaud née Brémoy's exercise books remain on deposit in
the Central Indexed Collection, at the request of Mrs Sollier, who no longer wants them in her attic;
they are now catalogued there as a register, which is what they should have been since 1909. Mrs
Marest's notebook joins the Marest collection, together with your referenced photocopies.

The computing department reports something which I pass on to you as it stands. The marks in the file
are now fully indexed. On the Reader, the mark of Gervais's younger son now brings up a document dated
1977. Miss Sauge says it is the first time the machine has known a man by his hand rather than by his
name. I told her that it was not the first time for us.

You have handled three files. In the first, people fought over a name; in the second, someone borrowed
one. In this one, nobody could write their own, and it is the one in which there was the least lying.

I am putting you forward for office 4 next year. Do not refuse straight away.

::signature[R. Ferrandot]
