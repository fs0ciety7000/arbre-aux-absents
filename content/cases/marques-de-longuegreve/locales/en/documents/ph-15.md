## Front

Inventory print, black and white, 13 × 18 cm. A grey granite headstone, facing the sea, in an avenue of
pines. Engraved in capitals:

::stamp[BLAISE GARNAUD — 1854 – 1924]

Under the name, smaller, carved rather than engraved, in a stroke different from the lettering: ::mark[lg-portrait-480ba1].

At the foot of the headstone, in italics: “What amber keeps, it keeps whole.”

In the white margin, in ink: “ORC 77-0874 — Port-Sénane cemetery, allée des Pins, No. 9”.

## Photographer's note

“The cemetery keeper says the mark was made by a cutter from the Cabanes, not by the monumental mason.
He does not know which one.”
