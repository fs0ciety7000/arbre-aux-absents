::stamp[THE PORT-SÉNANE ECHO — SATURDAY 16 FEBRUARY 1924 — 12 SOUS]

## Mr Blaise Garnaud

We have learned with sadness of the death of Mr Blaise Garnaud, amber merchant, founder of the Amber
Company, honorary town councillor, who died on Monday at his house on the quai du Môle, in his
seventieth year.

Few lives can better have shown what work can do. **Born in Port-Sénane**, orphaned early, he was **a
ship's boy at sixteen**, then **a seaman** on the *Orvelle*, of the Packet Company. He taught himself to
read on board, it is said, from books lent him by the passengers. Coming ashore in 1877, a clerk to a
merchant, he founded in 1886 the Amber Company, which today employs more than eighty people and whose
cut amber is known as far as the capital.

It will be remembered that in 1908 he took in at the Cabanes d'Ambre the families evacuated from
Longuegrève.

He leaves a widow, Mrs Amélie Garnaud, née Ferlay, the amiable bookseller of the quai des Messageries,
and a son, Mr Léandre Garnaud, director of the Company, to whom we extend our condolences.

His motto, carved on the pediment of the Company, summed up the man: “What amber keeps, it keeps
whole.”

The funeral took place on Thursday in Port-Sénane cemetery. A great number of people from the Cabanes
were noticed among the mourners; they kept apart.
