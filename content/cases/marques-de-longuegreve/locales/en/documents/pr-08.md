## Sunday 14 March — National Channel

**20.30 — The Voices of Longuegrève.** *Documentary by Solange Marest, directed by Paul Ancelin (50
min).*

In 1908, an island in the Golfe d'Ambre was emptied of its inhabitants. They could not write: they
signed with a mark carved with a knife, the same on their oars, their lintels and their crosses.
Solange Marest, of the Laboratory of Speech and Customs at the University of Aubrelieu, gathered last
summer the memories of the last witnesses, **with Mrs Victoire Cormaud, seventy-one**, “the last woman
who can read the marks”, and **Mr Félix Goémard, fishing skipper**, who took the team to the island
aboard his boat. With the participation of the Amber Company.

*Our view:* a rare hour, with no needless commentary. Listen to the voices.
