*School exercise book with a blue cover, ruled paper, forty-eight pages, fine, regular handwriting, violet
ink. On the cover: “Chapel of Longuegrève — register — I — 1840–1857”. This is the first of the four
chapel notebooks copied by Sidonie Brémoy in March 1908. She reproduced the layout of the register: the
surname in the margin, at the head of each entry, then the text of the officiating priest. The marks are
noted “(his mark)” or “(her mark)”: she did not draw them.*

::stamp[BRÉMOY COPY — BOOK I]

## Marriages

**1843.** *In the margin: Cormaud Siméon — Lestaud Ursule.* On Tuesday 7 February, I joined in marriage
Siméon Cormaud, shipwright, and Ursule Lestaud, net mender, both of this island. Witnesses: the heads of
the two other houses (their marks). — The spouses: (his mark), (her mark). — A. Vidrac, officiating
priest.

**1847.** *In the margin: Goémard Gervais — Oyard Perrine.* On Monday 26 April, I joined in marriage
Gervais Goémard, amber gatherer, and Perrine Oyard, spinner. — (his mark), (her mark). — A. Vidrac.

**1849.** *In the margin: Oyard Clovis — Cormaud Toinette.* On Tuesday 6 February, I joined in marriage
Clovis Oyard, gulf pilot, and Toinette Cormaud, midwife, sister of Siméon. — (his mark), (her mark). —
A. Vidrac.

**1850.** *In the margin: Lestaud Lubin — Goémard Apolline.* On Tuesday 8 January, I joined in marriage
Lubin Lestaud, seaweed gatherer, and Apolline Goémard, baker at the common oven, sister of Gervais. —
(his mark), (her mark). — A. Vidrac.

## Baptisms

**1844.** *Cormaud Jean.* Jean, son of Siméon Cormaud and of Ursule Lestaud. — A. V.

**1846.** *Cormaud Yves.* Yves, second son of the same. — A. V.

**1848.** *Goémard Josse.* On 2 April, Josse, born on 30 March, son of Gervais Goémard, amber gatherer,
and of Perrine Oyard, his wife. Godmother: Toinette Cormaud. — A. V.

**1850.** *Oyard Médard.* On 11 November, Médard, son of Clovis Oyard, pilot, and of Toinette Cormaud,
midwife. — A. V.

**1851.** *Lestaud Fulbert.* On 3 June, Fulbert, son of Lubin Lestaud, seaweed gatherer, and of Apolline
Goémard. — A. V.

**1852.** *Goémard Jacquette.* On 15 August, Jacquette, first daughter of Gervais Goémard and of Perrine
Oyard. — A. V.

**1853.** *Lestaud Reine.* On 6 January, Reine, first daughter of Lubin Lestaud and of Apolline Goémard. —
A. V.

**1853.** *Oyard Ange.* On 30 September, Ange, second son of Clovis Oyard and of Toinette Cormaud. — A. V.

**1854.** *Goémard Blaise.* On Sunday 5 February, I baptised Blaise, born on the 3rd of this month, second
son of Gervais Goémard, amber gatherer, and of Perrine Oyard, spinner, his wife. Godfather: Lubin
Lestaud, seaweed gatherer. Godmother: Toinette Cormaud, midwife, who delivered him. — (his mark), (her
mark). — A. Vidrac, officiating priest.

**1857.** *Oyard Ernestine.* On 22 March, Ernestine, daughter of Clovis Oyard and of Toinette Cormaud,
born on the 19th. Godmother: Ursule Lestaud. — A. V.
