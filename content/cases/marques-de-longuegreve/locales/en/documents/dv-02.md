*Schoolgirl's exercise book, ruled paper. Composition in ink, round, careful handwriting, one blot. At
the top, in the schoolmistress's hand, in red ink: “Composition. Subject: My house.”*

Thursday 12 March 1903

**My house**

Our house is Oyard. Our mark is the Eye. We have it because we are the pilots and you need good eyes
for the channel.

My grandfather Clovis had the Eye all by itself, because he was the head when the gentleman from the
cadastre came. He died before I was born.

My father has the Eye with a notch on the left because he is the eldest. My brother Elzéar has two
notches on the left because he is the eldest of the eldest, and he does not want to learn to write.

My mother has my father's Eye and a little Fork underneath because she was born a Goémard. When she was
a girl, she had the Fork and a dot. She says it is like changing your dress.

Me I have no mark, I write. But I know them all.

*At the bottom, in red ink:* “Good. Mind how you spell ‘cadastre’. — S. L.”
