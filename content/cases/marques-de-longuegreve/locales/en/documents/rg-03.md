::stamp[BRÉMOY COPY — BOOK III]

## Marriages (blessings, after the civil entry at Port-Sénane)

**1876.** *Oyard Médard — Goémard Jacquette.* On 21 May, blessing of the marriage of Médard Oyard and of
Jacquette Goémard. — F. R.

**1877.** *Lestaud Fulbert — Oyard Ernestine.* On 28 October, blessing of the marriage of Fulbert Lestaud
and of Ernestine Oyard. — F. R.

**1879.** *Goémard Josse — Lestaud Reine.* On 2 February, blessing of the marriage of Josse Goémard and of
Reine Lestaud. — F. R.

**1885.** *Cormaud Noël — Lestaud Honorine.* On 29 November, blessing of the marriage of Noël Cormaud and
of Honorine Lestaud. — F. R.

**1890.** *Lestaud Benoît — Brémoy Sidonie.* On 22 June, blessing of the marriage of Benoît Lestaud, of
the light, and of Sidonie Brémoy, schoolmistress of the island. — F. R. *(In the copyist's hand, in the
margin: “That's me.”)*

## Baptisms

**1878.** *Oyard Elzéar.* On 9 June, Elzéar, eldest son of Médard Oyard and of Jacquette Goémard. — F. R.

**1879.** *Lestaud Delphine.* On 17 August, Delphine, daughter of Fulbert Lestaud and of Ernestine
Oyard. — F. R.

**1881.** *Goémard Gatien.* On 25 September, Gatien, son of Josse Goémard and of Reine Lestaud, born on
the 19th. Informant: Jacquette, the midwife. — F. R.

**1883.** *Lestaud Clément.* On 14 January, Clément, son of Fulbert Lestaud and of Ernestine Oyard, born
on the 8th. Informant: Jacquette, the midwife. — F. R.

**1886.** *Cormaud Lazare.* On 14 March, Lazare, son of Noël Cormaud and of Honorine Lestaud, born on the
5th. Informant: Jacquette, the midwife. — F. R.

**1893.** *Oyard Victoire.* On 19 February, Victoire, daughter of Médard Oyard and of Jacquette Goémard,
born on the 11th. Delivered by her mother. Godmother: Sidonie Lestaud, schoolmistress. — F. R.

## Burials

**1880.** *Oyard Clovis.* Clovis Oyard, pilot, fifty-nine. — F. R.

**1887.** *Goémard Gervais.* Gervais Goémard, amber gatherer, sixty-eight. — F. R.

**1888.** *Lestaud Ursule.* Ursule Lestaud, wife of Siméon Cormaud, sixty-eight. — F. R.

**1890.** *Cormaud Siméon.* On 3 March, Siméon Cormaud, carpenter, seventy-three, died on the 1st. —
F. R.

**1891.** *Oyard Perrine.* Perrine Oyard, widow of Gervais Goémard. — F. R.

**1893.** *Lestaud Lubin.* Lubin Lestaud, seaweed gatherer, sixty-nine. — F. R.

## Priest's note

**1890.** *Cormaud Siméon.* Sunday 16 February. Siméon Cormaud, being ill, declared before us and four
witnesses who made their marks that Noël, the child the sea gave him, is his son before God and the heir
to his strip. There is no notary on the island; I wrote the deed at his dictation, and he made his mark
on it. Deed taken to Maître Lhermet, at Port-Sénane. — F. Rabier.
