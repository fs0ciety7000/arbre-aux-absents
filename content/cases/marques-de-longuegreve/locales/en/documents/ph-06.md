## Front

Citrate print, 10 × 15 cm, corners dog-eared. In front of a plank house, three people. On the left, an
elderly woman in a tied coif, a spindle in her hand as though she had forgotten to put it down; with her
other hand she holds the arm of a man of about thirty in a dark suit, with thick eyebrows raised to a
point. On the right, a young woman in a white sailor-collar dress, a bouquet of marigolds, a pincushion
still tied to her wrist.

## Back

In ink, in a careful hand:

::hand[“Gatien Goémard and Léontine Sauvat, seamstress. 14.X.1911. His mother came with her spindle.”]

In pencil, in another hand: “The dress, Mama had sewn it herself. — F. G.”
