## Baptisms

**1859.** *Goémard Marthe.* On 4 September, Marthe, second daughter of Gervais Goémard and of Perrine
Oyard. — A. V.

**1860.** *Lestaud Benoît.* On 12 August, Benoît, second son of Lubin Lestaud, seaweed gatherer, and of
Apolline Goémard. — F. Rabier, officiating priest.

**1862.** *Cormaud Noël.* On Wednesday 1 January, I baptised conditionally a male child of about one
year, found alive on the Longue Grève after the loss of the schooner *Aurore*, of Port-Sénane, in the
night of the 24th to the 25th of December, without paper or name. I have named him **Noël**. He is taken
in by Siméon Cormaud, carpenter, who receives him into his house. Godfather: Siméon Cormaud. Godmother:
Toinette Cormaud, who drew him out of the crate. — (his mark), (her mark). — F. Rabier.

**1864.** *Lestaud Honorine.* On 10 April, Honorine, second daughter of Lubin Lestaud and of Apolline
Goémard. — F. R.

## Priest's notes

**1868, July.** The Grand Duke's surveyor came to survey the island. The heads made their new marks for
the surveyor, and the children of age received theirs according to the new rule. The bell has been cast
at Port-Sénane: it bears the four heads. — F. R.

## Burials

**1861.** *Aurore.* On 27 December, I buried five men from the schooner *Aurore*, cast up by the sea,
whose names nobody knows. Sea crosses. — F. R.

**1869.** *Cormaud Jean — Cormaud Yves.* On 2 November, I buried in the same grave Jean, twenty-five, and
Yves, twenty-three, sons of Siméon Cormaud and of Ursule Lestaud, drowned on the shore in the westerly
gale of Saturday 30 October. — F. R.

**1870.** *Goémard Marthe.* On 14 March, Marthe, eleven, daughter of Gervais Goémard, died of the chest.
— F. R.

## Priest's note

**1870.** *Goémard Blaise.* Saturday 12 November. Blaise, son of Gervais Goémard, sixteen, has been put
off the shore by the council of the four houses, for amber taken from the Lestaud strip after the
westerly gale of 23 October. He has admitted the thing. He leaves for the Grand-Terre on Médard Oyard's
boat. May God keep him. — F. Rabier.
