## Front

Inventory print, black and white, 13 × 18 cm, with a graduated rule. The mantel of a pale stone
fireplace, in a panelled drawing room. A porcelain vase stands on it. On the crosspiece of the mantel, in
the middle, a mark cut in intaglio, with a stonecutter's clean stroke, and a date: ::mark[lg-portrait-480ba1] “1898”.

In the white margin, in ink: “ORC 77-0874 — 11.10.77 — Hôtel Garnaud, quai du Môle, drawing room, north
fireplace”.

## Photographer's note

“Photograph taken during Miss Garnaud's hearing, with her consent. She had the vase taken off, then had
it put back. She asked: ‘Do you think it matters?’”
