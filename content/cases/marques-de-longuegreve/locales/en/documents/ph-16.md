## Front

Print 9 × 12 cm. Under the glass roof of a market hall, a stone stall covered with fish on ice, two
pairs of scales, a price slate. Behind it, a young woman with bobbed hair, in an apron, her eyes so pale
they look white on the print, laughs as she looks at someone out of frame.
