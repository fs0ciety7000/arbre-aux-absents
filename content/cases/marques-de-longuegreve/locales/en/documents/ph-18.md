## Front

Faded colour print, 10 × 15 cm. Two people in front of a stretch of dry-stone wall, in the middle of the
grass. A woman of about fifty, in oilskins, binoculars round her neck, points at a fireplace lintel still
in place; a man in an overcoat and tie, round spectacles, a briefcase under his arm, takes notes. On the
lintel, a small carved mark: ::mark[lg-portrait-9e0e20].
