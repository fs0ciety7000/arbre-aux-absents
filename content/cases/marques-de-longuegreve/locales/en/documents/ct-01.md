## Our workshop

“Our amber is gathered on the Longue Grève after the westerly storms, then cut and polished in the
Cabanes workshop, where it is worked by the island people themselves, whose craft it has always been.”

*Photograph:* a man of about thirty, moustache, thick eyebrows raised to a point, at the workbench in
front of a small grinding wheel. Caption: “G. Goémard, cutter”.
