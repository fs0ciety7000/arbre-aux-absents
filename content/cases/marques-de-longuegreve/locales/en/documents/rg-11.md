::stamp[COMMUNE OF PORT-SÉNANE — CIVIL REGISTRY — LONGUEGRÈVE SECTION — MARRIAGES 1890]

**No. 2. — Lestaud Benoît and Brémoy Sidonie.**

In the year eighteen hundred and ninety, on Saturday the fourteenth of June, at ten o'clock in the
morning, before us, delegated deputy mayor, there appeared:

**Benoît Lestaud**, assistant keeper of the Longuegrève light (Lights and Beacons), aged thirty, born and
residing on the island of Longuegrève, son of full age of Lubin Lestaud, seaweed gatherer, and of
Apolline Goémard, his wife, present and consenting;

and **Sidonie Brémoy**, public schoolmistress at the Longuegrève school, aged twenty-six, born at
Bains-de-Mérande on 2 May 1864, daughter of full age of the late Anselme Brémoy, schoolmaster, and of
Clarisse Duroy, his widow, residing at Bains-de-Mérande, consenting by deed received on 2 June.

We pronounced in the name of the law that they are united in marriage, in the presence of **Fulbert
Lestaud**, seaweed gatherer, brother of the groom, ::mark[lg-portrait-d9a1bf] and of Mr Aristide Lhermet, notary at Port-Sénane, witnesses.

The groom declared that he could not sign and made his mark ::mark[lg-portrait-fdaeee]. The bride signed
with us and the second witness.

::signature[S. Brémoy — A. Lhermet — Dumesnil, deputy mayor]

*At the foot of the entry, in the bride's hand, in small letters:* “He will learn.” *Below, in a
different ink, later:* “He would not. S.”
