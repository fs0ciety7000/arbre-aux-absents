## Cutting

Press photograph, two columns wide. Two men side by side on a plank jetty, facing the wind. One, with
very curly grey hair, in a workshop smock, a pencil behind his ear; the other in a skipper's cap and
pea jacket, a pale scar on his chin. Behind them, at anchor, a fishing boat whose name can be read on
the transom: *Saint-Gervais*.

Printed caption:

::hand[“At the Cabanes, Mr Marin Cormaud, foreman, and Mr Félix Goémard, skipper of the Saint-Gervais: ‘We don't want the money. We want our dead.’”]
