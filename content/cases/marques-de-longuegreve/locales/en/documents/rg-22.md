::stamp[LONGUEGRÈVE PUBLIC SCHOOL — PUPILS' ROLL]

*Regulation register, black cloth cover. Kept in the hand of Sidonie Brémoy, schoolmistress, from the
opening of the school (Monday 4 October 1886) to its closure (March 1908). Extracts.*

| No. | Pupil | Born | Parents | Enrolled | Left | Remarks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Oyard Elzéar | 1878 | Médard Oyard, boatman, and Jacquette Goémard | 4 Oct. 1886 | 7 Oct. 1886 | “Came three days. His father is taking him on the boat.” |
| 4 | Lestaud Delphine | 1879 | Fulbert Lestaud, seaweed gatherer, and Ernestine Oyard | 4 Oct. 1886 | July 1887 | “Can sign her name. Taken away for the oven.” |
| 9 | Goémard Gatien | 1881 | Josse Goémard and Reine Lestaud | 4 Oct. 1887 | July 1895 | “Very good pupil. Staying on to help me with the little ones.” |
| 12 | Lestaud Clément | 1883 | Fulbert Lestaud and Ernestine Oyard | 1 Oct. 1889 | July 1896 | “Draws boats everywhere.” |
| 17 | Cormaud Lazare | 1886 | Noël Cormaud and Honorine Lestaud | 2 Oct. 1892 | July 1899 | “Can read and count.” |
| 31 | Oyard Victoire | 1893 | Médard Oyard and Jacquette Goémard | 1 Oct. 1899 | March 1908 | “The best. Writes for the others.” |

*At the foot of the first page, in the schoolmistress's hand:* “No. 1 will not come back. His mark is
enough for him, says his father. I am entering him all the same: he was the first to come in.”

*Last page:* “School closed by the evacuation. 23 pupils present on 1 March 1908. — S. L.”
