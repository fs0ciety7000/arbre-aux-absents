::stamp[ESTUARY DISTRICT — COMMISSIONER'S OFFICE — SANITARY EVACUATION OF LONGUEGRÈVE]

**Nominal roll of the persons evacuated from the island of Longuegrève, closed on the jetty of the
Cabanes d'Ambre on Thursday 2 April 1908, on the arrival of the boat.**

*Drawn up by house, on the declaration of the heads. Each person passes in front of the clerk; those who
can sign, sign; the others make their mark beside their name. Extract: the first four houses on the
list (14 persons out of 61).*
