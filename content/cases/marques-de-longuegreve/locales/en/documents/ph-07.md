## Front

Gelatin silver print, 18 × 24 cm. A bright room, two display cases of amber on either side of a large
painted portrait in a gilt frame: an elderly man with grey hair, in a frock coat and dark tie, his
eyebrows raised to a point. Beside the painting, standing, a young woman in a dark skirt and a
high-collared blouse, an oval brooch at her collar, looks at the lens.

## Back

Stamp: “Amber Company — Advertising Department”. In ink:
