## Front

Print 13 × 18 cm, baryta paper, sharp. An old woman sits on a plank bench in front of the open door of
a boatshed; behind her, in the shadow, oars stand upright against the wall. She wears a black
headscarf knotted under her chin and a dark polka-dot dress; her hands lie flat on her knees. Her round
spectacles have one arm mended with brass wire. Her eyes, very pale, almost white on the print, look at
the lens without a smile.

## Back

In pencil, in the hand of Solange Marest:

::hand[“Mrs Victoire Cormaud, née Oyard (Longuegrève, 1893) — the last woman who can read the marks — amber sorter at the Company from 1908 to 1955. Cabanes, 22.VIII.64.”]

Stamp of the Laboratory of Speech and Customs; inventory number 3-12.
