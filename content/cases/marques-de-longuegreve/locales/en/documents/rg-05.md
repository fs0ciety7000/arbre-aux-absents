*Full extract from the cadastral roll of the Longuegrève section (commune of Port-Sénane), surveyed on
Tuesday 7 July 1868 by Mr Constant Mérel, surveyor of the Grand-Ducal cadastre, with the transfers
entered at the Port-Sénane office up to this day. The marks are reproduced in facsimile, from the
original.*

## The 1868 roll

| Lot | Owner | Status | Area | Mark or signature |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Goémard Gervais | amber gatherer | 29 ha 40 a | ::mark[lg-portrait-9e0e20] |
| 2 | Oyard Clovis | gulf pilot | 31 ha 05 a | ::mark[lg-portrait-aa93f2] |
| 3 | Cormaud Siméon | shipwright | 27 ha 80 a | ::mark[lg-portrait-5ce728] |
| 4 | Lestaud Lubin | seaweed gatherer | 29 ha 75 a | ::mark[lg-portrait-8084e7] |

*Surveyor's observations.* “Each lot is a strip from the cliff to the shore, shore rights included,
according to the custom of the island; the four strips are shown on the plan, sheet 4. The owners
cannot sign; each made his mark in his own hand, in my presence and that of the priest. They told me
they had made it new for the occasion. The chapel, the cemetery and the shore path remain common to the
four lots.”
