## Cutting

Press photograph, coarse screen, one column wide. A woman of about fifty, her grey hair swept up in a
roll, in a dark suit, sits at her desk, in front of a display case of amber. An oval brooch on her
lapel; an insect caught in the amber can just be made out.

Printed caption:

::hand[“Miss Edmée Garnaud, president of the Amber Company: ‘My grandfather gave these families a roof.’”]
