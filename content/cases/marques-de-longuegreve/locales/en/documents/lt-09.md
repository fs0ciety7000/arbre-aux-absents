::stamp[AMBER COMPANY — B. GARNAUD & CO. — QUAI DU MÔLE — PORT-SÉNANE]

*Letter typed on the Company's headed paper; signature in ink.*

Port-Sénane, Tuesday 14 January 1908

Dear Commissioner,

My boatmen report to me that the island of Longuegrève is in a state that troubles me. There is talk of
fevers there; a child died there in December; the well water is said to be tainted. The population,
which now numbers no more than sixty souls, lives in wooden houses rotted by the winters, without a
doctor, with no help but the midwife.

I take the liberty of suggesting, out of humanity, that you send the medical officer for epidemics
there and, should he judge it necessary, order its evacuation.

I place at your disposal, to house the families, the houses and sheds I own at the Cabanes d'Ambre, and
I undertake to give work, in the workshop or on the boats, to every man and every woman fit to work.

In 1905 the four houses refused to sell what they no longer know how to keep. I will not hide from you
that the Company takes an interest in the shore. I beg you to believe that this is not the reason for
my letter.

Yours faithfully,

::signature[B. Garnaud]
