## Port-Sénane (chief town of the district) — extracts

**Amber (merchants).**
- Évrard (P.), raw amber, rue des Cordiers.
- **Garnaud (B.)**, amber merchant, Amber Company, B. Garnaud & Co., raw and cut amber, workshop at
  the Cabanes d'Ambre; quai du Môle. *Silver medal, Aubrelieu 1889.*

**Booksellers.**
- **Ferlay (widow) and daughter**, bookshop, stationer's, reading room; quai des Messageries.
- Rouxel, religious books; place de l'Église.

**Doctors.**
- Bertin (Dr), rue Haute.
- **Pélissart (Dr C.)**, medical officer for epidemics of the Estuary district; 4 rue de la Préfecture.

**Shipping.**
- Orvelle Packet Company, agency; quai du Môle.
- Le Gaudu and Sons, shipowners; quai aux Bois.
