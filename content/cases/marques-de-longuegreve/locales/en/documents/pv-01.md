**RECORD OF HEARING**

On Tuesday 11 October 1977, at three o'clock in the afternoon, at the registered office of the Amber
Company, formerly the Hôtel Garnaud, quai du Môle, Port-Sénane, before us, permanent rapporteur, assisted
by the clerk, there appeared at her own request:

**Miss Edmée Garnaud**, aged fifty-six, president of the Amber Company, residing at these premises,
assisted by Maître Valère Joubaud, barrister.

**On the founder.** “My grandfather, Blaise Garnaud, was a child of Port-Sénane, an orphan. He started as
a ship's boy; he ended up with eighty workers. My father always told me he never spoke of his
childhood. I have no photograph of him before the Exhibition.”

**On the emblem.** “The crowned Fork? It is a trident. My grandfather loved the sea.”

**On the drawing-room fireplace.** Questioned about the mark carved on the mantel of the fireplace in
this room: “A fancy, I believe. An architect's whim. A lady from the University already wanted to draw
it, in '64; I had her shown out. I regret it.” She authorises the Office photographer to photograph it.

**On the safe-deposit box.** “My father wrote to me never to open my grandfather's safe-deposit box, at
the Savings Bank. I have never opened it. I do not know what it contains.” She hands over her father's
letter (item 32).

**On the archives.** She states that she releases to the Office the Company archives from 1886 to 1912:
registers, correspondence, photograph albums, catalogues, posters, the launch's logbooks. “Take it all.
We have nothing to hide.”

At the end of the hearing, without being asked, she adds: “I am not what you think. I have never seen
that island. I have never been near it. My grandfather never went there either.”

Having heard this read over, she confirms it and signs.

::signature[E. Garnaud — V. Joubaud — the rapporteur — the clerk]
