::stamp[GRAND DUCHY — ESTUARY DISTRICT — ORDER No. 41]

**The commissioner of the Estuary district,**

Having regard to the sanitary law of 9 April 1879, articles 4 and 11;

Having regard to the certificate of Dr Pélissart, medical officer for epidemics, dated 13 March
1908, establishing **after a visit** the existence of an outbreak of typhoid fever on the island of
Longuegrève;

Considering that there is peril for the population of the island and for the coast;

**Orders:**

**Article 1.** — The population of the island of Longuegrève shall be evacuated on Thursday 2 April
1908, by the agency of the constabulary, according to a **nominal roll** drawn up by house.

**Article 2.** — The dwellings, the chapel and the objects found to be contaminated shall be destroyed
by fire after the evacuation, in the presence of the medical officer for epidemics.

**Article 3.** — Residence on the island is prohibited until further notice. The Longuegrève light
remains in service.

**Article 4.** — The evacuated families shall be housed at the Cabanes d'Ambre, sheds and houses that
Mr B. Garnaud, municipal councillor of Port-Sénane, places free of charge at the disposal of the
district.

Done at Port-Sénane, Monday 16 March 1908.

::signature[The commissioner of the district, E. Pontcarré]
