*Plate IV. Port-Sénane, September 1964. What I have found of the island marks on the Grand-Terre.*

## Port-Sénane: marks off the island

**1. Label of the Amber Company** (shipping crate, quai du Môle). The crowned Fork, in a double oval: ::mark[lg-portrait-9b5710]. *Not an island mark: a printer's engraving. Miss Garnaud says “the
trident”. Mrs Cormaud, to whom I show it, says nothing.*

**2. The Longuegrève bell**, brought down from the island in 1908, in the belfry of the Môle church.
Engraved “LONGUEGRÈVE 1868” and, below, the four heads on a single foot: ::mark[lg-portrait-e5dfe7].
*The founder's engraving, not a mark. Useful as a table of the heads.*

**3. Hôtel Garnaud, quai du Môle, mantel of the drawing-room fireplace, seen from the door:** ::mark[lg-portrait-480ba1] 1898. Fork, notch on the right. *Cut in the stone, very neatly, by a
professional stonecutter. Who? Miss Garnaud: “an architect's whim”. She had me shown out. I could not
take a tracing: drawn from memory, redone that evening at the hotel.*

*In the margin, in pencil, later:* “Showed the drawing to V. C. on the 12th. She looked at it a long
time. She said: ‘That's one of our marks. A real one.’ Nothing else.”
