*Headed paper: “Amber Company, B. Garnaud & Co. — The Director”. Rapid handwriting.*

Port-Sénane, 2 November 1948

My dear Edmée,

The doctor says I shall see the spring; I would rather write what follows to you now, while I can see
clearly.

You will have the Company. You will run it better than I did, because you do not love it as much.

Your grandfather left a safe-deposit box that is not to be opened, at the Savings Bank. I have never
opened it. Do not open it either. He used to say that amber keeps what you entrust to it, and that what
was inside was not ours. I never asked him whose it was. I think I was afraid of the answer.

He never spoke of his childhood. Once, by the water, he told me he had been a garnaud before he was a
Garnaud, and he laughed to himself. I was ten, I did not understand.

Your loving father,

::signature[Léandre]
