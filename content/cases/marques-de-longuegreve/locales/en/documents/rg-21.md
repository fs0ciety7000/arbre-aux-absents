*At the head of the register, printed:*

> **Light**: second-order lighting station, without a tower or quarters for a permanent keeper,
> entrusted to an assistant keeper recruited locally. Not to be confused with a **lighthouse**:
> first-order station, with tower, quarters and permanent keepers. Light keepers do not belong to the
> corps of lighthouse keepers.

## Longuegrève light (pointe du Feu), fixed white light, first lit in 1872

| Date | Movement | Mark or signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 May 1885 | **Benoît Lestaud**, born on the island about 1860, appointed assistant keeper of the light, at 300 fl. a year. Cannot sign, mark. | ::mark[lg-portrait-fdaeee] |
| 1890 | Inspector's report: “The light log is kept by Mrs Lestaud, schoolmistress, the keeper's wife, who writes at his dictation. Perfectly kept. The keeper makes his mark at the foot of each page. Nothing to criticise.” | *Inspector Vauclin* |
| 1902 | Increase: 340 fl. a year. | ::mark[lg-portrait-fdaeee] |
| 20 April 1908 | The Longuegrève light is converted to an unattended gas light, the island being under a residence ban. **Keeper dismissed**, with three months' wages. | *Inspector Mallard* |

*Inspector's marginal note, 1908:* “The keeper's wife asks to keep the light log. Refused: property of
the administration. Log deposited in the archives of the subdivision.”
