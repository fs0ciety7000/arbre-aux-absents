::stamp[REPUBLIC OF VELMARCHE — STATE LANDS ADMINISTRATION — COASTAL DIRECTORATE]

Aubrelieu, Tuesday 14 June 1977

**To the Director of the Civil Re-registration Office**

**Subject:** island of Longuegrève (Golfe d'Ambre) — proposed nature reserve — request for the
reconstruction of the beneficiaries of the registered owners (Act of 14 May 1973, Art. 15).

Dear Sir,

The Nature Reserves Service has applied for the island of Longuegrève to be classified as a strict
nature reserve (1976 note on the bird life). The island, of one hundred and eighteen hectares, has
been uninhabited since 1908 and under a residence ban since that date. It has never been
expropriated: the cadastre still holds it in four strips, in the names of the four owners registered
in 1868.

The State Lands Administration offers, for the whole, compensation of **two hundred and ninety-five
thousand new florins**, to be divided among the beneficiaries. It cannot pay this sum to names. The
island's parish register having been destroyed in 1908, by order of the district commissioner, during
the sanitary burning, I would ask you to be good enough to reconstruct the filiations in the forms laid
down by Article 15.

I enclose the extract from the 1868 cadastral roll, with the owners' marks, reproduced in facsimile by
the photographic service of the cadastre.

::signature[B. Fontanet, inspector of State Lands]
