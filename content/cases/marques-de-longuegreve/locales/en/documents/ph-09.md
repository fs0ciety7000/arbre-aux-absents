## Front

Print 13 × 18 cm, baryta paper. A windswept seaside cemetery, rows of wooden crosses behind a low wall.
In the foreground, a leaning cross; on its wood, under the crosspiece, a mark carved with a knife, sharp
on the print: ::mark[lg-portrait-aa93f2]. An old woman in a black headscarf bends towards it, one hand on
the crosspiece. Behind her, a man of about fifty holds his hat against his chest because of the wind; a
pale scar on his chin. The other crosses, in the background, are illegible.

## Back

In pencil, in a man's hand, heavily pressed:

::hand[“With Mrs Victoire and the lady from the University, at Longuegrève cemetery. Her grandfather Clovis's cross. 29 August 64. — F. Goémard”]
