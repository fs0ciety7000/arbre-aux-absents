Port-Sénane, Thursday 22 September 1977

**To the Director of the Civil Re-registration Office, Aubrelieu**

**Subject:** island of Longuegrève — petition of the State Lands of 14 June 1977 — objection.

Dear Sir,

I have the honour to form an objection, on behalf of the **Amber Company**, a general partnership
with its registered office at Port-Sénane, quai du Môle, represented by its president, **Miss Edmée
Garnaud**, to the above-mentioned petition, on the following grounds.

**1. The concession.** The Amber Company works the shores of Longuegrève by virtue of a State
concession granted on 1 June 1908 and renewed on 2 June 1958 until 31 May 1983. For sixty-nine years
no one but the Company has gathered the island's amber; for sixty-nine years its possession has been
peaceful, public and continuous. An expropriation that ignored the concession holder would be a
despoilment.

**2. The mark.** The petition invites the Office to reconstruct filiations from “marks”. With all
due respect to the legislator of 1973, my client observes that **a mark is not a signature**. A stroke
of the knife states neither a name, nor a date, nor a will. Anyone can carve a fork on an oar.

**3. The certificate.** The 1908 evacuation was ordered by the commissioner of the Estuary district
on the strength of a **health certificate** from the medical officer for epidemics. This
**certificate is a public instrument**. It is authentic until proved forged. The Company did no more
than house, at its own expense, at the Cabanes d'Ambre, families whom the authorities had driven from
an unhealthy island, and give them work.

**4. My client's family.** Miss Garnaud is the granddaughter of the founder, **Mr Blaise Garnaud**, a
child of Port-Sénane, an orphan, a ship's boy at sixteen, who created the Company in 1886 by dint of
hard work, and the daughter of the late **Mr Léandre Garnaud**, director of the Company until 1949.
She has never laid claim to any share of the island. She asks only that she not be stripped of what
her family has built.

My client remains at the Office's disposal to be heard at her registered office.

Yours faithfully,

::signature[V. Joubaud]
