::stamp[COMMUNE OF PORT-SÉNANE — CIVIL REGISTRY — LONGUEGRÈVE SECTION — MARRIAGES 1885]

**No. 3. — Cormaud Noël and Lestaud Honorine.**

In the year eighteen hundred and eighty-five, on Saturday the twenty-first of November, at ten o'clock
in the morning, before us, deputy mayor delegated to civil registration in the commune of Port-Sénane,
there appeared:

**Noël Cormaud**, shipwright, aged about twenty-five, residing on the island of Longuegrève, *born at sea
or on the shore of Longuegrève about 1860, of parents unknown, taken in and brought up by Siméon
Cormaud*, shipwright of the said place, and Ursule Lestaud, net mender, his wife, who, being present,
declare that they consent to the marriage as if they were his father and mother; the intending groom
producing, in lieu of a birth entry, an act of notoriety drawn up on 2 November last;

and **Honorine Lestaud**, net mender, aged twenty-one, born and residing on the said island, daughter of
full age of Lubin Lestaud, seaweed gatherer, and of Apolline Goémard, baker, his wife, present and
consenting.

We pronounced in the name of the law that they are united in marriage, in the presence of Messrs Hilaire
Robin and Prosper Lainé, of Port-Sénane, witnesses, who signed with us.

The spouses and those consenting for them declared that they could not sign and made their mark: the
