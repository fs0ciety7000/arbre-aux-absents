## Front

Print 10 × 15 cm. A woman of about thirty, in oilskins, short-haired, crouching on the sand; she holds a
tern gently between her hands and slips a ring on to its leg. Her large ears stick out from her short
hair. Behind her, the shore stretching away out of sight, and the light on the point, tiny.
