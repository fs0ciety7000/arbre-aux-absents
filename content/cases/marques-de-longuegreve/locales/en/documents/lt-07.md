Les Cabanes, Sunday evening 14 March 1965

Dear Miss Marest,

We watched the programme at Marin's, on the Friendly Society's set. Everybody cried when the bell came,
even Félix, who says he didn't. I never knew I had a voice like that. Thank you for putting in
everybody's names, and the marks too, even if you couldn't see them well.

I am writing to you about something I didn't say at the studio. The Garnaud girl was there, for the
Company; she was polite to me, I didn't want to say anything in front of her.

What you drew on the quay, in the house of the crowned Fork, ask yourself who Gervais's younger son
was. I never knew: at home we didn't talk about it, he was one they had taken away. But a mark, Miss,
doesn't lie, and that one I read the way I read my own.

I didn't say it in front of the Garnaud girl. I don't know if I did right.

With love, and Félix sends his regards.

::signature[Victoire Cormaud née Oyard]
