::stamp[COMMUNE OF PORT-SÉNANE — CIVIL REGISTRY — LONGUEGRÈVE SECTION — MARRIAGES 1879]

**No. 1. — Goémard Josse and Lestaud Reine.**

In the year eighteen hundred and seventy-nine, on Saturday the twenty-fifth of January, at eleven
o'clock in the morning, before us, deputy mayor delegated to civil registration in the commune of
Port-Sénane, there appeared:

**Josse Goémard**, amber gatherer, aged thirty, born and residing on the island of Longuegrève, son of
full age of Gervais Goémard, amber gatherer, and of Perrine Oyard, spinner, his wife, present and
consenting;

and **Reine Lestaud**, spinner, aged twenty-five, born and residing on the said island, daughter of full
age of Lubin Lestaud, seaweed gatherer, and of Apolline Goémard, baker at the island's common oven, his
wife, present and consenting.

The banns having been published without objection, and the intending spouses having declared their wish
to take each other as husband and wife, we pronounced in the name of the law that they are united in
marriage.

In the presence of **Médard Oyard**, boatman, twenty-eight, brother-in-law of the groom, ::mark[lg-portrait-4db872] and of
**Benoît Lestaud**, of no occupation, eighteen, brother of the bride, ::mark[lg-portrait-fdaeee] witnesses.

The spouses, the fathers and mothers and the witnesses declared that they could not sign and made their
mark: the groom ::mark[lg-portrait-df3f6c], the bride ::mark[lg-portrait-87e85e].
