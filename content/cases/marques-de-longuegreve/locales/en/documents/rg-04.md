“Chapel of Longuegrève — register — IV — 1894–1908”.*

::stamp[BRÉMOY COPY — BOOK IV]

## Baptisms

**1903.** *Oyard Anne.* On 12 April, Anne, daughter of Elzéar Oyard, boatman. — Father Lucas, visiting.

## Burials

**1899.** *Cormaud Toinette.* Toinette Cormaud, widow of Clovis Oyard, midwife, seventy-three. The whole
island. — Father Lucas.

**1902.** *Goémard Apolline.* Apolline Goémard, widow of Lubin Lestaud, seventy-four. The oven has passed
to Ernestine. — Father Lucas.

**1907.** *Oyard Anne.* Friday 20 December, Anne Oyard, four, daughter of Elzéar, died of the croup.
Buried by me, for want of a priest. — S. L.

**1908.** *Oyard Médard.* Monday 2 March, Médard Oyard, boatman, fifty-seven, died of the chest, which he
had suffered from for ten years. Buried by me, for want of a priest. — S. L.

**1908.** Sunday 29 March, last mass in the chapel, said by Father Lucas, who came specially. He took
away the sacred vessels. He did not take away the register: “the commission wants it”. — S. L.

## Afterword

Copied by me, Sidonie Lestaud, born Sidonie Brémoy, schoolmistress, in March 1908, at night, from the
chapel register that the commission is going to burn. Benoît held the lamp. I copied everything, even
what I did not understand, even the crossings-out.

**None of these dead died of the fever.** Anne Oyard died of the croup, which everyone knows. Médard
Oyard died of the chest, which he had been coughing with ever since I came to the island. There was no
other death this winter.

I leave these exercise books to whoever wishes to read them.

::signature[Sidonie Lestaud, née Brémoy — Longuegrève, Wednesday 1 April 1908]
