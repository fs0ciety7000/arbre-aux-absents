::stamp[AUBRELIEU POLICE TECHNICAL LABORATORY — HANDWRITING SECTION — REPORT No. 77-1182]

**Subject:** comparison of the typewritten characters of the health certificate of 13 March 1908 and of
the letter from the Amber Company to the district commissioner, dated 14 January 1908.

**Documents compared:** health certificate signed by Dr Pélissart (district archives, bundle 1, item 2);
letter from B. Garnaud to Commissioner Pontcarré (bundle 1, item 1); as controls, eleven typewritten
documents from the district and the Seamen's Hospital, March–April 1908.

**Findings.**

1. The lower-case *e* shows, in both documents, an eye clogged with ink, constant, due to a dirty or
   distorted typeface.
2. The figure *8* shows, in both documents, a vertical split in the lower loop.
3. The line spacing and the left margin are identical to a tenth of a millimetre; the ribbon has left the
   same smudge to the right of the capitals.
4. None of the eleven control documents shows these peculiarities: the machines of the district and of
   the Seamen's Hospital are of another model.

**Conclusion.** The health certificate of 13 March 1908 and the Amber Company letter of 14 January 1908
were typed **on the same typewriter**, which is not an official machine.

::signature[For the head of section, R. Dauvergne, expert]
