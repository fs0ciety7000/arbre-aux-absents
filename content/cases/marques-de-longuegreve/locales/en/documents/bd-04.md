| LG-64-04 | B | Monday 24 August 1964 | Les Cabanes d'Ambre, the Friendly Society's shed | Victoire Cormaud, née Oyard, aged 71 | 12'05 |

*Transcript by S. Marest. Mrs Cormaud asked for the machine to be stopped before this account, then
said: “No, put it back on. It has to be somewhere.”*

[00'06] **VICTOIRE.** Old Josse died the winter after. In January 1909, here, at the Cabanes, in the hut
that smelled of glue, because it was the old shed where they glued the crates. Josse Goémard. Head of
the Fork, the best amber gatherer on the island, along with his father.

[01'10] **MAREST.** Were you there?

[01'12] **VICTOIRE.** I was there. Reine was there, his wife, Reine Lestaud, and their son Gatien. Gatien
Goémard, he was twenty-seven. My mother had gone to fetch the priest from Port-Sénane. The old man had
me sit beside the bed, because I could write: he knew I could write. And then he didn't want me to
write. He said: “Put your pencil down.” I put it down.

[02'40] **MAREST.** And what did he say?

[02'45] **VICTOIRE.** *[long silence]* He said: “It was me took the amber from the Lestaud strip, in '70.
The boy said it was him.” That's all. And then he turned his face to the wall. Reine said: “Hush.” She
said that to her dying husband, “hush”, and she went out. Gatien stayed there looking at him. He died
the next morning.

[05'02] **MAREST.** Who was the boy?

[05'04] **VICTOIRE.** I don't know. I don't know who the boy was. In '70, I wasn't born. At home, we didn't
talk about '70. People said it wasn't a year. *[silence]* My mother would have known. I never asked
her. I never repeated that to anyone, except to you, now, to your machine.

[07'30] **MAREST.** The Lestaud strip, that was…

[07'32] **VICTOIRE.** Lubin's strip. Taking amber off another man's strip, with us, that was worse than
stealing a hen. The council of the four put you off the shore. You had no right to gather any more.
As good as saying you had no house any more.

*[08'40 – 12'05: Mrs Cormaud talks about Reine, who lived at the Cabanes until 1920 and was still
spinning at well past eighty… sixty-seven, she corrects herself. End of side B.]*
