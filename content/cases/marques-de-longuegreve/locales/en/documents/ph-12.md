## Front

Print 9 × 12 cm, deckle edges. A small garden under a trellis of roses. An elderly woman, in a shawl,
sits on an iron chair, a pince-nez on a black cord on her nose, a closed book on her knees. Behind her,
standing, a woman of about thirty, high bun, also wears a pince-nez clipped on her nose.

## Back

In ink, in a round hand:

::hand[“Aunt Sidonie, retired, in Mama's garden. With me. Summer 1930. — Berthe-Marie”]

In pencil, in another hand: “My mother and my aunt. They wore the same pince-nez; people teased them.
— G. S.”
