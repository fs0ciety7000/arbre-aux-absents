Les Cabanes d'Ambre, Tuesday 9 March 1909

Dear Mr Mayor,

For twenty-two years I was the public schoolmistress at the Longuegrève school, a section of your
commune, until the evacuation of 2 April 1908.

The island's chapel register was burned on 4 April along with the chapel. It was the only complete
record of the births, marriages and deaths of the section: half the island's children were never
declared at the town hall, because nobody sails over in winter.

Before the fire, I made a copy of it, in four exercise books, which I hold at your disposal. I offer
it to you so that the children of the island may have a civil record, and so that one day what is
theirs may be given back to them.

Yours respectfully,

::signature[Sidonie Lestaud, née Brémoy, former schoolmistress]

*In the margin, in red ink, in the mayor's hand:* “The Longuegrève section no longer exists. Filed.
Return to the person concerned. — Corre”
