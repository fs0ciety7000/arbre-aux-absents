::stamp[STATE LANDS ADMINISTRATION — MARITIME CONCESSIONS — GOLFE D'AMBRE — No. 208]

## Instrument of concession (Monday 1 June 1908)

Between the State Lands Administration, represented by the collector at Port-Sénane, and the **Amber
Company, B. Garnaud & Co.**, represented by Mr Blaise Garnaud, its manager, it is agreed:

**Article 1.** — The shores of the island of Longuegrève, from the high-water mark to the low-water
mark, along the whole west coast known as “the Longue Grève”, are granted to the Amber Company for
fifty years, from this day.

**Article 2.** — The grantee alone has the right to gather amber there, through its agents, residence
on the island being prohibited. Fee: 400 fl. a year.

**Article 3.** — *The concession covers the amber of the shores, not the land.* The owners entered in
the cadastre retain their rights over the lots; the prohibition of residence does not entail
dispossession.

::signature[The State Lands collector, Gauthier — B. Garnaud]

## Rider of renewal (Monday 2 June 1958)

Concession No. 208 is renewed for twenty-five years, until **31 May 1983**, on the same terms, the fee
being raised to 1,800 new florins a year. Article 3 is maintained.

::signature[For the State Lands, M. Chevrier — E. Garnaud, president]
