## Front

Modern print from a glass plate, bordered in black (the frame of the plate). A plank jetty; behind it, a
motor launch whose name can be read on the stem, *Trident*. A bearded man, in cap and jersey, is at the
helm. In front of the launch, on the jetty, seven people lined up as if for a roll call: four men and
three women. A woman of about forty, a pince-nez on her nose, clutches an exercise book to her chest. A
teenage girl with very light eyes stands beside an old woman in a coif.

## Caption

Caption from *The Echo* of 7 April 1908, copied on to the negative sleeve:

::hand[“On the Cabanes jetty: the schoolmistress and the children of the island.”]

*Note by the newspaper's archivist, on the back of the sleeve:* “Plate 1908-27. Reused in 1977 for the
article ‘The Cabanes want their island’. Skipper of the launch: A. Ténier. Other persons not identified
by the newsroom.”
