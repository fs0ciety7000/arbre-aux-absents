::stamp[MARITIME ENROLMENT — PORT-SÉNANE QUARTER — ROLL OF SHIP'S BOYS AND NOVICES]

*Cloth-bound register, folio 214. Two entries on the same page, in the same clerk's hand, on Monday 8
May 1871.*

## No. 3317

*In the margin:* **Goémard Blaise, known as Garnaud.**

“Blaise, known as Garnaud, born at Longuegrève about 1854, son of Gervais Goémard, amber gatherer, and
of Perrine Oyard, his wife, living at the said place; height 1.58 m, chestnut hair, grey eyes; no
residence in Port-Sénane. Shipped as ship's boy on the packet *Orvelle*, Orvelle Packet Company, Captain
Le Gaudu. Cannot sign, has made his mark.”

Mark: ::mark[lg-portrait-480ba1]

*Clerk's note, in pencil:* “Asked his surname, answered: Blaise, garnaud. Written as such.”

*Later entries:*
- 1873: “novice”.
- 1877: “paid off from the *Orvelle* at Port-Sénane; seaman; has signed: B. Garnaud”. *(The signature is
  in a careful hand, separate letters.)*
- 1878: “struck off the roll at his request, gone into trade”.

## No. 3318

“Ange Oyard, born at Longuegrève about 1853, son of Clovis Oyard, pilot, and of Toinette Cormaud, his
wife; height 1.71 m. Shipped as novice on the coaster *Sainte-Aude*. Cannot sign, has made his mark.”

Mark: ::mark[lg-portrait-29ab43]

*Later entry:* “Lost at sea off Tennok, November 1880.”
