## Front

Print 13 × 18 cm, sepia. A small-paned shop front, books in piles in the window; above the door, in
painted letters, “Ferlay Bookshop”. On the doorstep, a woman of about forty, low bun, half-moon
spectacles held by a cord, holds an open book against her chest.
