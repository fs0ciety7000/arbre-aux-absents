**RECORD OF HEARING**

On Thursday 6 October 1977, at half past two in the afternoon, at the home of the person appearing, 11
rue des Tilleuls, Bains-de-Mérande, before us, permanent rapporteur, assisted by the clerk, there
appeared:

**Mrs Ginette Sollier**, aged forty-six, post office employee, great-niece of Sidonie Lestaud, née
Brémoy.

“My great-aunt Sidonie was schoolmistress on Longuegrève, from the opening of the school until the
evacuation. She married Benoît Lestaud out there, **the lighthouse keeper** of the island; that's what
we always said at home. She came back here in 1928, when he died, to her sister's, my grandmother's.
She died in 1950; I was nineteen. Her papers stayed in the attic, in a trunk. When I read the notice in
the paper, I thought of the exercise books.”

The person appearing hands over to the Office (Sollier collection):
- **four school exercise books**, a copy of the chapel register of Longuegrève, from 1840 to 1908, with
  a signed afterword;
- the roll register of the Longuegrève school;
- an exercise book belonging to the pupil Victoire Oyard;
- letters from Sidonie to her sister Berthe;
- a letter from Sidonie to the mayor of Port-Sénane, returned;
- photographs, including the school in 1897 and Sidonie in the garden in 1930.

“She used to say the copy was the only thing she ever stole in her life: the register, at night, while
the priest slept. She also used to say nobody had ever thanked her.”

Having heard this read over, she confirms it and signs.

::signature[G. Sollier — the rapporteur — the clerk]
