::stamp[NATIONAL ARCHIVES — DEPOSIT AND COMPUTING CENTRE — LX-2 READER]

Aubrelieu, Friday 30 September 1977

**Subject:** deposit in the Central Indexed Collection (FCI) of the collections relating to ORC file
77-0874.

The following were deposited and indexed on the Reader this week:

1. the civil registers of Port-Sénane, sections of Longuegrève (until 1908) and of the Cabanes d'Ambre
   (from 1908), originals and court duplicates, with the ten-year indexes;
2. the rolls of the Port-Sénane Maritime Enrolment (ship's boys, novices, seamen);
3. the archives of the Estuary district (commissioner, medical officer for epidemics);
4. the registers of the Port-Sénane Seamen's Hospital;
5. the staff registers and the light logs of the gulf (Lights and Beacons);
6. the Grand-Ducal cadastre of the islands of the gulf (rolls and plans);
7. the Marest collection (Laboratory of Speech and Customs, University of Aubrelieu): field notebook
   No. 3, photographs, transcripts of tapes LG-64-01 to LG-64-07.

**Names.** The mainland clerks wrote the island names as they heard them: *Gouémard* or *Guémard*,
*Oyart*, *Cormault*, *Lestot*. The Reader groups these spellings under the name in use.

**Marks.** The marks are indexed after Mrs Marest's notebook, under their **LX reference**. The LX-2
keyboard has neither dot, nor slash, nor plus sign. To search for a mark, type:

- the head by its letter (Fork Y, Eye O, Arrow A, Gallows P);
- the sea child's bar: **b**;
- the notch: **g** (left) or **d** (right), from top to bottom;
- the grain: **pt** (two grains: **pt-pt**);
- the heel: **t** followed by its head, without a space;

each sign separated from the one before by a hyphen, in the reading order of plate I. Thus the cross
of Siméon Cormaud's second son, drowned in 1869, is searched as [[A-d|code-a-d]].

A head on its own fits in one letter: that is too short for the Reader. Search instead for the name of
the head of the house. The sea mark (bar alone) is not indexed.

The LX references are being added in pencil to the photocopies of the notebook as they are checked;
not all of them are there yet. A reference missing from the photocopies is not a mark missing from the
Reader.
