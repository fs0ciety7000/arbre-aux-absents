::stamp[COMMUNE OF PORT-SÉNANE — CIVIL REGISTRY — LONGUEGRÈVE SECTION — MARRIAGES 1876]

**No. 4. — Oyard Médard and Goémard Jacquette.**

In the year eighteen hundred and seventy-six, on Saturday the thirteenth of May, at ten o'clock in the
morning, before us, deputy mayor delegated to civil registration in the commune of Port-Sénane, there
appeared at the Town House:

**Médard Oyard**, boatman, aged twenty-five, born and residing on the island of Longuegrève, a section
of this commune, son of full age of Clovis Oyard, gulf pilot, and of Toinette Cormaud, midwife, his
wife, present and consenting;

and **Jacquette Goémard**, of no occupation, aged twenty-three, born and residing on the said island,
daughter of full age of Gervais Goémard, amber gatherer, and of Perrine Oyard, spinner, his wife,
present and consenting;

who requested us to proceed to the celebration of their marriage, the banns of which were published at
this Town House on Sundays 30 April and 7 May last, without objection. After reading the documents and
chapter VI of the title on marriage, each of the intending spouses having declared their wish to take
each other as husband and wife, we pronounced in the name of the law that they are united in marriage.

In the presence of **Josse Goémard**, amber gatherer, twenty-seven, brother of the bride, ::mark[lg-portrait-df3f6c] and of
**Fulbert Lestaud**, seaweed gatherer, twenty-four, ::mark[lg-portrait-d9a1bf] witnesses residing on the said island.

The spouses, the fathers and mothers and the witnesses declared that they could not sign and made their
mark: the groom ::mark[lg-portrait-4db872], the bride ::mark[lg-portrait-804ae3].
