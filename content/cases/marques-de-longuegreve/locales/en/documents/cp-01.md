## Front

Phototype postcard, about 1905. Printed caption: “Our Coasts — Longuegrève: the amber shore. — Orvelle
Phototype Works.” An immense shore after the storm, covered in wrack. In the foreground, a young man
with thick eyebrows, a basket on his shoulder; behind him, beside a basket set on the sand, an older
man, moustached, with curly hair. In the distance, a row of gatherers bent double.

## Back

5-sou stamp, cancelled at Port-Sénane on 13 November 1906. To Mrs Berthe Brémoy, rue des Tilleuls,
Bains-de-Mérande. In a fine hand, in violet ink:

::hand[“My dear Berthe, here is our shore, they sell it on postcards in town now. In front, with the basket, that's Gatien, Josse's son, my former pupil; behind, old Noël, who didn't want to pose. They gather the amber after the westerly gales, all night long, with lanterns. Love, Sidonie.”]

*Letter enclosed from Ginette Sollier, 14 October 1977:* “Found in my aunt's missal. I am sending it to
you; it may be of use.”
