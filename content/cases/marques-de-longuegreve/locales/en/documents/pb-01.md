::stamp[AMBER COMPANY — POSTER No. 2 — DUMESNIL-CORRE LITH.]

Lithographed poster in four colours, 80 × 120 cm.

At the top, in large gilt letters: “**Longuegrève Amber**”. Below, smaller: “since 1886”.

In the centre, in a medallion, the crowned Fork: ::mark[lg-portrait-9b5710]. Behind it, a shore strewn
with chips of amber gleaming in the setting sun; in the distance, the outline of a long, low island,
without houses.

At the foot: “Amber Company — Port-Sénane — quai du Môle” and, in very small print, “Dumesnil-Corre
Lith.”
