::stamp[COMMUNE OF PORT-SÉNANE — CIVIL REGISTRY — MARRIAGES 1878 — TOWN]

**No. 61. — Garnaud Blaise and Ferlay Amélie.**

In the year eighteen hundred and seventy-eight, on Saturday the fourteenth of September, at eleven
o'clock in the morning, before us, mayor of the commune of Port-Sénane, there appeared:

**Blaise Garnaud**, clerk to Mr Évrard, amber merchant, aged twenty-four, *born at Port-Sénane on 3
February 1854*, residing in rue des Cordiers, son of a father and mother deceased whose given names he
declares he does not know, as appears from an **act of notoriety** drawn up by the justice of the peace
on 2 September last on the declaration of seven witnesses, men of the port, standing in lieu of a birth
entry;

and **Amélie Ferlay**, bookseller, aged twenty, born and residing at Port-Sénane, quai des Messageries,
daughter under age of Jérôme Ferlay, bookseller, and of Marie Tessier, his wife, present and consenting.

We pronounced in the name of the law that they are united in marriage, in the presence of Messrs Jérôme
Ferlay, father of the bride, Paul Évrard, merchant, Hilaire Robin and Prosper Lainé, witnesses.

The spouses, the bride's parents and the witnesses signed with us.

::signature[B. Garnaud — A. Ferlay — J. Ferlay — M. Tessier — P. Évrard — H. Robin — P. Lainé — The Mayor, Corre]

*Marginal note (1880):* “Birth of Léandre, son of the spouses, on 12 March 1880.” *The Ferlay bookshop,
quai des Messageries, appears on the trade-licence roll of the same year under the name of “Ferlay
father and daughter”.*
