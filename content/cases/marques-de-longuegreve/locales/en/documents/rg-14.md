## Death entry No. 71

In the year nineteen hundred and twenty-four, on Monday the eleventh of February, at four o'clock in the
afternoon, there died at his home, the Hôtel Garnaud, quai du Môle, **Blaise Garnaud**, amber merchant,
founder of the Amber Company, honorary town councillor, aged seventy, *born at Port-Sénane on 3 February
1854*, son of a father and mother deceased, husband of Amélie Ferlay.

On the declaration of **Léandre Garnaud**, director of the Amber Company, aged forty-three, son of the
deceased, and of Mr Auguste Ferrel, clerk, who signed with us after reading.

::signature[L. Garnaud — A. Ferrel — For the mayor, Bouvard]
