::stamp[AMBER COMPANY — PACKAGING SAMPLE No. 3]

Label printed in two colours, 21 × 14 cm, nailed to a crate plank.

At the top, in ornate lettering: “Amber Company — B. Garnaud & Co. — Port-Sénane”.

In the centre, in an oval medallion, the emblem of the firm, the crowned Fork: ::mark[lg-portrait-9b5710].

Below: “Longuegrève Amber — Shore quality — Net weight: … kg”.

At the foot, the motto, in italics: “*What amber keeps, it keeps whole.*”

*Handwritten note on the back of the plank, in pencil:* “1910 model. The trident redrawn by Corre. Mr
Garnaud had the right-hand tine redone three times.”
