## Front

Citrate print, 13 × 18 cm, faded at the edges. Some twenty children in front of a brick wall, in three
rows: the big ones standing, the little ones sitting on the ground. In the centre, seated on a chair, a
young woman with a high bun, a pince-nez on a black cord on her nose, holds in front of her a little girl
of three or four. Beside her, standing, a tall boy of about fifteen rests his hand on the shoulder of a
smaller one.

## Back

In violet ink, in a fine, regular hand:

::hand[“Longuegrève school, June 1897. In the middle, me, with little Victoire Oyard on my knee, who would not stay sitting on the ground. Behind, Clément Lestaud (14) and Lazare Cormaud (11). Standing on my right, Gatien Goémard, who helps me with the little ones. — S. Brémoy, schoolmistress.”]

In pencil, in another hand: “Aunt Sidonie, her first school. — G. S.”
