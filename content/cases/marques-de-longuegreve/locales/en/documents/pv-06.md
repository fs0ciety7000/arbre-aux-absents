**RECORD OF THE OPENING OF A SAFE-DEPOSIT BOX**

On Friday 4 November 1977, at ten o'clock in the morning, at the Port-Sénane Savings Bank, lower
strongroom, on requisition by the Office, in the presence of the manager of the Bank, we proceeded to
open **safe-deposit box No. 108**, rented on 6 June 1908 “on behalf of” a mark, the opening of which is
reserved “to the bearers of the marks” of the four houses of Longuegrève, in the presence of one bearer
of each mark.

There appeared, each for his or her house, and each drew in the Bank's register, beside their name, the
head of their house:

| House | Representative | Head drawn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goémard | Mr Félix Goémard, fishing skipper | ::mark[lg-portrait-9e0e20] |
| Oyard | Mrs Rose Pellerin, née Oyard, fish seller | ::mark[lg-portrait-aa93f2] |
| Cormaud | Mr Marin Cormaud, foreman | ::mark[lg-portrait-5ce728] |
| Lestaud | Miss Aline Lestaud, ornithologist | ::mark[lg-portrait-8084e7] |

There also appeared, as a witness, **Miss Edmée Garnaud**, president of the Amber Company,
granddaughter of the renter. She asked to draw the head of the Goémard house too, “since it is mine,
it seems”. Mr Félix Goémard handed her the pen.

The safe-deposit box contained:
1. **four caskets** of oak marked with the four heads (inventory: item 52);
2. **a notebook** bound in black cloth, sealed, deposited in 1924 (item 53).

Each representative opened the casket of his or her house. The pieces were weighed and described.
Rough estimate, made on the spot by Miss Garnaud herself at the request of the persons appearing: “one
hundred and eighty thousand new florins, at least. It is the finest amber I have ever seen.”

The notebook was opened in the presence of all. Miss Garnaud asked to read it first; the persons
appearing consented. She read it through without a word, then passed it to Mr Goémard.

Having heard this read over, they confirm it and sign.
