::stamp[POSTS AND TELEGRAPHS — CABANES D'AMBRE OFFICE — 3 APRIL 1908 — 6.40]

**TO COMMISSIONER ESTUARY DISTRICT PORT-SÉNANE**

GOEMARD JOSSE REFUSES STOP FORCE NOTHING STOP GOING MYSELF BY LAUNCH STOP ANSWER FOR HIM STOP
GARNAUD

*In blue pencil, in the commissioner's hand:* “Send copy to Sergeant Massé. Let Mr Garnaud deal with
it. — E. P.”
