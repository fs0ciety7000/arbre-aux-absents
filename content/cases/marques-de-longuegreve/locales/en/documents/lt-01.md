::stamp[CIVIL RE-REGISTRATION OFFICE — DOCUMENT OPENED AT A HEARING ON 12.10.1977]

*Envelope of stout paper, yellowed, closed with brown wax, never opened. Address in a careful hand,
separate letters: “For Josse Goémard, at Longuegrève. By the launch.” Opened with a paper knife by the
investigator, in the presence of Mr Félix Goémard and Mr Marin Cormaud. One sheet folded in four, paper
without a letterhead.*

Port-Sénane, 20 March.

Josse,

I have never said anything about 1870. I never will. Nobody will learn it from me, not your wife, not
your son, not mine.

I know what you have thought of me since January, and you are right. I do not ask you to forgive me
the shore.

I ask you one thing only: get into the boat on the 2nd. They will burn everything, they told me so. I
do not want them to find you inside.

There will be a roof for you at the Cabanes, and work for the boy. I will not come to see you if you
do not want me to.

Your brother.

::signature[B. ::mark[lg-portrait-480ba1]]

*Investigator's note:* “Mr Félix Goémard states that his grandmother Reine received this letter in
March 1908, that his grandfather did not open it, and that she kept it closed until her death; it passed
to his father Gatien, then to him. ‘We never opened it because it wasn't ours.’ He read the letter
twice, then asked: ‘What brother?’”
