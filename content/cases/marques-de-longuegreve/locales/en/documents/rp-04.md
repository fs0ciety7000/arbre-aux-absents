::stamp[LIGHTS AND BEACONS — LONGUEGRÈVE LIGHT — LIGHT LOG]

*Regulation exercise book. Printed columns: date, lit, extinguished, weather, remarks. A fine, even
hand, violet ink; at the foot of each page, the keeper's mark.*

| Date | Lit | Out | Weather | Remarks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 March | 18.05 | 6.30 | W. strong | Nothing. |
| 2 March | 18.06 | 6.28 | W. strong | Médard Oyard died in the night, of his chest. He had been coughing since All Saints'. |
| 13 March | 18.20 | 6.10 | W. gale | No boat. Heavy sea all day. |
| 14 March | 18.22 | 6.08 | W. moderate | **The district doctor came on the Company's launch, towards ten o'clock. He saw Clément's little ones, over the measles, and old Jacquette, the midwife, who took him round the houses. He looked at the throats and at the well. He said there was nothing.** Left again at three. |

*Foot of page:* for the keeper, S. L. — ::mark[lg-portrait-fdaeee]

| Date | Lit | Out | Weather | Remarks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 March | 18.28 | 6.02 | N.W. | The constable brought a paper. Evacuation on 2 April. Nobody understands. |
| 2 April | 18.50 | — | calm | The evacuation. They called the houses from the list. Old Josse would not go aboard. Benoît stayed at the light, as the order requires. |
| 3 April | 18.52 | 5.30 | calm | Mr Garnaud came himself this morning. He spoke alone with old Josse, near Gervais's cross. Josse went aboard at eleven. |
| 4 April | 18.55 | 5.25 | calm | The commission's fire. The houses, the chapel, the register. I write here now only for the light. |

*Foot of page:* for the keeper, S. L. — ::mark[lg-portrait-fdaeee]

| Date | Lit | Out | Weather | Remarks |

| 20 April | — | — | — | Inspector's visit. The light is converted to gas. The keeper is dismissed. Handed over the log. |
