## Front

Photographic postcard, about 1925. Printed caption: “Port-Sénane — Les Cabanes d'Ambre.” A hamlet of
plank huts beside a basin, a large workshop building with tall windows, two boats alongside.

## Back

Addressed to Miss Rose Oyard, care of Mrs Tanguy, rue Basse, Saint-Aubert. In ink:

::hand[“My Rose, the workshop started up again on Monday, we're sorting the winter lot, it's a fine one. Your father made his mark on his pay again and the bookkeeper sighed. Marin says to tell you he can tie a bowline. Come back for the shore festival. Your aunt, Victoire Cormaud, née Oyard.”]
