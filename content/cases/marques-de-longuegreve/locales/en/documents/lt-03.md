*Engraved visiting card, “Doctor Cyprien Pélissart — Medical Officer for Epidemics of the Estuary
District — 4 rue de la Préfecture”, written on front and back in black ink.*

Thursday evening.

Dear Sir,

Your certificate is ready; your clerk brought it to me typed, I have nothing to correct in it. I shall
sign it this evening so as not to delay the commissioner, who wants his order on Monday.

I shall go and see the island on Saturday with your launch, if the sea allows; I am told the wind will
not drop before then.

Yours very truly,

::signature[C. Pélissart]

*In pencil, across it, in a careful hand, separate letters:* “Filed. No reply. — B. G.”
