*The shore book: notebook bound in black cloth, 96 pages, sealed with brown wax; seal broken in the
presence of the representatives of the four houses. Careful handwriting, separate letters. Each page is
signed with the same mark, bottom right. Extracts transcribed by the investigator.*

## First page

“1870. I took the blame for J. The house needed an eldest son.

1908. I took them off the island as I was taken off it. I gave them a roof and a wage. I owe them the
shore. What follows is each house's shore share, as if they were still gathering. After each westerly
gale I take the finest piece from each strip, and I put it aside. It is not a gift. It is an account.”

## A page from 1915

“2 February. For the Eye, a piece with a whole fly inside, its wings open. Elzéar picked it up and sold
it to me without looking at it. I give it back to his house. It is the finest of the year.”

::mark[lg-portrait-480ba1]

## A page from 1921

“I have written the testament. Léandre will ask for nothing: he is a better man than I am. Amélie has
known since the Exhibition; she has never said a word.”

::mark[lg-portrait-480ba1]

## Last page (winter 1923)

“Sixteen winters. I shall not go to the shore again. I learned to write so as to sign Garnaud; I never
knew how to sign any other way than this. The rest to the amber.”

::mark[lg-portrait-480ba1]
