## Original (commune register)

**No. 6. — Lestaud Fulbert and Oyard Ernestine.**

In the year eighteen hundred and seventy-seven, on Saturday the twentieth of October, at ten o'clock in
the morning, before us, delegated deputy mayor, there appeared: **Fulbert Lestaud**, seaweed gatherer,
aged twenty-six, born and residing on the island of Longuegrève, son of full age of Lubin Lestaud,
seaweed gatherer, and of Apolline Goémard, baker, his wife; and **Ernestine Oyard**, of no occupation,
aged twenty, born and residing on the said island, daughter under age of Clovis Oyard, gulf pilot, and
of Toinette Cormaud, midwife, his wife; the fathers and mothers of both parties present and consenting.

We pronounced in the name of the law that they are united in marriage, in the presence of two witnesses
from Port-Sénane, Messrs Hilaire Robin, carter, and Auguste Vasse, docker, who signed with us.

The spouses and the fathers and mothers declared that they could not sign and made their mark: the
