## Front

Colour print, 13 × 18 cm. Longuegrève cemetery, one morning after the wind has dropped; the oak crosses
set upright again, the grass mown. In the middle, a new cross, of pale oak. Under the crosspiece the
four heads are carved, side by side: ::mark[lg-portrait-9e0e20] ::mark[lg-portrait-aa93f2] ::mark[lg-portrait-5ce728] ::mark[lg-portrait-8084e7]. Below, smaller, another
mark, in a careful stroke: ::mark[lg-portrait-480ba1].

Round the cross, seven people. A man in a skipper's cap, a scar on his chin; a man with very curly grey
hair holding an old oar; a woman in oilskins, binoculars round her neck; an old woman with very pale
eyes, leaning on a stick; a woman in a dark suit, an amber brooch on her lapel, who does not know what
to do with her hands; a woman in a raincoat; and, a little apart, a woman holding a tape recorder under
her arm.
