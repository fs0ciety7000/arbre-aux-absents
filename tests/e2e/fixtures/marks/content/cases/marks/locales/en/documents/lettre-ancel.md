---
title: Letter from Ancel to Ida
author: Ancel Morvan
---
My dear Ida,

The workshop is busy: three clocks mended this week. Little Tomas finally sleeps
through the night. Kiss your mother for me, and tell her the blue trunk is safe.

I think of you, *Ida Brask*, as on the very first day.

::signature[Ancel Morvan ::mark[portrait-a], clockmaker]
